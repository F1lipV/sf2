**Bear** is [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]]'s fourth [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]]. He is one of the men who have been charmed by Widow. His weapon is a heavy hammer; which has a similar appearance to [[Northern Hammer]], but works like a [[Dadao]]. He is a bulky, strong and muscular man with a scar on his face that runs through his left eye. He wears brown fur-lined clothing and has brown hair and a beard.

## Trivia\[]

- Bear is one of several bodyguards in the game to use an unobtainable weapon. The other are [[Shin]] from [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]], [[Puma]], the bodyguard after him, and [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]]'s bodyguards from [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI]].
- His name is based upon an animal of cold regions, like all of Widow's bodyguards.
