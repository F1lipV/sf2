**Widow** is the fifth demon [[Bosses (SF2)|boss]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]], first appearing in [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V: The Greatest Temptation]].

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What is the significance of Widow's appearance in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Widow is the fifth demon the player encounters. She's a charmer with teleportation abilities and wields weapons such as Widow's Fans, Ghostly Kunai, and Assassin's Daggers. Her magic includes Energy Ball, Ice Ball, and Ice Pillar. Her bodyguards, named after cold-region animals, reflect her cold-heartedness and indifference. Unique among demons, Widow hasn't been conquered by any of Titan's bodyguards. Players earn the Purple Seal for defeating her and her weapon after a second eclipse defeat.

**How does Widow's appearance differ from a traditional Maiko Geisha?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Widow's attire diverges from a typical Maiko Geisha. She dons a red Kimono, with bandage-like sleeves and a cut-out at her right thigh, exposing her muscular physique. Contrary to a traditional Maiko, Widow lacks white facial makeup, but features a red dot on her forehead, akin to an Indian Bindi.

**What is the role of Widow in Act V: The Greatest Temptation of Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Widow is a charmer and boss in Shadow Fight 2's Act V: The Greatest Temptation. She lives in a war-ravaged land. The group, led by Shadow and May, ventures to her territory after receiving information from Wasp. Widow's martial skills include Widow's Fans, Ghostly Kunai, and Assassin's Daggers, and she can use magic like Energy Ball, Ice Ball, and Ice Pillar. To obtain the Orange seal, Shadow must defeat Widow and her five bodyguards.

## Appearance\[]

Widow closely resembles a Geisha, with a few exceptions. She has a Shimada hairstyle held together with hairpins, and a red Kimono which has sleeves underneath that resemble bandages. The Kimono is cut out at her right thigh, reveals her muscular/toned thigh muscles, able to kick strong. She does not have the white makeup on her face like the traditional Maiko, only having a red dot on her forehead, which is similar to the Bindi of Indian culture.

## Storyline\[]

### Sensei's Story Part 5. Humiliated and Insulted\[]

After [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]] is defeated, she told [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] what actually happened; Widow is a woman who fell in love with the [[Prince]]. However, her love is unrequited and Prince rejected her. This broke Widow's heart and caused her to want to rule all men and play them off, making them fight each other as a way of her revenge. Widow starts by manipulating [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]], the Prince's warlord, to make him overthrow Prince. Widow promised Shogun a magical Sphere that would turn him into a great warlord, but would also cause him to become her loyal slave.

When Sensei arrives at Widow's place, Widow becomes angry seeing him and wants to make him suffer, as she does not believe in men anymore. Widow's followers proceed to attempt to do the job, but they are defeated by Sensei. Before Widow proceeds to fight Sensei, she states that she will strike back for what the Prince has done to her and that Sensei will deliver this message to him. Sensei, however, defeats her and manages to speak some sense into her, saying that he sympathizes with her. Realizing that Sensei is honest with his intention to help, Widow informs him that Prince had come and seized the Sphere from her. Shogun's attempt to stop it was useless and Prince took him to be executed in the place where he had first met Widow. She also warns Sensei that the Prince has become wild after he got hold of the Sphere.

### Act V: The Greatest Temptation\[]

After the defeat of Wasp, [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] and the group continue their journey to get the remaining seals. Sensei tells Shadow that this is not their war, but Widow seems to be a worthy opponent and claims that they should pay her a visit. The group then moves on and they eventually meet Widow. Upon eyeing her, [[Sly (SF2)|Sly]] feels a strong attraction towards her. Sensei, however, is strong enough to resist it. Unusually for Widow however, upon seeing Shadow, who is unaffected by her, she is impressed by him and claims she will keep an eye on him.

The story then goes on where Shadow defeats her bodyguards and eventually battles her and wins, and she hands him the Orange Seal.

### Gates of Shadows\[]

Widow and the rest of the demons team up to stop Shadow from closing the [[Gates of Shadows]]. She battles Shadow fifth in line.

### Interlude\[]

Shadow, on his quest to break the seals, encounters Widow again in Act V of [[Interlude]] where she attempts to stop Shadow from reopening the Gates of Shadows. Before the fight, Widow tells Shadow that [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]'s minions are different from the enemies Shadow has faced so far and that he would not be able to defeat them. After defeat, Widow tells Shadow that Titan has been leading him from the very beginning like a puppet master and tells him that even now he is doing what Titan wishes.

## Personality\[]

Widow is a powerful warrior whose beauty mesmerizes all men who get a glimpse of her. Sly, when he sees her for the first time, becomes infatuated with her and tries getting her gifts like gems and stones. Even Sensei feels the pull of her magic, but his years of martial arts mastery and discipline keep him from this temptation. Widow's first four bodyguards were in fact suitors enthralled by her charm. They fight Shadow because they thought he was trying to get Widow's attention. The only man unaffected by her beauty was Shadow himself. Widow is never easily pleased. However, Shadow does catch her eye. She is not as aggressive as most of the other demons, barring [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]].

## Bodyguards\[]

Widow's bodyguards are mainly men who have been charmed. The only exception is [[Puma]], a woman who serves Widow to help her achieve her ambitions. Most bodyguards attack Shadow in an attempt to eliminate the competition, because they are mistaken in the belief that Shadow desires Widow as they do.

1. [[Irbis]]: bodyguard armed with [[Steel Batons]]
2. [[Wolf]]: bodyguard armed with [[Swords]]
3. [[Capra]]: bodyguard armed with [[Silver Spear]]
4. [[Bear]]: bodyguard armed with _[[Unavailable Known Items (SF2)|Heavy Hammer]]_
5. [[Puma]]: bodyguard armed with a _Sentinel_ _Kusarigama_

> Bear and Puma's weapons are unobtainable, though the sillouette of Bear's hammer is exactly the same as the Northern Hammer. Strangely though, it's Super Slash is the same as the Dadao.

## Equipment\[]

Widow uses a pair of fans as weapons. They work similar to sword-type weapons, but have a shorter range and a different super-slash.

For [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapons]], Widow first uses Ghostly Kunai, then switches to Assassin's Daggers after facing defeat in one round.

For [[Magic|magic]], she uses [[Energy Ball]], Ice Ball (after losing once) and Ice Pillar (after losing twice).

When fought in Gates of Shadows and Interlude, she uses Assassin's Dagger and Ice Pillar.

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

- **Teleportation**

![[assets/Untitled.gif|Untitled]] ![[assets/Untitled.gif|Untitled]]

![[assets/Untitled.gif]]

Widow has the ability to teleport; she disappears in the cyan beams coming from her fans and shows up attacking from the back. It can be dodged using any movement combo. Players also can interrupt the attack by hitting Widow before she finishes her teleportation. Widow uses this perk after losing once.

- **Lifesteal**![[assets/EnchantmentLifeDrain.png|EnchantmentLifeDrain]]

Widow's Fans and ranged weapons are enchanted with Lifesteal, giving Widow a chance on every successful hit to replenish her health by 250% of the damage dealt to the player.

- **Regeneration**![[assets/EnchantmentRegeneration.png|EnchantmentRegeneration]]

Widow's armor and helm are enchanted with Regeneration, giving a chance to regenerate 4.5% of her health for 5 seconds after being hit. Appears after Widow loses twice.

## Strategy\[]

![[assets/WidowTeleportCounter.gif|Shadow lands the  magic on Widow, then successfully counters her Teleportation attack using the Moon Sabers' spinning slash.]] ![[assets/WidowTeleportCounter.gif|Shadow lands the  magic on Widow, then successfully counters her Teleportation attack using the Moon Sabers' spinning slash.]]

![[assets/WidowTeleportCounter.gif]]

Shadow lands the [[Quantum Leap]] magic on Widow, then successfully counters her Teleportation attack using the Moon Sabers' spinning slash.

Widow is considered one of the easiest demons in the game to defeat, due to lacking abilities that can decisively turn the tide of the battle in her favor. Her fans share a similar fighting style to the Swords and the Sabers, but has shorter overall range in exchange for longer hitstun for her spinning and lower slashes, preventing those attacks from being countered if blocked. One issue with Widow is her fans' Lifesteal enchantment, which lets her restore health by a huge 250% of the damage dealt. Therefore, if she lands a combo hit on Shadow, she may regain all of her health back even when she's low on health before the combo hit. Of course, the Regeneration enchantment, which appears after Widow loses twice, does little to brighten the scene as it simply makes Widow even more difficult to take down.

None of Widow's attacks are particularly quick, therefore rushing her down with a fast weapon like Knives, Knuckles and Swords can be extremely effective. However, Widow's fans still has several quick attacks in its arsenal, so caution is advised when going in hand-to-hand, especially with her fans' Lifesteal enchantment being able to easily undo the damage Shadow's hard hits inflicted. By rushing her down, Shadow can easily prohibit Widow from using her Teleportation attack and her magic, as all of them has windup and can be interrupted with relentless waves of attacks.

## Trivia\[]

- Widow's Ice Ball is one of her magic attacks that is not obtainable. As of Christmas 2016 update, players can however obtain the [[Shard of Eternal Ice]] magic which functions in a similar manner.
- Widow's fans (and other fans weapons) are one of the only weapons that can be thrown in fights. The others are Staff weapons.
- None of her bodyguards wield weapons from Act V.
- Widow is the only demon who has somewhat affectionate feelings for Shadow.
  - It is later revealed that Shadow looked very similar to the Prince, whom Widow once fell in love with.
- Her counterpart in [[Shadow Fight 1]] is [[Huntress]].
- Widow is the only demon who was not conquered by any of Titan's [[Bodyguards|bodyguards]], Shadow's unfortunate mirrors.
- Widow's former name was Huntress.
- Widow regenerates after being hit in her head, however, she does not wear any helm.
- Widow is the only person in the whole game to have ever seen [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]'s face.
