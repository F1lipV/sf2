**Puma** is [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]]'s fifth and last [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]]. Unlike other bodyguards of Widow, Puma is a woman who serves Widow to help her achieve her goals. She uses the Sentinel Kusarigama, a unique type of [[Kusarigama]] which cannot be obtained by the player. She wears a brown robe with yellow fur linings and a brown belt with a purple gem in front of her waist.

## Trivia\[]

- Puma is the last female bodyguard encountered in the game.
- She is the second bodyguard in the game to use a kusarigama. The other is [[Reaper]] from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]].
- Puma is one of several bodyguards in the game to use an unobtainable weapon. The other ones are [[Shin]], [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]'s first bodyguard, [[Bear]], the bodyguard before her, and [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]]'s bodyguards.
- Her Sentinel Kusarigama has a similar sickle head like that of the [[Heavy Kusarigama]] used by [[Outcast]], however, it has a claw-shaped weight, while the Heavy Kusarigama used by Outcast has a ball-shaped weight with spikes. These features are only realistic to her avatar though, as during battle, her kusarigama appears to be just like the default kusarigama; only with the fixed claw.
- She is the only female bodyguard of Widow.
  - She is also the only female bodyguard to use an unobtainable weapon.
