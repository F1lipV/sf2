**Irbis** is [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]]'s first [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]]. He is one of the men charmed by Widow. Irbis uses [[Steel Batons|batons]] as his weapon. He is very musclular, has grey hair and wears a blue headband with a sleeveless fur-rimmed blue shirt. After his defeat, he reveals that before being charmed by Widow, he used to be a raider.

## Trivia\[]

- Irbis is supposed to wear the Two-faced Helm. But, due to a bug, the helm is not displayed and he appears without helm instead.
- He is the third bodyguard to use batons, the first being [[Brick]] from [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] and the second [[Bull]] from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]].
- In [[Shadow Fight Shades|Shadow Fight: Shades]], when Kanade defeated Flauros (the Mercenary who hunted him down) , Irbis was revealed that he is Kanade, Kenji and Dragon's uncle as Kanade referred him as "Uncle".
