**Corsair** is the fourth [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], met in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 3]]. Like all other bodyguards of Titan, he is the evil counterpart of [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] from another dimension. In that dimension, he was not strong enough to resist the pull of Shadow Energy and ended up getting corrupted by it. Corsair takes over [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]'s fleet after he defeats her in the other world.

He reveals that by answering the call of the ocean, he has unlocked his full power and potential. Since Wasp was weak, he took over her fleet after defeating her in battle and has now become a well known and feared pirate. The only laws he follows are the ones he makes himself and now he is free to kill anyone who stands in his way.

After he is defeated, he claims that he will now have to spill innocent blood in order to make up for his failure as the ocean "does not excuse mistakes".

## Equipment\[]

Corsair uses [[Plasma Rifle]] as his [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] of choice.

He wears Sturdy Barbarian Armor as his [[Armor (SF2)|Armor]].

His [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapons]] of choice are Mortal Thorns. However, he uses Landmines in Sparring (Switch edition).

He uses Torturer's Rage as his [[Magic]].

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

Corsair utilizes perks and enchantments that help him with his attacks and defense (just like Shadow and Titan's other Bodyguards).

- **Helm Breaker** ![[assets/IconHelmBreaker.png|IconHelmBreaker]]

A 25% chance to increase Corsair's Head Hit damage by 59% for 5 seconds after dealing a Head Hit.

- **Pain Rage** ![[assets/IconPainRage.png|IconPainRage]]

A 30% chance to increase damage by 49% for 8 seconds after receiving a Head Hit.

- **Block Breaker** ![[assets/IconBlockBreaker.png|IconBlockBreaker]]

A 30% chance to bypass the players' blocks.

- **Ricochet** ![[assets/IconRicochet.png|IconRicochet]]

A 40% chance to block all damage from an incoming ranged attack.

- **Avenger** ![[assets/IconAvenger.png|IconAvenger]]

A 35% chance to trigger Avenger, which grants a Critical Hit with with 141% damage for 5 seconds after receiving a Critical Hit. Only in [[Eclipse]] replays.

- **Lifesteal**![[assets/EnchantmentLifeDrain.png|EnchantmentLifeDrain]]

Corsair's Plasma Rifle is enchanted with Lifesteal, giving him a chance on every successful hit to replenish his health by 250% of damage dealt to the players.

- **Magic Recharge**![[assets/EnchantmentMagicRecharge.png|EnchantmentMagicRecharge]]

Corsair's Armor and head are enchanted with Magic Recharge, giving him a chance to increase his magic recharge from taking a hit by 700%.

- **Enfeeble**![[assets/EnchantmentEnfeeble.png|EnchantmentEnfeeble]]

Corsair's ranged weapon is enchanted with Enfeeble, giving him a chance to weaken players' attacks by 75% for 10 seconds after a successful hit.

## Trivia\[]

- Corsair's Plasma Rifle has a different enchantment than the one in the shop.
- He and the other Bodyguards of Titan (except for [[Guru]]) use gem weapons.
- Like Shadow, he and the other Bodyguards of Titan utilize both perks and enchantments.
- He is the only character in the game who wears a western-style armor through his avatar.
