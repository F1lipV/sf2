**Emperor** is the fifth and final [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], met in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 3]]. Like all other bodyguards of Titan, he is the evil counterpart of [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] from another dimension. In that dimension, he was not strong enough to resist the pull of Shadow Energy and ended up getting corrupted by it. After defeating [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]] in that dimension, he had become the new ruler of Ivory City.

Emperor is an evil tyrant who considers himself to be superior than others, and has an absolute hatred for commoners, very similar to the [[Prince]] (the Shadow Energy possibly made him delusional enough to believe that he is indeed the Prince after being called the Prince for so long). Since he vanquished its previous ruler, he claims that the Ivory City was his to rule by rights. He says that Shadow was weak and represented the "quintessential" of all the things he hated about common people.

After his defeat, he curses Shadow and conceitedly claims that his followers would flay him alive should he dare set foot in his kingdom.

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**Who did Emperor defeat to become the ruler of Ivory City?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Emperor claimed the rule of Ivory City post his victory over the former ruler, Shadow. He viewed Shadow as weak and emblematic of all he detested in commoners.

Provided by: Fandom

**What led to Emperor's corruption by Shadow Energy?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

The corruption of Emperor by Shadow Energy was triggered by several incidents. Upon learning that Queen Moira was aware of the missing Sphere, he was infuriated. Further, Marcus revealed the Legion Ambassador's illicit shadow energy trade. Although the Emperor suspected a Dynast's involvement, he was more perturbed by the perceived treachery of the prince/princess. A subsequent duel with Marcus, which the Emperor lost, left him drained and vulnerable.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is Emperor's attitude towards commoners?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Emperor (SF2) views himself as superior and has a strong disdain for commoners. He asserts that the Ivory City is his by right, having overthrown its previous ruler, Shadow. He saw Shadow as the epitome of all he detested about common people. Despite his defeat, he arrogantly threatens Shadow with retribution from his followers if he dares to enter his kingdom.

Provided by: Fandom

**How did Emperor become the bodyguard of Titan?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Emperor, originating from a different dimension as an evil version of Shadow, succumbed to the power of Shadow Energy. His strength was insufficient to resist this force. He ascended to the role of Ivory City's ruler after overcoming Shogun. As Titan's fifth and ultimate bodyguard, Emperor is distinguished by his disdain for commoners and his inflated self-worth.

Provided by: Fandom

**What was the outcome of the battle between Emperor and Shogun?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Despite being outnumbered in the fight against Shogun, Emperor emerged victorious, seizing Shogun's seal. Shogun was left in disbelief and denial, fearing that Emperor, whom he misidentified as a prince, would usurp his empire.

Provided by: Fandom

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

Emperor utilizes perks and enchantments that help him with his attacks and defense (just like [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]], Titan's other bodyguards, [[May (SF2)|May]], [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]] himself, and some [[Underworld|underworld]] [[Bosses (SF2)|bosses]]).

- **Cobra** ![[assets/IconCobra.png|IconCobra]]

A 100% chance that during the first 3 seconds of a round Emperor's First Strike would reduce the players' health by 15% (20% in [[Eclipse]] replays).

- **Avenger** ![[assets/IconAvenger.png|IconAvenger]]

A 60% chance to trigger Avenger, which grants a Critical Hit with 141% damage for 5 seconds after receiving a Critical Hit.

- **Martial Spirit** ![[assets/IconMartialSpirit.png|IconMartialSpirit]]

A 50% chance to increase Emperor's magic recharge from a successful unarmed attack by 100%.

- **Block Breaker** ![[assets/IconBlockBreaker.png|IconBlockBreaker]]

A 25% chance (35% in Eclipse replays) to bypass the players' blocks.

- **Ricochet** ![[assets/IconRicochet.png|IconRicochet]]

A 50% chance to block all damage from an incoming ranged attack.

- **Magic Recharge**![[assets/EnchantmentMagicRecharge.png|EnchantmentMagicRecharge]]

Emperor's Cobra's Tooth is enchanted with Magic Recharge, giving him a chance to increase his magic recharge from a successful hit by 300%.

- **Damage Absorption**![[assets/EnchantmentBodyProtection.png|EnchantmentBodyProtection]] ![[assets/EnchantmentHeadProtection.png|EnchantmentHeadProtection]]

Emperor's Armor and head are enchanted with Damage Absorption, giving him a chance to absorb all damage dealt to his body and head by a single strike.

- **Lifesteal**![[assets/EnchantmentLifeDrain.png|EnchantmentLifeDrain]]

Emperor's ranged weapons are enchanted with Lifesteal, giving him a chance on every successful hit to replenish his health by 250% of the damage dealt to the players.

## Trivia\[]

- Cobra's Tooth wielded by Emperor has a different enchantment than the one in the shop. The Cobra's Tooth in the shop is enchanted with Poisoning; however, Cobra's Tooth wielded by Emperor has the Magic Recharge enchantment.
- Emperor has Damage Absorption on his helm, even though he doesn't wear any helm.
- He and the other bodyguards of Titan (except for [[Guru]]) wield gem weapons.
- He is the last bodyguard encountered in the entire game.
