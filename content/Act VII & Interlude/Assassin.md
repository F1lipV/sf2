**Assassin** is the first [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], met in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 3]]. Like all other bodyguards of Titan, he is the evil counterpart of [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] from another dimension. In that dimension, he was not strong enough to resist the pull of Shadow Energy and ended up getting corrupted by it. Consequently, he took over the Order after defeating its previous leader [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]].

Physically, he appears as a dangerous warrior holding a sword, dressed in a dark robe with a cowl covering his face. As his name suggests, he is an expert of stealth and silent killing. He says that Shadow should have been the most famous assassin as his primary skill is killing and wonders why the latter is wasting his time with the "common rabble". He also tells Shadow that, after taking over the Order and killing [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] and [[May (SF2)|May]] from his dimension, he is the one who decides who lives and who dies.

After Shadow defeats him and spares his life, Assassin threatens him that this act of mercy would one day cost him dearly.

## Equipment\[]

Assassin uses the [[Devastator]] as his [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] of choice.

As his name suggests, he wears Assassin's Robe as [[Armor (SF2)|Armor]] and Assassin's Cowl as [[Helm (SF2)|Helm]].

His [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapons]] of choice are Mortal Thorns.

He uses [[Toxic Cloud]] as his [[Magic]].

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

Unlike all previous bodyguards. Assassin has perks and utilizes enchantments that help him with his attacks and defenses (just like Shadow, Titan's other Bodyguards, May, Titan himself, and some underworld bosses).

- **Avenger** ![[assets/IconAvenger.png|IconAvenger]]

A 50% chance to trigger Avenger, which grants a Critical Hit with 134% damage for 5 seconds after receiving a Critical Hit.

- **Block Breaker** ![[assets/IconBlockBreaker.png|IconBlockBreaker]]

A 20% chance to bypass the players' block.

- **Steel Foot** ![[assets/IconStunningFoot.png|IconStunningFoot]]

A 20% chance to knock down the player with a kick. This chance is doubled if Assassin is in the air.

- **Mirror** ![[assets/IconMirror.png|IconMirror]]

Blocking a player's hit gives Assassin a 30% chance to get +85% damage for 2 seconds.

- **Precision**![[assets/EnchantmentCriticalChance.png|EnchantmentCriticalChance]]

Assassin's Devastator is enchanted with Precision, giving him a chance to make a Critical Hit with increased damage.

- **Regeneration**![[assets/EnchantmentRegeneration.png|EnchantmentRegeneration]]

Assassin's Armor is enchanted with Regeneration, giving a chance to regenerate 4.5% of his health for 5 seconds after being hit.

- **Magic Recharge**![[assets/EnchantmentMagicRecharge.png|EnchantmentMagicRecharge]]

Assassin's Helm is enchanted with Magic Recharge, giving him a chance to increase his magic recharge from taking a Head Hit by 700%.

- **Lifesteal**![[assets/EnchantmentLifeDrain.png|EnchantmentLifeDrain]]

Assassin's ranged weapons are enchanted with Lifesteal, giving a chance on every successful hit to replenish his health by 250% of the damage dealt to the player.

## Trivia\[]

- Assassin's Devastator has a different enchantment than the one in the shop.
- Assassin holds a Devastator when fighting Shadow, but in his avatar he appears with a katana.
- He is the only bodyguard of Titan who does not wield a weapon from [[Act VII]].
- According to QNA, Assassin lost his right arm and replaced with a cybernetic arm in his Shades avatar is because he was buried under the rubble of Titan’s Citadel and couldn’t reach his shard. Because of the same events, he lost his hand.
