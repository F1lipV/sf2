**Shroud** is a [[Mini-bosses (SF2)|mini-boss]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]], and one of [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]'s strongest fighters. He is equipped with the [[Reaver]], a two handed weapon that's even slower than conventional weapons of that category in exchange for the ability to deal devastating damage should he successfully land a hit.

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What is the name of the weapon Shroud uses in Act VII?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Shroud wields a unique weapon called the Reaver in Act VII. This weapon has the distinctive feature of unfolding during specific attacks, similar to two-handed weapons.

**Who defeated Shroud turning him into a servant?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Shadow overcame Shroud in combat, leading to Shroud's servitude to Titan. Despite Shroud's strength, Shadow defeated him with ease. Shroud, however, insisted his actions were voluntary, asserting that Titan's rule prevented worlds from self-destruction.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is unique about Shroud's weapon, the Reaver?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

The Reaver, Shroud's weapon, is a distinctive mix of a dane axe and a scythe with a foldable blade that unfolds during a particular attack. This weapon, used by Shroud and a Factory Looter in Shadow Fight 2, shares similarities with Two-handed Weapons but has unique differences. Players can unlock the Reaver at level 45.

Provided by: Fandom

**What role does Shroud play in the Shadow World?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In the Shadow World, Shroud, a fallen champion, serves Titan. He first tries to capture Shadow but is halted by Kali, enabling Shadow's escape. Shroud later wreaks havoc, causing destruction and taking Cypher and others hostage. He insists his actions, despite their violence, are voluntary, asserting that Titan's rule will prevent the worlds from self-destruction.

Provided by: Fandom

**Who was Shroud a champion for before his defeat?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Shroud, prior to his defeat, was a champion who stood up to Titan. Following his defeat, he turned into a servant for Titan, becoming one of his most formidable fighters armed with the Reaver. Shroud's allegiance to Titan was not due to mind control, but a conscious choice, driven by his belief in Titan's ability to prevent world destruction through his rule.

## Story\[]

Shroud was a champion that challenged Titan. However, he is defeated by Titan, just like the other champions before him. Since then, Shroud has become a servant of Titan. After [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] arrived in the Shadow World, he approaches Shadow and commands him to put his weapon down and go with him, but Shadow fights instead. Shroud, being a very strong foe, is almost about to defeat Shadow when [[Kali]] interferes and manages to temporarily immobilize him, giving Shadow and herself the time to escape. At the time when a tournament is being held in the Incubator, Shroud has gone insane, destroying everything that goes in his way. After Shroud has broken into [[Cypher]]'s Hideout and holds Cypher and the others at ransom, Shadow goes back to the hideout to fight Shroud, and managed to defeat the fallen champion with little difficulty. After that, Shroud claimed that he is not under Titan's mind control, and that all his actions were carried out of his own free will. He believes that Titan's conquest and rule save the worlds from destroying themselves. Suddenly, [[May (SF2)#Justice|Justice]] appears and interrupts him. She orders that Shroud will be placed in the Incubator's feeding tube to "serve as nutrition for a new generation of Titan's military", essentially announcing his death.

## Battle\[]

In the first fight at the Factory, Shroud's defense is extremely high, making it impossible to deplete all his health away. Just like the challenges, there is only one way to win without depleting a single piece of Shroud's health. Players win after they take damage, prompting Kali to interfere and stun Shroud with Energy Ball.

Shroud can be beaten in the second fight with him at the Hideout. He utilizes perks this time.

### Perks\[]

- **Block Breaker ![[assets/IconBlockBreaker.png|IconBlockBreaker]]**

Shroud's attacks have a 15% chance to bypass Shadow's block.

- **Enlightenment ![[assets/IconEnlightenment.png|IconEnlightenment]]**

Shroud will survive the first death blow from Shadow, and replenish 10% of his health. This perk appears after Shroud lost one round, and can only be activated once per round.
