**May** is a main character in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. She is met in [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]], after [[Shin]] is defeated.

![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

## Appearance\[]

May is the one of the two characters who have their pupils visible in the entire series. Her eye color is light green. She has orange hair with bangs in the front.

While under [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]'s control in Act VII, her eyes are completely white just like the demons in the game, her skin is pale white and her hair color is red-orange.

## Personality\[]

May has a kind, cute and caring personality, especially towards [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] whom she later ends up with at the end of the series. She is brave and also intelligent in several aspects, especially in weapons. She is also quite vocal regarding her opinions and speaks her mind freely.

After getting seized by [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], her personality completely changes and she becomes insane and ruthless. Shadow faces a hard time fighting with her aspects, especially strategically, because she is greatly aware of Shadow's fighting style, having seen all of his previous fights. After being defeated by Shadow, she transforms back to her normal state.

## Story\[]

May introduces herself as the local armor and weapons expert of Act I's town, and insists that [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] should take her along on his journey. She promises to make sure his equipment is in good condition and on the journey, she serves as the assistant of Shadow and a person who generally has a deal of information to provide on mysterious subjects. She gives information about [[Tournament (SF2)|Tournament]], [[Challenge]], and other things so that Shadow could improve himself and defeat [[Bosses (SF2)#Demons|demons]] more easily.

After they reach the [[Gates of Shadows]], [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] informs that only someone with flesh and bones can seal the Gates, pointing out Shadow is unable to do that. May reluctantly volunteers to close the Gates after Sensei and [[Sly (SF2)|Sly]] refuse to do it. As the Gates begin to shut, a powerful torrent of wind rushes towards them and drags May along with it. Shadow tries to catch her, but she is whisked away inside the Gates just as they slam shut.

In order to save May, Shadow must open the Gates once more. Sensei tells Shadow that it seems the seals were connected to their Demons, so Shadow must defeat all six demons again to break the seals and reopen the gates. After he defeats all of the demons once more, Shadow proceeds to enter the opened Gates to save May.

## Justice\[]

**Justice** is the name given by [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]] to May during the time when she is under the influence of his mind control. Being mind-controlled, she is evil, just like him, wants to take revenge from Shadow, and serve Titan and be his mindless slave. Her eyes are completely white just like every demon in the game. She wears a blue colored technological Armor. She appears at the middle of Chapter 2, and at Chapter 3, she unmasks herself and is fought as a mini-boss.

## Act VII\[]

After [[Shroud]] has become insane, Justice is sent by Titan to neutralize Shroud and capture Shadow. When Shadow wins the Incubator tournament, he receives news that Shroud has broken into the Hideout and holds [[Cypher]] and the others at ransom, demanding that Shadow fight him. Shadow agrees to the challenge. After Shroud is defeated, Justice appears. She orders that Shroud be placed in the Incubator's feeding tube to "serve as nutrition for a new generation of Titan's military". Afterwards, Justice recognizes Shadow as an extraordinary fighter and commends his style, saying that despite his disguise, she knew who he was all along. It is then revealed that Justice is May. Completely under Titan's control, her eyes are now white like a demon, and she is subservient and obedient to Titan. She asks Shadow if he wishes to take Titan's place. Then she sees [[Kali]] and thinks that Shadow had already moved on and found a new "girlfriend". She says that Titan was right about him all along - Shadow is not saving the world, all he wants are "chicks" and "power". Kali rebukes May and tells her that she is nothing but Shadow's liaison officer and that Shadow had come across the Gates for May. Cypher interrupts, revealing that he had now activated the Hideout's defense circuit, effectively trapping May. She realizes that she has been tricked and threatens that Titan would kill them all. Cypher states that May had lost her mind because of Titan and as long as Titan holds grip over her mind, she will remain a mindless and obedient servant.

After all of Titan's bodyguards are defeated, Titan summons May, which surprises Kali. May reveals that she killed her captors back at the hideout and managed to escape which enrages Kali. May tells her to restrict herself as she has brought Cypher to watch her fail and challenges Shadow to a fight with her. She taunts him by saying that she doubts Shadow can win as she knows all his fighting styles. Boosted by shadow energy, May has incredible strength and possesses various perks and abilities seen use by Shadow and the demons, but Shadow still managed to defeat her and break Titan's control over her. Kali tells May to go back through the Gates, saying that after freeing her, Shadow has only one thing left to do - fight Titan. Although reluctant at first, May finally leaves.

May anxiously awaits Shadow's return after she returns from the Gates. As the Gates begin to crumble, she spots a silhouetted figure emerging from the dust and rubble - Shadow has not only managed to avoid the explosion and return through the Gates, he has also got his physical body back. The two hug and then decide to return home.

## Fight with May\[]

May is fought in Act VII: Chapter 3 as a mini-boss. The players must defeat her before they can challenge Titan. Two rounds must be won in order to win. There is a total of three rounds that can be fought. The timer for each battle is 99 seconds, as per usual conditions.

Boosted by shadow energy, May now possesses incredible strength and can use various perks and abilities that is seen used by Shadow and the demons to inflict heavy damage and reduce the damage she receives.

### Equipment\[]

- Weapon: [[Blaster Tonfas]]
- Armor: Alloy Armor
- Helm: Faceless Mask
- Ranged Weapon: Mortal Thorns
- Magic: [[Wrath of Flame]]

### Perks and Enchantments\[]

- **Invisibility ![[assets/IconInvisibility_Blue.png|IconInvisibility Blue]]**

May can become invisible after blasting a puff of smoke around her and stays the same until the player damages her heavily. When invisible, May will not use ranged weapons and magic. But she still can shoot energy missiles with her Blaster Tonfas.

- **Teleportation**

May has the ability to teleport by which she disappears in the cyan beams coming from her weapons and shows up attacking from the back. Replaces Invisibility after May is defeated once.

- **Avenger** ![[assets/IconAvenger.png|IconAvenger]]

A 60% chance to trigger Avenger, which grants a Critical Hit with 141% damage for 5 seconds after receiving a Critical Hit.

- **Helm Breaker ![[assets/IconHelmBreaker.png|IconHelmBreaker]]**

A 10% chance to increase Head Hit damage by 59% for 5 seconds after dealing a Head Hit.

- **Block Breaker** ![[assets/IconBlockBreaker.png|IconBlockBreaker]]

A 40% chance to bypass the players' blocks.

- **Cobra** ![[assets/IconCobra.png|IconCobra]]

A 100% chance that during first 3 seconds of a round May's First Strike would reduce players' health by 25%. Appears after May is defeated once.

- **Magic Recharge** ![[assets/EnchantmentMagicRecharge.png|EnchantmentMagicRecharge]]

May's Blaster Tonfas are enchanted with Magic Recharge, giving her a chance to increase her magic recharge from a successful hit by 300%.

- **Overheat![[assets/EnchantmentOverheat.png|Overheat]]**

May's Blaster Tonfas are enchanted with Overheat, giving May a chance in each strike to put a buff on herself that makes her next strike inflict 200% more damage. Replaces Magic Recharge after May is defeated once.

- **Regeneration** ![[assets/EnchantmentRegeneration.png|EnchantmentRegeneration]]

May's armor and helm are enchanted with Regeneration, giving a chance to regenerate 4.5% of her health for 5 seconds after being hit.

- **Damage Absorption** ![[assets/EnchantmentBodyProtection.png|EnchantmentBodyProtection]] ![[assets/EnchantmentHeadProtection.png|EnchantmentHeadProtection]]

May's armor and helm are enchanted with Damage Absorption, giving her a chance to absorb all damage dealt to her body and head by a single strike. Replaces Regeneration after May is defeated once.

- **Weakness** ![[assets/EnchantmentWeakness.png|EnchantmentWeakness]]

May's ranged weapon is enchanted with Weakness, giving her a chance to weaken players' attacks by 75% for 5 seconds after a successful hit.

- **Bloodrage** ![[assets/EnchantmentBloodrage.png|EnchantmentBloodrage]]

May's ranged weapon is enchanted with Bloodrage, giving May a chance to deal 200% more damage on her hit, but would also lose 30% of damage dealt in health. Replaces Weakness after May is defeated once.

## Trivia\[]

- She in Eclipse mode fight is the most difficult non raid opponents in the game, being even more difficult than eclipse Titan.
- Her mini-boss abilities are the same with other demon bosses; [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]] (Invisibility) and [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]] (Teleportation).
- May is named after a month in the calendar that shares the same name as her. This habit "naming female characters after a month" eventually became a "tradition", as a [[June (SF3)|female character]] in [[Shadow Fight 3]] was named after the sixth month (June), and the [[April|storyteller]] of [[Stories]] Mode in [[Shadow Fight 4 Arena|Shadow Fight 4: Arena]] is named after the fourth month (April).
