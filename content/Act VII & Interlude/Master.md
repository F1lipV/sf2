**Master** is the second [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], met in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 3]]. Like all other bodyguards of Titan, he is the evil counterpart of [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] from another dimension. In that dimension, he was not strong enough to resist the pull of Shadow Energy and ended up getting corrupted by it. After defeating [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]], he had then taken over his Academy instead of continuing on his journey. He considers himself to be the greatest martial arts teacher the world has ever known and establishes a school to further assert his superiority.

Physically, he appears as a strong warrior with long braided hair. He wears a red sleeveless jacket with dragons imprinted on the front. Master sees himself as a warrior without any equal. He also considers himself to be a peerless teacher, but instead of imparting knowledge, he uses duels with his disciples as a means to reassert his superiority.

Even after his defeat, he gloats about how the great school that he founded will ensure his name is remembered forever while Shadow will be simply forgotten.

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**How did Master become corrupted by Shadow Energy?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In a different dimension, Master from Shadow Fight 2 succumbed to the pull of Shadow Energy due to his inability to resist it, leading to his corruption. Post-corruption, he assumed leadership of his gang, guiding them down a destructive path. He further tried to lure Shadow with the prospect of jointly leading nations, indicating that Shadow could have led a legion if he had shown more courage.

Provided by: Fandom

**What happened to Master after defeating Hermit in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Following his victory over Hermit in Shadow Fight 2, Shadow, also referred to as Master, engages in further tournaments and challenges to gather funds for purchasing powerful equipment and weapons. He also confronts Crane, a former disciple of Sensei who had joined Hermit. After Shadow defeats Crane, Crane repents and apologizes to Sensei.

Provided by: Fandom

**Why does Master consider himself the greatest martial arts teacher?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Master from Shadow Fight 2 perceives himself as the top martial arts teacher due to his exceptional combat skills and the establishment of his martial arts academy. He reinforces his dominance through duels with his students and believes his legacy will persist through his academy, even post-defeat.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the purpose of the school established by Master in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Master sets up a school post his victory over Hermit, taking control of his Academy. He perceives himself as the world's top martial arts instructor and uses his school to validate his supremacy. The school's primary purpose is to train warriors to capture Hermit's Magic, with the exception of Mantis, a warrior who seeks to eliminate magic from martial arts.

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

Master utilizes perks and enchantments that help him with his attacks and defense (just like Shadow and Titan's other Bodyguards).

- **Avenger** ![[assets/IconAvenger.png|IconAvenger]]

A 20% chance to trigger Avenger, which grants a Critical Hit with with 138% damage for 5 seconds after receiving a Critical Hit.

- **Concussion** ![[assets/IconConcussion.png|IconConcussion]]

A 35% chance to prevent player use of magic or ranged weapons for 8 seconds after a Head Hit.

- **Steel Foot** ![[assets/IconStunningFoot.png|IconStunningFoot]]

A 25% chance to knock down players with a kick. This chance is doubled if Master is in the air.

- **Full Power** ![[assets/IconEnergyRush.png|IconEnergyRush]]

A 60% chance to increase Master's magic damage by 56% for 4 seconds after his magic is fully charged.

- **Helm Breaker** ![[assets/IconHelmBreaker.png|IconHelmBreaker]]

A 60% chance to increase Master's Head Hit damage for 5 seconds after dealing a Head Hit. Appears after Master is defeated once, in [[Eclipse]] replays only.

- **Magic Recharge**![[assets/EnchantmentMagicRecharge.png|EnchantmentMagicRecharge]]

Master's Composite Sword is enchanted with Magic Recharge, giving him a chance to increase his magic recharge from a successful hit by 300%.

- **Regeneration**![[assets/EnchantmentRegeneration.png|EnchantmentRegeneration]]

Master's Armor is enchanted with Regeneration, giving a chance to regenerate 4.5% of his health for 5 seconds after being hit.

- **Shielding**![[assets/EnchantmentShielding.png|EnchantmentShielding]]

Master's helm is enchanted with Shielding, giving him a chance to reduce incoming damage by 75% for 7 seconds after receiving Head Hit.

- **Overheat**![[assets/EnchantmentOverheat.png|EnchantmentOverheat]]

Master's ranged weapon is enchanted with Overheat, giving a chance in each strike to put a buff on him for 5 seconds that makes his next strike inflict 200% more damage.
