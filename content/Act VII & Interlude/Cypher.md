**Cypher** is a main character in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is a wise, genius old man who is trapped in the Shadow World for a long time. He becomes the replacement for [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]]'s role in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]], describing the rules of [[Challenge|challenges]] (except for the Stone Grove Challenge, which is explained by [[Ancient]] and Spaceship Challenge, which is explained by Kali) and [[Duel]] to the players, and reminds them each time they level up.

Cypher possesses a vast knowledge about the Shadow World. He often informs [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] and [[Kali]] about crucial information that can help them in beating [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]].

## Mini-boss Battle\[]

After 15 stages of the Hideout [[Tournament (SF2)|tournament]] are completed, Cypher will challenge Shadow to a friendly sparring to test his prowess. He wields the [[Telescopic Staff]] as his weapon and uses the Landmines as ranged weapon. Two rounds must be won in order to score a victory. There is a total of three rounds that can be fought. The timer for each battle is 99 seconds, as per usual conditions.

## Trivia\[]

- Cypher's landmines are different than the one in the shop; it blinks with red light instead of green light.
- He is also an inventor, having invented [[Cronos]].
- Cypher has several very strange occurrences and bugs that rarely happen:
  - Cypher can perform a "parry". In the very first frame of Cypher's landmine planting animation, he is able to block an attack while being uninterrupted. This allows Cypher to proceed with the landmine planting process.
  - Additionally, Cypher can do the same thing, but instead of proceeding with the landmine planting process, he can instead cancel it into another action. This means that, technically speaking, the player is never safe when they are facing against Cypher as he can parry any of their attacks and retaliate instantly, leaving the player with no options to counter or avoid the next hit.
  - However, these bugs are not unique to Cypher. If [[Kid]] equips Landmines, he can also perform the same bugs in the dojo. The player can also perform these bugs themself, however, it is very hard to replicate due to the extremely small window of time. It is safe to say that these bugs are caused by landmines.
