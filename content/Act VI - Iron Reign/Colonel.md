**Colonel** is the fourth [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]]. He is a high-ranking officer in Shogun's military army. Colonel appears donned in orange armor, with his hair tied in a short bun and a short beard.

Colonel wields a Bisento, a type of polearm weapon. It functions like the [[Naginata]].

## Trivia\[]

- Like other bodyguards of Shogun, Colonel wields an unobtainable weapon.
- His name is his rank in the army, just like all bodyguards of Shogun.
- He is the only bodyguard of Shogun who insults [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] after defeat by calling him a ‘monster’.
