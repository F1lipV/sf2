**Captain** is the second [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]]. He is an officer in Shogun's military army. Captain appears covered in red armor, and has a scar on his left eye.

Captain wields a long and heavy sword called the Odachi/Nodachi. It functions like the [[Dadao]]. At first, he does not believe Corporal's claim that [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] is the Prince of Ivory City. He later believes it after Shadow defeats him, asking for mercy and promising that he will leave the city.

## Trivia\[]

- Like other Shogun's bodyguards, Captain wields an unobtainable weapon.
- His name is his rank in the army, like all of Shogun's Bodyguards.
- Ōdachi (large/great sword), also known as Nodachi (field sword), is a traditional Japanese sword that was mainly used for ceremonies. Due to the size and weight, ōdachi is rarely used for battle because it is difficult to wield.
- He, along with General, Shogun's final bodyguard are Shogun's only officers who appear without the design of his seal on their armor.
