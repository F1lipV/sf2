**Corporal** is [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]]'s first [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]]. He is a soldier in Shogun's military army. Corporal is seen covered in armor and has a white bandana covering his forehead. He is a confident but gullible person.

Corporal wields a Jumonji Yari, a type of [[Yari]] which has a pair of curved blades around its central blade. This weapon can not be found in the game; it therefore can not be wielded. Despite being different, it functions similar to the Yari. Corporal is the first bodyguard to mistake [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] as the former Prince of Ivory City.

## Trivia\[]

- Like all other bodyguards of Shogun, his weapon is unobtainable.
  - Together with the Jumonji Yari, he also uses the same unobtainable Ice Ball [[Magic|magic]] used by [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]]. As of Christmas 2016 update, players can now obtain the [[Shard of Eternal Ice]] magic which functions in a similar manner.
- According to Wikipedia, Jumonji Yari can be called [[Magari Yari]] or vice versa, as they both have small differences on the curvature of their blades.
- The word Corporal is still used in modern military ranks. It is the same for some of his fellow bodyguards like [[Major]], [[Captain]], [[Colonel]], and [[General]].
- In [[Shadow Fight Shades|Shadow Fight: Shades]], Corporal is referred to as Lieutenant due to being promoted to such as he protected [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]] at the cost of his arm.
