**Wasp** is the fourth [[Bosses (SF2)|demon]] boss in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. She is fought at the conclusion of [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV: Pirate Throne]].

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What is the significance of Wasp's green and yellow clothing in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Wasp, the fourth demon boss, is recognized by her green and yellow clothing. This color combination is consistent in her outfit, headband, and naginata. Her character design also features a double bun hairstyle, a sleeveless shirt, bridal-style gauntlets, and completely white eyes, a characteristic of demons in the game.

![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In the Old Wounds timeline of Shadow Fight 2, Wasp's in-battle appearance maintains her hair in two buns. However, her character image displays a change, showing her hair in a ponytail.

Provided by: Fandom

**What strategies can be used to dodge Wasp in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, dodging Wasp, the fourth demon, involves strategic moves like rolling, ducking, or jumping over her attacks. Precise timing is essential, especially when attempting to cancel her attack by striking just before she launches off the wall. Be aware of Wasp's Precision enchantment on her Naginata and ranged weapon, which increases her damage output. Her armor and helmet also have a Damage Return enchantment, potentially returning 85% of inflicted damage back to Shadow.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the story behind Wasp's transformation into a demon boss in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Wasp transforms into the fourth demon boss following her father, the Pirate King's, disappearance. She adopts his title, becoming the new Pirate King. Wasp is unique among demons for her ability to sprout wings and fly, using this to launch attacks. Her confrontation with Shadow occurs at the conclusion of Act IV: Pirate Throne.

Provided by: Fandom

**How does Wasp's naginata contribute to her fighting style in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Wasp's naginata is a crucial part of her combat strategy. This polearm weapon is used in tandem with her unique flying ability. Wasp can ascend onto a mast, grow wings, and fly horizontally, using her naginata to slash her adversary. This is a lethal attack, but can be avoided with precise timing. The naginata also possesses a Precision enchantment, enhancing melee damage.

Provided by: Fandom

## Appearance\[]

Wasp wears mainly green-colored clothes with yellow rims. She sports a double bun hairstyle, a headband, a sleeveless shirt, and bridal-style gauntlets. Her eyes are completely white like other demons (except [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]])

In the Old Wounds timeline, her clothing is now one piece. Her cleavage is also visible. Her hair is also now down, and she has visible pupils. Her naginata in her avatar also appears slightly different.

## Storyline\[]

### Sensei's Story Part 4. The Story of Revenge\[]

Using the information provided by [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]], [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] goes to the port to find the magical Sphere.

When arriving there, he meets with Wasp, the daughter of the Pirate King. She asks him if he was sent by [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]] to retrieve the Sphere. Sensei plays along by confirming it. Unfortunately, it was a trick question, as Wasp reveals that the Sphere had been taken yesterday. She then orders her crew to do away with Sensei, saying that she will never forgive liars. When her crew fails to do so, Wasp immediately fights him. She reminds herself that she is not letting herself lose to an old man. Despite this, she is still defeated by Sensei.

Wasp then decides to tell Sensei where Widow's hiding place is, as she is not paid to fight for Widow's honor. Wasp also reveals that Widow was in love with the [[Prince]]. However, he rejected her love. Due to this, Widow is using Prince's warlord, [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]], for her revenge. Widow is planning to rule over men and play them off. The one she chooses to hold the magical Sphere will be granted immense power, but at the same time will also turn him into Widow's loyal slave.

### Act IV: Pirate Throne\[]

Wasp's father, the Pirate King, has gone missing. The Pirate King's crew has been passed on to Wasp. The crew all suspect Wasp murdered the Pirate King. Some still serve her, although they are full of dislike and suspicion, while the other crew members decide to rebel against her and form the "New Blood". Because of it, Wasp has gone into hiding.

Wasp's "secret amulet", which is said to have granted her immense power, is actually the seal that [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] and the others have been looking for. As Shadow takes down her crew, it is revealed that Wasp did indeed kill the Pirate King on Widow's suggestion in order to seize power.

### Gates of Shadows\[]

Wasp and the rest of the demons team up to stop Shadow from closing the [[Gates of Shadows]]. She battles Shadow fourth in line.

### Interlude\[]

Wasp, like her fellow demons, aims to stop Shadow from reopening the Gates. Before fight, she tells him that no one knows where Titan came from, only that he is different. After Shadow defeats Wasp and her bodyguards, she tells him that he would not want to see what lies beyond the Gates and that it was now too late to save his friend. She says he must go back while he can.

## Personality\[]

Wasp's personality differs from the typical aggressive nature of the other demons. Wasp is not necessarily aggressive herself, but does display a confident and cunning side. She appears quite malicious during her first encounter with Shadow, stating how Shadow has been helping her all along by ridding her of those who resist her. This exchange also reveals her cunning side, showing how she took advantage of Shadow without his knowledge.

When Wasp is defeated, it is suggested that she may not be as malicious as she first appeared. It is revealed that owing to her gullible nature, she accepted Widow's suggestion to betray her father, leading to a lot of conflict between her and her own crew.

Wasp admits that she only followed Widow's advice as she wanted to show men that it was women who should rule. She is shown to be confident like the Butcher, telling Shadow to "fight her for real", adding "if you can".

## Bodyguards\[]

The first three of Wasp's "bodyguards" (which truly her enemies), are the members of New Blood, a group consisting of crew members that have rebelled against her. The last two bodyguards are the few members that are still loyal (but not that loyal) to her.

1. [[Kraken]]: bodyguard armed with _[[Unavailable Known Items (SF2)|Two-Handed Sword]]_
2. [[Cleaver]]: bodyguard armed with [[Big Swords]]
3. [[Shark]]: bodyguard armed with [[Krises (SF2)|Krises]]
4. [[Bosun]]: bodyguard armed with [[Glaive]]
5. [[Whaler]]: bodyguard armed with [[Trident]]

## Equipment\[]

Along with Widow, Wasp is shown wearing a unique garb that is not obtainable in the armory, with what seems to be a Snake Harness modified to have sleeves and trousers of loose-fittings. Her weapon of choice is her Naginata (called Wasp's Naginata), a type of polearm weapon similar to the Yari. For ranged weapons, she uses Silver Shurikens, Assassin's Daggers (after being defeated once), and Shurikens of Night (after being defeated twice). Wasp uses [[Dark Implosion]], [[Lightning Arrow]] (after being defeated once), and [[Fire Pillar]] (after being defeated twice) as magic. When fought at [[Gates of Shadows]] and during the [[Interlude]], she uses Shurikens of Night and Fire Pillar.

## Perk and Enchantments\[]

- **Walljump**

![[assets/Wasp_Flight_Magic_SF2.gif|Wasp Flight Magic SF2]] ![[assets/Wasp_Flight_Magic_SF2.gif|Wasp Flight Magic SF2]]

![[assets/Wasp_Flight_Magic_SF2.gif]]

Wasp has a unique flying Perk, with which she can jump onto either wall during the fight, sprout wings and launch off of it, flying and impaling the opponent with the Naginata. Doing so allows Wasp to traverse the combat area towards the opposite end, leaving the player vulnerable afterwards to her ranged attacks and, worse case scenario, her magic abilities.

With perfect timing, the attack can be jumped over, or alternatively players can either duck or roll forwards/backwards to avoid the attack, however, if her flight attack is successful, the players must react quickly and close the distance to keep Wasp from using her Ranged Weapons and Magic. Wasp cannot use this attack if the player is standing between her and the wall, which can be used to the player's advantage. She also cannot use this attack if she is too far from either wall. Players can attack Wasp just before she launches off the wall to cancel the attack, but the timing is very short. Players cannot stop her flying attack once she reaches the wall by jumping. She will use this perk only after losing one round.

- **Damage Return**![[assets/EnchantmentDamageReturn.png|EnchantmentDamageReturn]]

Wasp's armor and helm are enchanted with Damage Return, giving a chance that players will take 85% of damage dealt on every hit.

- **Precision**![[assets/EnchantmentCriticalChance.png|EnchantmentCriticalChance]]

Wasp's Naginata and ranged weapons are enchanted with Precision, granting her more chance to make a Critical Hit with increased damage. Appears after Wasp is defeated twice.

## Strategy\[]

![[assets/WaspFlightDodge.gif|Shadow dodges Wasp's Flight attack, then closes in to defeat her using the  magic.]] ![[assets/WaspFlightDodge.gif|Shadow dodges Wasp's Flight attack, then closes in to defeat her using the  magic.]]

![[assets/WaspFlightDodge.gif]]

Shadow dodges Wasp's Flight attack, then closes in to defeat her using the [[Wrath of Flame]] magic.

Wasp is a deceptive character who baits Shadow to go for a wall game. While most scenarios are better with a wall game, Wasp is the opposite. Her flight attack is almost impossible to dodge at point blank, so it is advised to plant Shadow between her and the closest wall. It is also advised not to use weapons with high damage output in single bursts due to her Damage Return.

Due to the annoyance of Wasp's flight attack, weapons with extremely long _disjointed_ range, like Kusarigama (conveniently unlocked at level 24) and Sai are recommended to outwit Wasp's Naginata without having to go too close to her and leaving themselves open to the flight attack, which deals significant damage. If you're pushing Wasp near a wall with your attacks, **stop attacking her** and wait for her to use the flight attack before attacking again. The invincibility frames of Wasp's flight attack lasts a very long time, even after the attack has initially concluded, so if the player wishes to counter her flight, do so with a ranged weapon attack. The magic attacks [[Fire Pillar]], and in Eclipse mode, [[Force Ray]], [[Toxic Cloud]], [[Wrath of Flame]] and [[Torturer's Rage]] can reliably punish Wasp due to their long active frames, but the windup for Force Ray and Toxic Cloud are much longer and requires the user to duck the lunge or stand closer to Wasp's takeoff point to gain the extra time needed, in which both has higher risks of failing compared to rolling or standing farther away; and Wrath of Flame only works up close, which can be worked around by positioning Shadow farther away from Wasp and moving closer to her while she's recovering via rolling.

Aside from that there's not much more to be noted about Wasp, as the flight attack is the only thing that makes Wasp challenging.

## Trivia\[]

- Wasp and her status as a pirate captain is most likely inspired by Shi Yang, who hailed after the Golden Age of Piracy and was rendered as the most successful pirate in history.
- Wasp's clothes, headband and naginata are all green in color.
- Wasp is the least aggressive of the Demons and reveals herself only because Shadow demands it, not wanting a fight herself.
- She is the first female demon encountered in the game.
- Wasp is the only demon, besides [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]] and [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], who does not dual-wield her weapon.
- Wasp can sprout wings like an actual wasp, when using her flying perk by leaping from a wall and thrusting anything in its path.
- Wasp is the only demon that has a family member mentioned in-game, that being the Pirate King, her father.
- Wasp's picture, along with many other bosses, characters and fighters, was changed in one of the early Shadow Fight 2 updates.
