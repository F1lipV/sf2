**Shark** is [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]'s third [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]]. She is a member of the New Blood, like [[Kraken]] and [[Cleaver]]. Shark wields the [[Krises (SF2)|Krises]] as her weapons. She wears blue sleeveless clothes with a brown belt and a red cap, and she also has a pair of golden earrings. Her hair is a light shade of yellow.
