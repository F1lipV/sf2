**Cleaver** is the second [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]. He is a member of the New Blood, like [[Kraken]] and [[Shark]]. He uses [[Big Swords]] as his weapon of choice. He wears black pants and a green bandana, as well as golden wrist guards and a few tattoos on his upper body.
