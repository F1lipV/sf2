**Kraken** is the first [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]. He is a member of the New Blood, along with [[Cleaver]] and [[Shark]]. Kraken wears a blue belt. He has no shirt or headgear, wears a spiked pauldron on one shoulder and has his arms adorned with square-shaped tattoos. He wields an unobtainable Two-Handed Sword.

## Trivia\[]

- He is the second bodyguard to be named after a mythical creature, the other being [[Dragon]] from [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]].
- The two-handed sword he wields has the same model as the [[Dadao]], with the only difference being that his weapon is coded distinctly from within the files.
- It does not make much sense for him to be a "Bodyguard" for Wasp, since New Blood is a group against Wasp.
