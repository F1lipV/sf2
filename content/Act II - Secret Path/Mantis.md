**Mantis** is the third [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]] in [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]] of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He wields [[Oriental Sabers]] as his weapons of choice. Mantis, like all disciples of Hermit, is named after a martial arts style: _the Praying Mantis Kung Fu_. Like the earlier disciples of Hermit, he really does not respect his master, due to his strong aversion of [[Magic]]. Unlike other disciples, who want to use Hermit's powers for themselves, Mantis wants to destroy it because he thinks that Magic has no place in martial arts. Mantis is well-built and appears as a monk like figure, covered in red robes.

In Act II, Mantis tells [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] that he will get Hermit's power and it will end with him. Once he is defeated, he reveals Hermit's secret, Magic, and warns Shadow to beware of magic.

He returns in [[Interlude]], where he fights Shadow as Hermit's third bodyguard.
