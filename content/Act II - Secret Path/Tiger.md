**Tiger** is the fourth [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]] who first appears in [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]]. He is a disciple in Hermit's school. Like every other bodyguard of Hermit, he takes his name from a martial arts style,\*The Tiger Style Gong fu\*. He has intentions similar to the other bodyguards of Hermit which is to acquire Hermit's power, [[Magic]]. Tiger appears as a warrior with his hair tied in a bun, and covered in armor. He uses [[Steel Claws]] as his weapon to fight against [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]].
