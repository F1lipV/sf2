**Dragon** is the first [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]]. Dragon, like all of Hermit's bodyguards is named after a style of martial arts: _the Hidden Dragon_.

Dragon is one of Hermit's disciples. Like the other disciples (other than [[Mantis]]), he only wants to get Hermit's secret power so he can use it for his own purposes.

Dragon wields a [[Staff]]. He does not wear a helmet, and instead wears a green bandana and green robes with gold trimmings and gold ornaments. He sees himself as the superior of Hermit's disciples, yet is the weakest.

When defeated, Dragon admits he must "learn his lesson of humiliation". In the [[Interlude]], it is seen that he is still in Hermit's academy and fights [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] as Hermit's first bodyguard.

## Trivia\[]

- Dragon is one of the only two bodyguards who have a name of a mythological beast, the other being [[Kraken]].
- In _[[Shadow Fight Shades|Shades]]_, his name is Ryuji, possibly a play on the word ‘Ryu’, meaning a Japanese dragon.
- According to Shades, he is Kanade and [[Kenji]]'s cousin (for Kenji is unknown) and (possibly) [[Irbis|Ibris]]' nephew.
