**Hermit** is a [[Bosses (SF2)|demon boss]] and the second demon encountered in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Hermit is an elderly and wise teacher who is feared for his devastating, mysterious powers. He runs a martial arts school which, built by himself from scratch; upon doing so, Hermit held a competition to find the one he deems worthy to be taught his secret power. Hermit's bodyguards are the competitors of his competition, four of which are fighters who want to discover what his hidden power is and use it for their own benefits, while the other one only aims to destroy it.

**What is the significance of Hermit's martial arts school in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

The martial arts school of Hermit in Shadow Fight 2, built by Hermit, is the sole operational fighting academy in the town. Hermit, a respected demon boss and sage, uses this school to identify deserving students for his powerful, enigmatic abilities, termed Magic. The school is a hub of intrigue, with disciples training under Hermit to acquire his Magic, except for outliers like Mantis, who rejects the role of magic in martial arts.

**What are some of the mysterious powers that Hermit possesses?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Hermit, a Sensei from Shadow Fight 2, is renowned for his mystical abilities. He's the second seal-wielding Demon and can cast advanced magic spells, often used in combat. His unique demon skill is creating magical thunder. He also uses Chinese one-handed swords, or Jian swords. Despite opening a school to share his knowledge, most of his students are self-serving, hoping to uncover the secrets of his immense power.

In Shadow Fight 2, the protagonist Shadow encounters Hermit as the second demon. Upon Hermit's defeat, Shadow gains the Green Seal and Hermit's secret power, Magic. An additional victory over Hermit in Eclipse mode yields his weapon as a reward.

**What is the story behind Hermit's fear of his own magic?**

In Shadow Fight 2, Hermit is fearful of his magic due to the potent forces it can release. He once caused fear with his magic, but now acknowledges forces far more powerful, which he doesn't fully comprehend. He cautions Shadow about these forces, notably Titan, capable of controlling people's will. Despite his fear, Hermit is prepared to battle to hinder the reopening of the Gates of Shadows.

## Appearance\[]

Hermit has long white facial hair and wears a light green tanzhuang that is decorated with the symbol of the demons (the gilded symbol that is also on [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]'s body armor), and a big conical hat. He also wields [[Hermit's Swords]].

In the Old Wounds timeline, he remains the same, with only slight changes, such as modified designs on his attire, and visible pupils.

## Storyline\[]

### Sensei's Story Part 2: Shadow of the Future\[]

After saving the [[Prince]] from Lynx and hearing his story about Hermit who trained his soldiers, [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] is certain that must be the reason the Prince become an assassination target. He, accompanied by Prince, decides to confront Hermit.

When they meet, Hermit is displeased to find that Prince is still alive. He admits that it is true that he betrayed Prince and has hired Lynx to eliminate him, but he needs to do it all for his school's future, which is at stake. Hermit then chooses to eliminate Prince with the help of his disciples, saying that Prince is a sacrificial needed to attain glory for his school. However, Sensei proceeds to stop Hermit and fights him and his disciples. After Sensei is victorious, he berates Hermit for selling his honor by betraying someone, stating that no one would want to follow a traitor's lead. Hermit retorts that this is the only way to save his school, and he reveals that [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]] has ordered him to train his men and promised him protection. Prince corrects him that they are his men, and asks Hermit to tell him the bandit's hideout location, if he still has honor. Hermit replies by telling them where to find [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]], the Prince's kidnapper who was hired by Shogun.

### Act II: Secret Path\[]

After [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]]'s victory against Lynx, Sensei tells the group about his disciple who left to try the new schools in the next town. He says that the town is known for its fighting academies and the group travels there. However, when they reach the town, Sensei finds the academies in ruins and is distressed. He is shocked to find that there is only one functional fighting school there which is being run by Hermit. According to [[Dragon]], one of the competitors, there is a competition, full of aspiring warriors that are trying to get a place at Hermit's academy. The winner will get to know what Hermit's secret power is.

Shadow then participates in the competition and defeats more and more of the competitors, all the while learning more about Hermit, his school, his teachings and the competitors themselves. It is revealed that they actually resent him and only want to discover his secret in order to use it for their own benefits. Later, they also find out from Mantis that Hermit secret power is [[Magic|magic]], which is even more powerful than that of the much greater demons.

Shadow finally meets [[Crane]], Sensei's missing disciple who is now competing in Hermit's competition in hopes of learning the secrets to his power. Sensei is shattered upon finding out what Crane has done. When Shadow bests Crane in a fight, he comes to his senses and apologizes to Sensei.

At last, Shadow meets Hermit himself. Hermit invites Shadow to do battle against him and test his skills. During the fight, Hermit holds back initially but when Shadow shows his talent, Hermit immediately steps up his game, using extremely advanced techniques. Hermit then introduces his legendary magic, firing electric bolts and water blasts. He also unleashes his own self-controlled thunderstorm which makes lightning zap the ground.

Shadow, however, still manages to defeat Hermit. Hermit is impressed and negotiates with Shadow. He asks Shadow to visit the next town, battle the band of outlaws there, and eliminate their leader - who is none other than the gang leader Butcher who is harassing him, trying to discover the secret to his combat prowess. As gratitude to Shadow's willingness to help, Hermit taught him the secret to his power, Magic.

### Interlude\[]

Shadow aims to defeat Hermit in order to re-open the [[Gates of Shadows]], so he can rescue [[May (SF2)|May]]. Before the fight, Hermit warns Shadow that there was a time when everyone was afraid of his magic, but now there are far more powerful forces-things that even he, Hermit, does not know about. All he knows is that they should be feared. Shadow still fights with Hermit's bodyguards and then Hermit, defeating them all. Hermit realizes Shadow simply will not stop and warns him that [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]] can control people's will and Shadow will not be able to fight that.

## Equipment and Abilities\[]

While he is shown wearing a tanzhuang, Hermit is seemingly donning an attire consisting of a Robe and a Conical Helmet during combat, he wields a pair of jian, which uses a similar attack pattern to that of the basic Sais, the only difference it has is its Super Slash demonstrating multiple sword thrusts against opponents. With his mastery in magic, he wields [[Lightning Arrow]] in the first two rounds, with [[Water Ball]] utilized after being defeated twice. Hermit uses various ranged weapons, including Throwing Spikes, Hunting Knives (after defeated once), and Needles (after defeated twice).

Hermit's secret power is found in his knowledge of magic, as divulged by Mantis. Not only is his magic advanced, in the sense that magic spells of the caliber which he uses will not be unlocked for more than three acts, but Hermit is also the first enemy to use magic. He switches his ranged weapon for each round the player defeats him.

Also, like his fellow demons, Hermit has a special perk which allows him to summon a lightning storm. He activates it by propelling himself into the air. He then appears to meditate, and whilst levitating, he summons lightning bolts to strike where the player is. To interrupt Hermit's storm perk, the player can either hit him with a ranged weapon while he is levitating or attack him with melee attacks. The latter is a risky move though, because his spinning swords can hit the player, leaving them vulnerable to his first lightning.

## Personality\[]

Hermit is one of the less aggressive demons. He does not often insult, and most of the things he says are benevolent, such as his frequent combat advice. Hermit, being a teacher, believes strongly in his teachings, and he only fights for the benefit of the challenger - to teach the challenger. Hermit acknowledges a strong fighter when he meets one, and places trust in those who he believes are reliable. He entrusts Shadow of dealing with the [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]], in exchange for his magic.

However, Hermit shows a cautious side in addition to his calm, benevolent side. His personality becomes adamant when he is warning Shadow not to reopen the Gates of Shadow and release Titan, and even goes as far as assigning all of his disciples and bringing himself along to fight Shadow to stop him. Hermit also shows his fear, as he warns Shadow that Titan is dangerous.

In addition, Hermit leaps into action very quickly, instantly challenging Shadow to a duel the first time he meets him.

## Bodyguards\[]

Hermit's bodyguards are his experienced disciples and are competitors in his tournament. All of them hold no genuine respect for Hermit: Four of them only want his secret power, while one aims to destroy it.

1. [[Dragon]]: Bodyguard armed with [[Staff]]

4. [[Tiger]]: Bodyguard armed with [[Steel Claws (SF2)|Steel Claws]]

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

- **Lightning Storm**

![[assets/Lightning_Storm_Magic_SF2.gif|Lightning Storm Magic SF2]] ![[assets/Lightning_Storm_Magic_SF2.gif|Lightning Storm Magic SF2]]

![[assets/Lightning_Storm_Magic_SF2.gif]]

Hermit can use a deadly magic by levitating and meditating. While levitating, he spins his swords to hit Shadow, knocking him down or preventing him from interrupting the skill. He summons a storm wherein lightning from the sky perpetually attempts to hit Shadow, instantly knocking him to the ground and dealing damage on impact. Hermit remains in this state until knocked off by the player using any form of attack, or until the round ends. The lightning bolt is counted as a magic attack; therefore, it cannot shock the player, or deal head hit or critical damage. Hermit will start using this perk after being defeated once.

- **Magic Recharge![[assets/EnchantmentMagicRecharge.png|Magic Recharge]]**

Hermit's Armor and helm are enchanted with Magic Recharge, which grants him a chance to allow him to increase his magic recharge by 700% from getting hit.

- **Enfeeble![[assets/EnchantmentEnfeeble.png|Enfeeble]]**

Hermit's Swords and ranged weapons are enchanted with Enfeeble - this allows him to weaken the player's attacks by 75% for several seconds after a successful hit. Appears after defeated twice.

It is important to remember Hermit will have his magic charged and ready much faster than other opponents. Quick movement is advised because of this. When Hermit begins meditating one is advised to dish out a high-hitting kick while avoiding his spinning swords so that they do not get knocked down and in a compromising position to be struck by lightning. Aside from this, Hermit's damage output is lower than a usual boss. Heavy weapons with huge burst damage are a good option.

## Trivia\[]

- Hermit's Conical Hat is similar to the one in the shop, but is larger in size. His hair is also visible under the hat.
- The characterization behind Hermit drew inspiration from many East Asian media and literature, one being the stereotypical Master wise and knowledgeable in the practice of martial arts and self-discipline, the other being Hermit's circulation around magic as a caricature of the Chinese philosophy of Chi and the misconceptions it created.
  - The animated background used during Shadow's fight with Hermit and his bodyguards is a reference to the chess courtyard from the 2002 martial arts movie Hero, where the characters Nameless (Jet Li) and Sky (Donnie Yen) fight.
  - His bodyguards have names of animals, most of which are associated with Chinese culture.
  - Hermit's weapon is based on the Chinese Jian, a double-edged straight sword. As shown by Hermit, the martial arts behind the Jian sword traditionally include one-handed thrust-oriented strikes, although it also welcomes rapid slashing movements which Hermit has demonstrated only twice during the intro sequence.
- Hermit can be seen fighting Shadow at Ivory City (where [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]]'s empire and Shogun himself is located) in the intro.
- When Hermit wins a round while he is still in Lightning Storm mode, he will instantly snap to his end-of-fight stance on the ground, while still retaining the blue aura around him.
- His counterpart in [[Shadow Fight 1]] is [[Wanderer]].
  - In the German version of the game, Hermit is called Wanderer which has no direct translation but relates to Walker.
- Hermit is the least aggressive demon, which is reflected by his speech.
