**Crane** is the fifth and final [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]] in [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]] of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He, like other Hermit's disciples, wants to learn [[Magic|Hermit's magic power]]. Crane was earlier a disciple of [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]]. Sensei informs [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] after he defeats [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]] that his disciple (Crane) has gone missing and that Sensei wishes to search for him in the nearby town where events of Act II occur.

After Shadow wins the fight with Hermit's fourth disciple, [[Tiger]], Shadow and his company learn that the fifth disciple is none other than Crane himself who ran away in search of knowing and learning Hermit's secret power which is magic. He prevents Shadow from going ahead to battle Hermit so that only he acquires the secret power of Hermit. But after Shadow wins against him, he accepts defeat, apologises to Sensei, and admits that Shadow has made him realize his mistakes. However, he returns in [[Interlude]] to fight as Hermit's fifth bodyguard.

## Trivia\[]

- Crane is the only bodyguard in the entire game to fight barehanded.
- Crane's name, like all the previous bodyguards of Hermit, comes from a style of fighting: the _Fujian White Crane._
