**Ghost** is the fourth [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is a mysterious, quiet figure, who has learned a lot from his last experiences and seems to know more about Lynx than anyone else in the order. He seems to be the most agile and skilled member of the order besides [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]. When approached by [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]], Ghost claims that he defeated Lynx easily when he met him. He removed Lynx's mask, and saw only a dummy. Then, the real Lynx dug his claws into the back of his neck. Ghost claims to have "served him ever since". He likely gained his nickname "Ghost" because of his near death experience when he was played by Lynx.

Ghost wields a [[Ninja Sword (SF2)|Ninja Sword]]. He is the bodyguard in The Order who resembles Lynx the most. Ghost is fought first in [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] and again in the [[Interlude]].

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What role does Ghost play in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Ghost is the fourth bodyguard of Lynx and a skilled assassin. Known for his stealth, he can temporarily blind opponents. His weapons of choice are a pair of sai and a throwing knife, which he uses to poison opponents on successful hits. Ghost is encountered as the third boss in Act 1.

Provided by: Fandom

**How did Ghost manage to defeat Lynx in their encounter?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Ghost, an adept assassin, outwitted Lynx in their encounter. He claims to have easily bested Lynx, unmasking a decoy. Despite Lynx's surprise attack, Ghost prevailed. His victory can be attributed to his extensive knowledge about Lynx, derived from past experiences.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is unique about Ghost's fighting style in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight: Shades, Ghost is a formidable opponent as the third boss of Act 1. His fighting style is unique, centered on stealth. He has the ability to blind opponents temporarily and execute sneak attacks, showcasing his skills as a former bodyguard and skilled assassin.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the significance of Lynx's mask in his encounter with Ghost?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Ghost, a member of The Order, had a notable encounter with Lynx. Upon defeating Lynx and removing his mask, Ghost discovered a dummy. The actual Lynx then assaulted Ghost, resulting in Ghost's allegiance to Lynx. This event showcases Lynx's deceptive tactics and his capacity to control skilled assassins like Ghost.

Provided by: Fandom

**What does Ghost's character reveal about Lynx in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Ghost, the fourth bodyguard of Lynx in Shadow Fight 2, is a master of stealth. His interactions with Lynx, the boss of Chapter 4 Act 1 and leader of the Order, reveal Lynx as a formidable opponent. Lynx uses long claws and a kunai in combat. After being defeated by Lynx, Ghost claims to have served him, highlighting Lynx's dominance and power.

Provided by: Fandom
