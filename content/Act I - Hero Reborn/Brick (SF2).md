**Brick** is a [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is the second bodyguard of [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]. Brick is a member of the secret organization called The Order. Brick serves as the intimidator of The Order, and scoffs at [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] when he is challenged. He uses his size to intimidate challengers. Brick uses [[Steel Batons|batons]] for weapons. He is well built and has a yellow mohawk. Brick fights Shadow first in [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] and again later in the [[Interlude]].
