**Needle** is [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]'s third [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. She is the only female bodyguard of Lynx and the first female bodyguard encountered in the game. Her weapon of choice are the [[Sai (SF2)|Sai]]. She is the first to mention The Order of all the bodyguards. She upholds the Order's practices, and seems to understand the consequences of failure. She warns [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] that he will be actively pursued for attacking The Order. She appears in [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] and later reappears in the [[Interlude]] where she battles Shadow again as Lynx's third bodyguard. After defeating her, the [[Underworld]] will be unlocked.

## Appearance\[]

She sports golden hair and wears a grey bra under a blue shirt as well as grey fingerless gloves.
