**Lynx** is a [[Bosses (SF2)|demon]] boss in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is the first demon to be encountered in the game. Lynx has five [[Bodyguards|bodyguards]], who need to be defeated before the player can fight him. Lynx is the founder and the leader of an assassin organization known as "The Order". His bodyguards are members of this organization. He is the only enemy who has the Time Bomb [[Enchantments|enchantment]] on a weapon.

## Appearance\[]

Lynx looks much like the stereotypical Ninja, clad in gilded black Armor and a mask which leaves only his eyes and the upper portion of his nose exposed. He bears the Demons' symbol on his shoulder, headband, and belt.

In the Old Wounds timeline, Lynx does not wear the headpiece that he wears in Shadow's timeline and only has a piece of black cloth covering his nose and mouth. He wears a sleeveless dark-grey jacket with bright gold highlights and light-grey patches. He is also shown to have black hair which he wears in a bun. He also wears shiny light-silver armor on his forearms and has black cloth covering his biceps and elbows. He has pupils whereas in Shadow's timeline he does not have any. The symbol he has on his armor has a similar appearance to the symbol in Hermit's Armor.

## Storyline\[]

### Sensei's Story Part 1: The Witness\[]

Lynx, along with his assassins [[Spark (SF2SE)|Spark]] and [[Cleric]], chases after the [[Prince]] before [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] bumps into them and Prince asks for his help, saying that they are trying to murder him. Lynx tries to deceive Sensei by stating that the Prince is out of his mind and they were tasked with bringing the Prince back home. After seeing that Sensei is not believing it, Lynx decides to eliminate Sensei in order to remove witness. Sensei, however, manages to defeat Lynx and his bodyguards, saving the Prince's life. It is later revealed that Lynx was hired by [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]] in order to eliminate the Prince.

### Act I: Hero Reborn\[]

Lynx first appears at the tutorial, when Sensei tells [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] that he thinks that he is ready to defeat Lynx, noting that he is still in shape. Lynx does not acknowledge Shadow's appearance, calling him a "lowly worm" and saying that Shadow is not worthy of fighting him. Lynx believes he is too superior to bother wasting his time fighting Shadow, and introduces [[Shin]], the weakest member of his organization. He demands that Shin be defeated before he can have a chance of battling him.

When Shin is defeated, Lynx steps forward and introduces himself. He explains that he is the leader of the Assassin's Order. He states that Shadow is not worthy of the honor of battling him, but comments that the "battle" with Shin was entertaining, despite mocking him as well, stating that their battle was hardly a battle.

Lynx disappears for a time, until all of his bodyguards are defeated in combat and he has no choice but to face Shadow. When he is defeated, he is in denial. He becomes worried, because his entire organization is at stake. But when he learns that Shadow is only interested in his Seal, he hands over the seal with no complaints, calling the Seal a mere trinket. He tells Shadow to be gone from his sight, and warns him that he will not forget what Shadow has done.

### Gates of Shadows\[]

Lynx appears once more at the [[Gates of Shadows]], where he attempts to stop Shadow from using the demon seals to lock him and the other demons away. At the Gates of Shadows, Lynx must be fought once again but this time he is fought in the company of his fellow demons. Before the fight, he greets Shadow.

### Interlude\[]

After the Gates of Shadows, Lynx must be fought once again. This time, Lynx is fighting for a good cause, as he does not want Shadow to re-open the Gates of Shadows in order to save [[May (SF2)|May]], who was captured by [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]. He states that Titan is all-powerful and will wreak havoc upon their realm if Shadow were to open the Gates.

## Equipment and Abilities\[]

During combat, Lynx sports a Light Helm and a Leather Jacket as his armor (though in the cameo, he is shown wearing a samurai chest piece), he wields a pair of three-bladed retractable arm [[Lynx's Claws|claws]] as his primary weapon and, being the first enemy encountered to use [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapons]], carries Shurikens and Throwing Daggers. Both his claws and ranged weapons are [[Enchantments|enchanted]] with Time Bomb and Poisoning. The chance to get inflicted is very high, regardless of the player's level and Armor power. His special demon ability allows him to disappear in a puff of smoke and turn invisible.

He will start using ranged weapons and invisibility after the player defeats him once. Upon reaching the Gates of Shadows and during the Interlude, he uses [[Force Wave]] as his Magic.

## Strategy\[]

As mentioned above, Lynx uses many weapons that are not available to the player, namely Claws, ranged weapons and special abilities. Claws are weapons that excel in close combat (unsurprisingly), with quick hits and hits that causes long hitstun on the player, allowing for easy followups for Lynx which he usually exploits. While Lynx's claws are capable of long ranged combat, the moves that can do so are slow and have strict hitboxes, so using longer ranged weapons like Ninja Sword, Machetes or the Blood Reaper kusarigama will give the player a big advantage. Sais are also longer ranged weapons, but its endlag makes it undesirable against Lynx's counterattacks on block since his claws has a high chance to inflict time bomb, which in _Shadow Fight 2_ sends Shadow flying along with dealing a ton of damage. Due to this, even with a longer ranged weapon the player must still be careful when going in hand to hand.

Ranged weapons can be ducked under, rolled under, jumped over, and interrupted, but not blocked. Lynx's ranged weapons are also enchanted with time bomb, so if Shadow is too far away, time his rolls to get closer to Lynx by exploiting the downtime between his ranged weapon throws, then counter with a quick hit if he managed to get close.

As for his invisibility, a relentless wave of attacks should prevent him from going invisible. If he does go invisible, don't panic and focus on blocking and dodging his attacks, then use the camera's zoom angle to determine Lynx's position before countering.

## Personality\[]

Lynx is an arrogant character, believing very strongly in his abilities and viewing those who serve him as insignificant. He will not entertain any challengers until they have proven "worthy" of the honor of fighting him, a sign of his arrogance and belief of his own superiority over others.

Lynx, despite being arrogant and viewing most of the other people other than himself as pawns, values his organization the Assassin's Order highly. He is terrified at the prospect of losing his authority over the organization after he is defeated by Shadow, and is relieved to learn that Shadow is only after the seal he bears.

Lynx is extremely aggressive and quick-footed in combat, and his assassins respect and fear him immensely. He is also known to be deceitful and cunning, as he managed to trick Ghost, a skilled assassin, into serving him.

Lynx does admit when an opponent is stronger than him, however - even if it is extremely rare. This is shown when Lynx opens up about his extreme fear of Titan, and does everything in his power to prevent Titan from being freed. Lynx acknowledges Titan's strength. Lynx's inferiority to Titan is shown when Lynx is rendered incapable of movement from just hearing the voice of Titan ringing in his head.

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

- **Invisibility ![[assets/IconInvisibility_Blue.png|IconInvisibility Blue]]**

Lynx can become invisible after throwing a smoke bomb to the ground and stays the same until the player damages him heavily, the player deals a critical hit, or the round ends. While invisible, Lynx will not use ranged weapons. Lynx uses this perk after being defeated once.

- **Time Bomb![[assets/EnchantmentTimeBomb.png|Time Bomb]]**

Lynx's Claws and ranged weapon are enchanted with Time Bomb, giving him a chance to set a delayed explosive on Shadow, which will detonate after 2 seconds.

- **Poisoning![[assets/EnchantmentIntoxication.png|Poisoning]]**

Lynx's claws and ranged weapons are enchanted with Poisoning, giving a chance that Shadow will start losing 3% health per second over 5 seconds after being hit. This enchantment appears after he is defeated twice. It is immediately active in [[Gates of Shadows]] and [[Interlude]]. However, Lynx's claws will only contain the Time Bomb enchantment when obtained by the player.

Since Lynx has no enchantments on his body and has enhanced offensive capabilities, it is recommended to adopt a rush down strategy to prevent him touching you and lacing Poisoning or Time Bomb on you. Wielding a weapon with precision is also a good idea so that you can knock him out of his invisibility faster.

## Trivia\[]

- Real life ancient feudal shinobis used fist-loaded weapons in their arsenal of unconventional weapons, the closest variant similar to Lynx's claws is the Tekkō-kagi backhand hooks. The blades, however, weren't mechanically retractable and as lengthy as what Lynx currently wields.
- In conjunction with the game introducing Enchantment mechanics for the later chapters, Lynx is the only demon who does not have any [[Enchantments|enchantments]] on his [[Armor (SF2)|armor]] and [[Helm (SF2)|helm]] except for his personal weapon.
  - Lynx is the only demon who has two enchantments on his weapons, being Poisoning and Time Bomb.
    - After obtaining Lynx's [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]], the player can only get the time bomb enchantment but not poisoning.
- Lynx is the only demon, apart from [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], who does not use more than one magic.
- [[Tenebris]] also wields retractable claws although it has never been canonically confirmed if it's the exact same weapon as Lynx's.
- It is believed that Lynx used to be the hardest player to face in [[Shadow Fight 2]] due to his weapon perks.
- Interestingly, no one has ever seen his face except [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]].
