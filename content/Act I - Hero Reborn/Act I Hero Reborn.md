**Act I: Hero Reborn** is the first act in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It comprises 5 modes of play: [[Tournament (SF2)|Tournaments]], Storyline fights, [[Survival]], [[Duel]], and [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]], the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] exclusive.

Act I is set in a small town. [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] comes there after he had opened the [[Gates of Shadows]] and lost his flesh and soul, becoming a mere silhouette of his former self. In there, he meets [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] and [[May (SF2)|May]]. They accompany Shadow on his quest to collect the [[Gates of Shadows#Seals|demon seals]], in hope of closing the gates he opened and restoring his human form.

### Survival\[]

![[assets/Survival.png|Survival]]

[[Survival]] is unlocked after completing 7 Tournament stages. It is a mode where the player fights multiple enemies at once, up to 10 of them, in ascending difficulty. The more enemies defeated, the more coins the player receives. This fight takes place in a Bamboo Forest. At the completion of round 10, the player is awarded the achievement "Panda Bear" for the completion of Survival in Act I.

Rewards for completion of each round are:

1. 40 coins, 1 Green Orb (during Eclipse), 2 gems (in Special Edition)
2. 90 coins, 2 Green, 4 gems
3. 140 coins, 3 Green, 6 gems
4. 190 coins, 4 Green, 8 gems
5. 250 coins, 5 Green, 10 gems
6. 320 coins, 6 Green, 12 gems
7. 390 coins, 7 Green, 14 gems
8. 470 coins, 8 Green, 16 gems
9. 560 coins, 9 Green, 18 gems
10. 640 coins, 10 Green, 20 gems

### Duel\[]

![[assets/Duel.png|Duel]]

[[Duel]] is unlocked after defeating two of Lynx's bodyguards. It has a maximum of three rounds in every try; two of which must be won to score a victory. Rewards in this act are some XP and coins. The player is given random equipment in duels. It is only available every 4 hours and requires internet connection to initiate. It takes place in Misty Mountain.

### Old Wounds (Special Edition only)\[]

![[assets/Oldwounds.png|Oldwounds]]

[[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] is an exclusive game mode which tells the past of Sensei, narrated by Sensei himself. It is available only in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]], unlocked after completing three tournament stages. The story in this part is about Sensei's encounter with the [[Prince]] and Lynx. Rewards are an amount of gems. It takes place in Sacred Groves.

## Storyline\[]

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “ | _Once, I was a great invincible warrior and no one dared to stand in my way. I travelled the lands searching for a worthy battle, until I discovered the Gates Of Shadows. I broke the laws of elders and opened the gates. The demons of other worlds were beyond them. They rushed upon me, tearing my flesh, draining my soul, turning me into a Shadow. And now for eternity, I am doomed to wander the world, and fight the demons I freed!_ | ” |
| –Shadow's monologue in the game intro. | | |

Shadow was formerly a very powerful combatant. His arrogance led him to defy the rules established by his ancestors and he opened the Gates of Shadows, freeing the demons who had been sealed within it. Upon doing so, he quickly realized his mistake but it was already too late and he lost his flesh and soul to the demons, becoming a mere "shadow" of his former self, sans any physical body.

### Prelude\[]

After losing his body, Shadow returns to his native village where his Sensei also resides. Sensei mocks him for being "vain" and blames his arrogance and brashness for his current predicament. He decides to test Shadow to see what remains of his combat abilities. He trials Shadow against a punching bag, and from a glance concludes that Shadow still retains some of his fighting potential. Then Sensei tests Shadow against Kenji, his other disciple. Shadow is victorious, and Sensei commends him.

Sensei then suggests that Shadow challenge the demon Lynx for his seal. Lynx, however, pits him against his first bodyguard, Shin, saying that Shadow must defeat all his bodyguards before he can challenge him.

### The Order\[]

When Shadow successfully defeats Shin he meets a major character in the story - May. From this point onwards, May accompanies Shadow and Sensei on their quest to collect the demon seals. May also introduces Shadow to tournaments and survival, which he uses to earn money tofund his arsenal of weapons and armor. Shadow also learns about the criminal organization, the Order, being headed by Lynx. Shadow then defeats each of Lynx's bodyguards (Duels are unlocked upon defeating the second bodyguard), learning more and more about the Order and its members with each battle. He also fights Trickster, a talented fighter who gives up his Nunchacku if Shadow manages to best him in combat.

By this time, Shadow is ready to fight Lynx. The battle occurs at night time on a rooftop. Lynx invites Shadow to join the Order, but Shadow declines and insists a fight take place. The fight against Lynx however is a challenging one forcing Shadow to stay within close range, as Lynx uses ranged weapons which are unavailable for the player at that time. Shadow defeats Lynx, and Lynx becomes fearful of losing control of the Order. But when he realizes that Shadow only seeks the seal, he surrenders it and leaves. Shadow then unlocks the use of ranged weapons.
