**Shin** is [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]'s first bodyguard and the easiest one to defeat. He is the first bodyguard the players will encounter in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He makes his first appearance when Sensei demands Shadow To fight Lynx, but he dismisses it by saying he will not waste his time and orders his weakest bodyguard, Shin, to take care of Shadow. Thus Shadow goes on to fight Lynx's bodyguards in order to get to him and he begins with Shin. Shin is a bodyguard armed with Kunais. Shin is a member of the mysterious organization known as "The Order", which is run by Lynx. Shin later reappears to fight Shadow again in the [[Interlude]].

## Trivia\[]

- During the first fight, Shin can never deal a critical hit.
- In the first fight with Shin, when the player health is low, Shin will stop attacking and will be only dodging player attacks (Like [[Kenji]]).
- Shin is the only bodyguard in the whole game with no speaking role.
- He is also the only bodyguard to be referred to as weak, even before fighting the player.
- He wields melee Kunais that resemble [[Daggers]], but cannot be obtained by the player.
