**Staff of Night** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] that is unlocked in [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is a dark-purpleish staff with a red orb on the top. The Staff of Night is unlocked at level 27, along with the [[Two-handed Mace]]. It works like the [[Staff]] from [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]]. It is a part of the "Night" set, along with the [[Helm (SF2)|Crown of Night]], [[Armor (SF2)|Mantle of Night]], and [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|Shurikens of Night]], indicated by the red orb on most of the equipment from the set.
