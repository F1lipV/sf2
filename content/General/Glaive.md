**Glaive** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] unlocked at level 23 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It consists of two long blades connected to each other at its handle forming a quarterstaff-like weapon and has somewhat high damage, moderate range, and somewhat fast speed. Glaive is used by [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]'s fourth bodyguard, [[Bosun]], the fighter [[Fighters|Oldman]], and their [[Ninja]] counterpart.

## Trivia\[]

- The historical Glaive was a European polearm that consisted of a single-edged blade at the end of a pole, similar to Guandao or Naginata.
- Contrary to popular depiction, such a weapon would be quite impractical due to the chance of hitting oneself with the unwieldy weapon it would be.
