Challengers are skilled fighters that appear in [[Shadow Fight 2]] exclusively to challenge [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]], having heard of his remarkable skills and seeking a good fight. Whenever Shadow encounters any of them, the fight [[Difficulties|difficulty]] is displayed (except for [[Lilith]] and [[Festivus]]) and the players can choose to either update their equipment prior to the fight or simply proceed. The Challengers initiate a special mode of fight that works exactly like a single stage of [[Tournament (SF2)|Tournament]], having a maximum of three rounds timed at 99 seconds each. The players have the freedom to use their own equipment and the fight does not feature any other altered rules or conditions. The fight takes place at the location of the respective Act's [[Duel]].

Upon their defeat, the Challengers give up their weapons to Shadow. Other rewards include fight bonus and a small amount of experience. If the player loses, they are given two options: simply leave or try again. To retry, the player must pay +3 [[Currencies (SF2)|gems]] per every rematch. The player can pay a maximum of 30 gems for subsequent rematches and after the 10th try, it will always require 30 gems. Once a Challenger fight is fully declined, they can never again be re-sought for the same later on.

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What is the role of Challengers in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Challengers are skilled fighters who confront Shadow, the main character, drawn by his exceptional abilities. They initiate a unique fight mode, similar to a Tournament stage. These fights occur randomly in their respective Acts, following the defeat of the first bodyguard. Players can opt to upgrade their equipment before the fight, with the difficulty level displayed upon encountering a Challenger.

**How does the fight difficulty with Challengers in Shadow Fight 2 get displayed?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Challengers' fight difficulty is initially set at Insane or Impossible. This rating is based on the player's power and can be lessened by upgrading gear. The difficulty level is shown when a Challenger is encountered, giving the player the chance to enhance their equipment before the fight.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the special mode of fight initiated by Challengers in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Challengers in Shadow Fight 2 are skilled fighters who initiate a special mode of fight similar to a Tournament stage. The fight difficulty, initially set at Insane or Impossible, can be reduced by equipping superior gear. Players have the option to update their equipment before the fight.

Provided by: Fandom

**Can players update their equipment before fighting with Challengers in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, players can indeed upgrade their equipment before battling Challengers. The equipment, encompassing weapons, armor, helm, ranged weaponry, and magic, can be procured from the shop via coins or gems, or through defeating enemies and participating in raids. Upgrades for the equipment can be purchased with coins.

Provided by: Fandom

**How many rounds are there in the special mode of fight initiated by Challengers?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In the special mode triggered by the Challengers, there are up to three rounds. Each round lasts 99 seconds. Players are permitted to use their own gear without any rule alterations. The fight occurs at the location of the Act's Duels. Rewards for winning include the Challenger's weapon, fight bonuses, and a bit of experience.

Provided by: Fandom

## Tips\[]

- Challengers usually appear when the players level up. Get massive amounts of money to cover the equipment of the next level by fighting a lot on Survival mode upon reaching a new Act to farm money, which means that as soon as the Challenger appears, one can go to the shop and buy all the necessary equipment. With up-to-date equipment, every single challenger fight can be fought on Easy or Normal difficulty.
- Try to fight in [[Eclipse]] mode to collect [[Shadow Orbs]] and enchant equipment; enchanted equipment can make fights in Insane and Impossible difficulty a lot easier.

## Trivia\[]

- In the earliest versions of the game, every challenger had no particular speaking role of their own and used these same particular quotes before challenging the player:
  - _I heard some rumors about your strength. I offer you to try it against me. Accept my challenge and I will give you my weapon if you are victorious!_
  - If the player refused to fight them, Nova's particular quote would be shown for every challenger as a result. Also, challengers had no concluding words after being defeated by the player.
- All challenger weapons are enchanted with simple enchantments.
- All challenger weapons have functions identical to other weapons except for Wanderer's Staff, which has a different super slash from the usual Staves. (The Wanderer's Staff is also like the weapon from the Warlock Set)
- In real life, Ronin was a samurai with no lord or master during feudal Japan (1185–1868).
- Nova's eyes are white, just like [[Bosses (SF2)|demon bosses]] and characters corrupted by [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], which shows that Sensei's claim might be right, and the person that hired Nova is working for Titan.
- Since Nova is fought in the [[Interlude]], that challenger fight is the only challenger fight to reward the player with Platinum coins.
- If the player reaches Act VII and then gets challenged by Festivus or Lilith, then those fights will be the only challenger fights to reward the player with Credits.
