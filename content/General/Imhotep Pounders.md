**Imhotep Pounders** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act II Secret Path|Act II: Secret Path]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 8, along with the [[Tonfas]]. The Imhotep Pounders have the appearance of a pair of hammers. They work like the [[Steel Batons]] with the [[Battle Hammers]] Super Slash.
