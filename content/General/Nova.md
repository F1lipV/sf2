**Nova** is the seventh [[Challengers|challenger]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. She challenges the players to fight when they reach level 40-42. Nova is a mysterious character, her eyes are fully white, just like the [[Bosses (SF2)|demons]]. Sensei thinks it is possible that the person that hired her is somehow related to [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]. If the player wins, she will give them her weapon - [[Spiny Knuckles]], along with 25,000 platinum coins. If the player loses, then they can either:

- Leave
- Rematch by giving +3 gems for each retry (with a maximum of 30 gems, always requiring 30 gems after the 11th try)

**Who is Nova in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Nova is the seventh challenger and a mercenary who confronts players at level 40-42. She is distinguished by her fully white eyes, akin to the game's demons. Nova wields Spiny Knuckles and Throwing Axes, and uses Fire Splash magic. Her gear includes a Snake Harness and an Assassin's Cowl. Her knuckles bear a Poisoning enchantment, which gradually depletes an enemy's health after a hit.

## Appearance\[]

She is draped in black and purple garments, with arm warmers on her arms. Her hair is styled as a braid and has a slim body.

## Equipment\[]

- Nova uses Spiny Knuckles as her weapon of choice.
- For armor, she wears Snake Harness.
- For helm, she wears Assassin's Cowl.
- For ranged weapons, she uses Throwing Axes.
- For magic, she uses Fire Splash.

## Enchantment\[]

- **Poisoning**![[assets/EnchantmentIntoxication.png|EnchantmentIntoxication]]

Nova's Knuckles are enchanted with Poisoning, giving a chance that the enemy will start to lose 3% health per second over 5 seconds after being hit.

## Trivia\[]

- She is the only challenger that does not have any speaking lines after being defeated. This was the case with every other challenger in the earliest versions of the game.
- She, [[Lilith]] and [[Rose]] are the only female challengers.
- Irrespective of which act she gets encountered in, the soundtrack 'Clouds Heaven' will play in the background.
