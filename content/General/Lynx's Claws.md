**Lynx's Claws** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|boss weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are obtained by beating Lynx again in [[Eclipse|Eclipse mode]], after the player has completed [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]]. Upon obtaining, the claws automatically adapt to the player's level. The claws have a longer range compared to the other claws in the game. They are enchanted with [[Enchantments|Time Bomb]]. The formula for the power of the Time Bomb enchantment is as follows:

## Trivia\[]

- Similar claws are used by [[Tenebris]], the last eternal, although both differ in enchantments.
- It appears to have Poisoning when Lynx is defeated twice and at the [[Gates of Shadows|Gates Of Shadows]] and [[Interlude]] but when obtained by the player, it only has Time Bomb.
  - This makes it the first boss weapon to be enchanted with a simple recipe.
