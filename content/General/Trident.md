**Trident** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked in [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI: Iron Reign]]. Trident functions like the [[Yari]], except for the super slash. It is used by the [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] [[Whaler]].

## Trivia\[]

- Trident is the only weapon with an offensive move that does not directly use the weapon to attack (i.e. the second strike in its Super Slash, which is a punch).
- [[Whaler]] is the only character who uses this weapon.
