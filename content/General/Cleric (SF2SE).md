Cleric is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. He is the second and final bodyguard of the young [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]], encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] during Act I. He, along with Lynx and [[Spark]], was tasked with bringing the [[Prince]] back home. It is later revealed that their real task was in fact to eliminate the Prince.

Before his fight with [[Sensei]], Cleric discloses that The Order is involved in doing work which would be considered "too dirty even for the gods". They clean up other people's mess and correct their mistakes. By letting himself get involved in the Prince's affairs, Sensei has become one such mistake and needs to be eliminated.

He uses the [[Knuckles]] as his weapon and Throwing Daggers as his [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]].
