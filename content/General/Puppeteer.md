**Puppeteer** is a character in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is the owner of the [[Ascension]], which he referred to as "theater".

## Ascension\[]

Puppeteer approached Shadow and said that Shadow has an "interesting face" and invited him to become one of his stage actors.

The story that the Puppeteer is currently re-enacting tells of a Monk who once lived in a forest and protected wayfarers from ill-meaning spirits and criminals. The monk was highly valued by the grateful citizens. However, an envious king ordered the monk's execution. The loss of the monk meant that the forest was alive once more, and evil could prosper once again. A hero, played by Shadow, is needed to wear the Monk's gear and resume his role.

## Trivia\[]

- When the Ascension mode was disabled, Puppeteer did not appear after the player beat 12th stage tournament, although he still appeared to remind the players about whenever a Mythical [[Enchantments|enchantment]] is on or off. He then returned when the Ascension mode did in Underworld raids.
