**Silver Spear** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at [[Experience Points (SF2)|level]] 32, along with the [[Katars]]. Silver Spear functions the same as the [[Yari]] from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]]. It is used by the [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] [[Capra]], the fighter [[Fighters|Arrow]], and a [[Ninja]] counterpart.
