**Maces** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked at level 22 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked along with the [[Two-handed Cudgel]]. Maces function like the [[Steel Batons]]. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Plague]] and his [[Ninja]] counterpart.
