**False Gods** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special event weapon]] exclusive to the Shadow of Eternity 2024 event in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It can be purchased in a special paid offer for \$2.99 USD. This weapon is used by Berstuuk, the mad scientist.
