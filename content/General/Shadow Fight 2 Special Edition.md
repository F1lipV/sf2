**Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition** is the special paid version of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It was released on 17 August 2017 on Android and on 22 August 2017 on iOS. This version allows players to get [[Gem|gems]] easily, with most of the game modes rewarding the player an amount of them upon victory. In addition, Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition features an exclusive storyline: [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]]. Also, in this version, players have unlimited [[Energy (SF2)|energy]], meaning they can fight without any limit.

## Storyline\[]

Storyline is same as in Shadow Fight 2, except for Old Wounds, which is unlocked in first 6 Acts after the player wins 3 tournament fights in that act. In Old Wounds, the player takes the role of the young [[Sensei]], the mentor of the hero of Shadow Fight 2: [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]]. It is a story from the Sensei's past, with the [[Prince]]. There are three stages of Old Wounds in each act, with the exception of act 6, which only has 2.

One day, Sensei bumps into the Prince, whose soldiers are now controlled by [[Shogun]], and seeks Sensei's help in safeguarding his life against Lynx. After Sensei's victory against Lynx, and after Hermit's defeat, they learn that Hermit is training Shogun's soldiers for protection of his school from bandits. Hermit tells them where the kidnappers of the Prince (Butcher and his gang) are. Sensei learns that Prince is not very innocent after all and sacrificed other people's lives to save his from [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]]. After Sensei learns the truth, Prince escapes. After Butcher's defeat, he reveals that Prince is after the Sphere and Shogun is waiting for a ship at [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]'s harbour. Wasp reveals to Sensei that [[Widow]] is using Shogun and Sphere to get revenge from Prince for not responding to her love. After defeating Widow, Sensei learns that Prince has already got the Sphere despite Shogun's attempt to stop him, in vain. Sensei fights with Shogun, now under the control of the Prince, and after his victory against the Prince, [[Ancient]] uses his time travelling powers in order to take the Sphere back.

## Differences from the standard version\[]

### Storyline\[]

- Sensei's Story (Old Wounds) is available as a new mode of play exclusive to this edition.

### Gameplay\[]

- No advertisements.
- Unlimited energy.
- Some modes of play reward the players with gems (along with coins).
- There are only 12 challenge stages.
- The price of some equipment is cheaper.
- No delivery time when purchasing or upgrading equipment.
- Gem equipment level can be upgraded with either coins or gems.
- Max equipment level is 2 instead of 4.
- The level and enchantment power of a gem item do not scale to the players' level when bought.
- [[Equipment Sets (SF2)|Halloween 2016]] and [[Equipment Sets (SF2)|Valentine 2017]] special equipment can be bought from the store.
- In-app purchases give more coins and gems for the same price.
- [[Magic]] and [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|Ranged Weapons]] are able to be blocked.
- No [[Eclipse]] mode. (_Despite this, the players can still fight the six demon bosses again after their respective Act is completed to earn their weapons. In addition, this can be done without fighting their bodyguards._)
- No [[Shadow Orbs]] alongside manual enchanting equipment.
- No Monk's Set and Tempest Rage.
- No Sentinel's Set and Typhoon of Spirits.
- No [[Underworld|multiplayer mode]].
- [[Kid]] appears without in-app purchase when player unlocks Level 7, Act II.
- Failing to defeat any of the [[Bosses (SF2)|bosses]] a second time before [[Interlude]] will render the player unable to acquire that boss's weapon.

### Visual\[]

- Supports full-screen mode.
- Most locations have been redrawn from the original concept art.
- Has shaders for smoother lighting, just like in [[Shadow Fight Shades|Shadow Fight: Shades]].

### Audio\[]

- Features few newly remixed tracks from the original soundtrack.
