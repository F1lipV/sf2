**Festive Fans** is a 2021 Chinese New Year [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It was obtainable by purchasing the Dragon's Chest for 600 Patterned coins or 249 [[Gem|gems]]. Patterned coins was the special currency for the 2021 Valentine's Day and the Chinese New Year update, which could be obtained by defeating Son of Heaven. Festive Fans is enchanted with [[Enchantments|Poisoning]].
