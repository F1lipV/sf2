**Magari Yari** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V]]
in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 28, along with the [[Silver Glaive]]. Magari Yari functions like the [[Yari]], except for its super slash, which consists of four quick attacks. It is wielded by the fighter [[Fighters|Squirrel]] and her [[Ninja]] counterpart.
