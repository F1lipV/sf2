**Dissector of Hopes** is a 2016 Halloween [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It was obtainable during Halloween 2016 update. It was obtained either as a drop by defeating Morgana, the Halloween 2016 special raid boss, or by buying the [[Equipment Sets#Halloween 2016 Set|Spider Chest]] for 149 [[Gem|gems]]. Dissector of Hopes is also available in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]], where it can be bought from the [[Shop (SF2)|shop]].

## Trivia\[]

- Unlike in the Special Edition, the Dissector of Hopes from the Halloween 2016 and 2018 comes with the Bleeding enchantment.
- When first released, this weapon had the exactly same design as [[Grim Scythe]]. As of the Christmas 2016 update, however, this weapon received a new design.
- In the Special Edition, this weapon replaces Grim Scythe.
