**Big Swords** are a pair of [[Gem|gem]] [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked at level 15 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are enchanted with the [[Enchantments|Bloodrage enchantment]] and are unlocked along with the [[Axes]]. Big Swords function the same as [[Swords]], with the exception of the Super Slash Move, despite having the name name 'Big'. They are used by [[Cleaver]], [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]'s second [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] and the fighter [[Fighters|Hazzard]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 1]].
