**Barbed Glaive** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Interlude]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 40, along with the [[Dragon Teeth]]. It functions the same as the [[Glaive]] from [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV: Pirate Throne]].
