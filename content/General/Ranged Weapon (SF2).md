**Ranged Weapon** is one of the five types of [[Equipment (SF2)|equipment]] available in [[Shadow Fight 2]], along with [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]], [[Armor (SF2)|armor]], [[Helm (SF2)|helm]], and [[Magic (SF2)|magic]]. It is unlocked after defeating [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]. Most of ranged weapons can be bought from the shop with either [[Currencies (SF2)#Primary Currencies|coins]] or [[Currencies (SF2)#Secondary Currency|gems]], but they can also be obtained from other sources such as limited-time events. Ranged weapons cannot be blocked, but can be dodged by ducking, rolling forward, or rolling backward. For detailed description on each ranged weapon class, see [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)Ranged Weapon Classes|Ranged Weapon Classes]] subpage.

## List of Ranged Weapons\[]

### Shop Ranged Weapons\[]

There are two types of ranged weapons available in the shop: standard ranged weapons and super ranged weapons. Standard ranged weapons can be bought with either coins or gems, and can be upgraded to a higher level using coins, which do not come with enchantments. Super ranged weapons can only be bought with gems. Unlike weapons, armors and helms, super ranged weapons can be upgraded to the next level using coins, as long as there is no new ranged weapon unlocked in the shop. All super ranged weapons come with enchantments. When bought, the level and enchantment power of a super ranged weapon automatically scales to the player's current level.

## Trivia\[]

- All types of Shuriken and Kunai, as well as Circular Saw, deal 7 damage. Chakrams and Incinerator deal 9 damage. Needles type ranged weapons deal 3 damage each hit, they can deal up to 9 damage if all three projectiles hit. Landmines deal 23 damage.
- Enemies will not try to dodge ranged weapons at a certain distance.
- Some ranged weapons can be dodged by jumping as well, but this requires precise timing. It is recommended to not attempt this technique and to just use the techniques mentioned above.
- Though classified as a ranged weapon, Landmines are not used from range.
- Needles are the only available ranged weapon in the "Given ranged weapon only" challenge.
- Keen Chakrams are based on the real-life weapons called [Wind and Fire Wheels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_and_fire_wheels), which resemble [[Crescent Knives]] more in its usage rather than a ranged weapon.
- Bat Wings are the only shop ranged weapon that has stun (Only applies to normal edition as the special edition has Demon's Wing).
- Landmines and Dissecting Blades are the most expensive ranged weapons in the game, both costing 340 gems.
- Although Harpy's Bite has a dedicated model found within the game files, it appears to use the same model used by Zenith instead.
- The Tears of the Wind currently has several bugs:
  - Its sprite image represents a recolored version of Throwing Daggers, while it has the model of Hunter's Knives when thrown.
  - The ranged is thrown like a Chakram instead of Shurikens.
