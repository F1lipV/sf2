**Telescopic Staff** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the first weapon unlocked in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 2]] at level 47. Telescopic Staff is used by [[Cypher]], the fighter [[Fighters|Cygnus]] and their Looter counterparts.

## Trivia\[]

- Due to an oversight, the super slash's animation cannot be cancelled at the end, which forces the user to be vulnerable until the entire animation is played out. This causes the attack to have the longest endlag in the entire game, leaving the user wide open for punishes even after the attack has successfully landed.
  - This problem was slightly alleviated in an update where the attack received a cancel window at the end of its animation. However, it still takes a considerable amount of time to recover even with this new cancel window, putting the user at risk whenever this attack is used, albeit much less risky than it used to be.
