**Voodoo Spine** is a Halloween 2017 [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It was obtainable during Halloween 2017 update, as a possible reward by purchasing the [[Equipment Sets#Halloween 2017 Set|Mystical Chest]]. In order to purchase the chest, players needed 250 bone coins or 199 [[Gem|gems]]. Bone coins could be obtained by defeating Whisper, the Halloween 2017 special raid boss. Voodoo Spine is a stick with a bird's skull attached on it. The weapon came enchanted with the [[Enchantments#Simple Recipe|Magic Recharge]] enchantment. It functions similar to [[Staff]].
