**Hero's Will** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] obtained as a 10th anniversary gift by Nekki in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Players who logged in from September 20th, 2023 to an unspecified date received this weapon along with 200 [[Currencies (SF2)|gems]]. This weapon has the same moveset as the other Daisho weapons.

## Trivia\[]

- Hero's Will is the first special event weapon in game to have two non-mythical enchantments.
- It is the only weapon in Shadow Fight 2 that is enchanted with 2 Medium enchantments.
- Much like [[Daisho]] which also has two non-mythical enchantments, Hero's Will shares the same system of re-enchanting. In other words, when Hero's Will is re-enchanted, it will replace the current enchantment on the left and then swap the position of both enchantments. This way, the weapon will always have two enchantments and the enchantment that is going to be replaced is different for every enchantment attempts.
