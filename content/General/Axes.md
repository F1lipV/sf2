**Axes** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 15, along with [[Big Swords]]. Axes swing slowly, have moderate range and high damage. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Exile]] and his [[Ninja]] counterpart.
