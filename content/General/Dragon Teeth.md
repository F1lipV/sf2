**Dragon Teeth** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from the [[Interlude]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 40 along with the [[Barbed Glaive]]. They work like the [[Machetes]] from [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]]. They were used by [[Puppeteer]] as a raid boss in the Halloween 2021 event.
