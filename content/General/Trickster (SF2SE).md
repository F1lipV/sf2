**Trickster** is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. He is the first bodyguard of [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]], encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] during Act IV.

Before his fight with Sensei, he asks Sensei if he believes in fortune. He threatens Sensei and says he will make his fortune by taking all of Sensei's money and that Sensei will not have any luck escaping him.

He is dressed as a typical pirate in leather breeches and jacket, with a white undershirt and a sword on one hip. A red bandana is tied over his hair. He wields the [[Oriental Sabers]] as his weapon, and uses Throwing Daggers as his [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]].
