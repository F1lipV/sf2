**Heavy Staff** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] unlocked in [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]] at level 14 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It functions like the [[Staff]] from [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]], and has rope-shaped clothes attached to it. It is used by the fighter [[Fighters|Scourge]] and his [[Ninja]] counterpart.

## Trivia\[]

- Staff, along with Fans, are the only weapon types that lose contact with the wielder's hands during their super slash. Every other weapon type is always in the hands of its wielder.
- In Chinese, staff is referred to as **gun** (棍, literally rod, stick). Staff is known as one of the four major weapons in Chinese martial arts, along with the qiang (spear), dao (sabre), and the jian (straight sword). It is called, in this group, "The Grandfather of all Weapons".
