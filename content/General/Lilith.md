Lilith is the Halloween-special challenger in Shadow Fight 2. She wears a witch's suit and a witch's hat. Defeating her will reward the player with her weapon, [[Devil's Broom]]. If the player loses, there will be two options:

- Leave
- Retry by giving 1 gem

## Trivia\[]

- The fight with Lilith is the second fight that takes place in dojo, the first being the fight with [[Festivus]].

- She can be fought at any level, from 4 to 52.

- She, along with Morgana and her witch sisters, are the only characters in the entire game who have their avatars showing their full body.

- Full moon can be seen in her avatar's background.

- She is the second special-event challenger, the first being [[Festivus]].

- She is the second challenger to have an unknown difficulty level to her fight; the first being [[Festivus]].
