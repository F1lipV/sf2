**Coral Prickles** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 24, along with [[Kusarigama]]. They work like the [[Knuckles]] from [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]]. They are used by the Eternal [[Megalith]].
