### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**Who are the main characters in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, the protagonist is Shadow, a legendary warrior seeking a worthy opponent. Other primary characters are Sensei, May, and Sly. Supporting characters, appearing mainly in Act VII: Revelation, include Kali, Cypher, Ancient, Simon, and Lazarus. The narrative also features Shadow Demons, who escaped from the Gates of Shadows due to Shadow's actions.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the significance of Act VII: Revelation in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Act VII: Revelation, the final Act of Shadow Fight 2, unfolds in the Shadow World. Released in three separate chapters, it features unique locations for Tournament, Survival, Challenge, and Duel. The narrative revolves around Shadow's quest to rescue May and re-open the Gates, culminating in a showdown with Titan. Titan's plan is to harness Shadow's energy to obliterate any world obstructing his path.

Provided by: Fandom

**What role do the bodyguards play in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Bodyguards in Shadow Fight 2 serve as protectors for the demon bosses. Players must overcome these five bodyguards prior to engaging in battle with the demon. Each bodyguard, barring Shin, has unique speaking roles. The game offers customization options for the player's fighter, including a range of weapons, armor, and magical abilities to aid in defeating the bodyguards.

Provided by: Fandom

**Who are the Shadow Demons in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, the Shadow Demons, or Shadowland bosses, were once humans who escaped the Gates of Shadows. This occurred when the game's protagonist, Shadow, violated the 'Laws of The Elders' by opening these gates in pursuit of a formidable adversary. These demons, now the game's bosses, possess distinctive and potent abilities, combat benefits, and enchantments. To close the gates, Shadow must defeat these demons and secure their seals.

Provided by: Fandom
