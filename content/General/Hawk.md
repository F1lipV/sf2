**Hawk** is the [[Challengers|challenger]] in [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]], encountered when the player reaches level 8-11. He reveals that he has been traveling the land in search of a worthy opponent, much like [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]]. He believes that skill is more important than brute force and will reward the player with his [[Naginata]] along with 250 coins upon defeat. However, if the players lose, then they can either:

- Leave
- Retry by giving +3 gems for each retry (with maximum of 30 gems which means after 10th try, it always costs 30 gems)

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What level must the player reach to encounter Hawk in Act II?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Act II: Secret Path of Shadow Fight, the player encounters Hawk, a challenger seeking a worthy adversary, upon reaching level 8-11. Defeating Hawk, who values skill above brute force, earns the player his weapon, the Naginata.

Provided by: Fandom

**What reward does Hawk give the player upon defeat?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Defeating Hawk in Shadow Fight rewards the player with his weapon, the Naginata. Performance metrics like the number of head hits, shadow abilities, critical hits, first strikes, and maximum combo also influence the coin reward. Additionally, players can acquire Hawk's Feather, an Epic Special Move for Dadao.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the maximum number of gems a player can give for a retry against Hawk?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Upon losing to Hawk in the game, a player can opt for a rematch. Each retry requires the player to pay +3 gems. The maximum limit for a retry against Hawk is set at 30 gems, implying that from the 10th attempt onwards, the retry cost remains fixed at 30 gems.

Provided by: Fandom

## Personality\[]

Hawk looks for inner skill rather than strength in people. He is cool headed and respectful.

## Appearance\[]

He sports all-brown ninja-esque clothing and looks muscular in appearance.

## Equipment\[]

- For weapon, Hawk uses Naginata.
- For armor, he wears Snake Harness.
- For helm, he wears Snake Mask.

## Enchantment\[]

- **Bloodrage**![[assets/EnchantmentBloodrage.png|EnchantmentBloodrage]]

Hawk's Naginata is enchanted with Bloodrage, giving a chance to deal 200% more damage on his hit, but would also lose 10% of damage dealt in health. This enchantment won't work if it will cause Hawk to lose.
