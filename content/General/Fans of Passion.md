**Fans of Passion** are a 2017 Valentine's Day [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapon|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They were obtainable during the Valentine's Day 2017, 2020, 2021 and 2023 updates, as a first-place reward by defeating raid bosses ([[Vortex]], [[Fatum]], [[Arkhos]], or [[Hoaxen]]), or by purchasing the Casket of Fates ![[assets/Chest_val17.png|Chest val17]] and Ruby Treasure Chest ![[assets/Val20Chest.png|Val20Chest]]. Fans of Passion are enchanted with [[Enchantments|Poisoning]]. In the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]], they can be bought from the shop.
