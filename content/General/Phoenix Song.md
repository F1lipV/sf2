**Phoenix Song** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Set Weapons|special set weapon]] in Shadow Fight 2. It is the weapon of the Abyss Mark Set, which comes with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments#Mythical Recipes|Roaring Luminary]].

This weapon could be acquired by chance from opening the Ritual Chest during the Ritual Battles Event. Opening a chest costed 600 event coins.
