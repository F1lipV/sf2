**Ermine** is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. He is the second and final bodyguard of [[Widow]], encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] during Act V. He has also appeared in an event raid boss in Trial of the God event in 2025.

His avatar appears with a coat, and a bandana tied around his eyes indicating that he is blind. He also appears holding onto a cane. In addition to that, his hairstyle is just like Shadow's.

He wields a [[Dadao]] as his weapon of choice and uses the Throwing Daggers as his ranged weapon.
