Harbinger is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. He is the second and final bodyguard of [[Hermit]], encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] during [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]]. He claims that Hermit has told them all about the shadow world and promised them its power. Therefore he fights to defend Hermit so that one day the latter would share his knowledge with them.

Physically, he appears as a warrior with long hair, dressed in a traditional samurai's kimono with a dragon on the back. He wears a conical hat which covers most of his face.

He wields the [[Ninja Sword (SF2)|Ninja Sword]] as his weapon and uses the Throwing Daggers as his [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]].
