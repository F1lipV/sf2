**Lord of the Flame's Swords** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This weapon is available exclusively from an offer via in-game purchase. Lord of the Flame's Swords is enchanted with [[Enchantments|Time Bomb]].
