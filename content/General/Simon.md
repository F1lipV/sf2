**Simon** is a character in [[Shadow Fight 2]], who is an inhabitant of the Immortals' World and a pivotal non-playable character in the fourth tier of the [[Underworld]]. Serving as an uneasy ally and guide to [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]], [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]], and [[May (SF2)|May]], Simon possesses extensive knowledge of his dimension's history, the oppressive Obelisk, and the ruling Patriarch. Throughout the Tier 4 storyline, he maneuvers the group's journey to aid a rebellion, directing them to confront formidable entities to uncover the truth behind their interconnected worlds.

## Story\[]

After falling through [[Tenebris]]'s portal into the advanced Immortals' World, [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] defeats a robotic sentry named the [[Gatekeeper]]. Its creator, Simon, explains the bot was guarding the portal. Noting that Shadow's arrival has inadvertently strengthened the oppressive Obelisk, Simon warns that the world is ending and urges the group to follow him. Seeking a fugitive named [[Lazarus]], Simon leads them to a high-tech prison. Shadow defeats the warden, [[Saturn]], only to learn Lazarus has already escaped into the marshes. Simon explains that their world's peril began when the enigmatic Patriarch built the Obelisk. Tracking Lazarus into the marshes, Shadow defeats an entity known as [[The Blackness]], which restores Lazarus's fragmented spirit. Revealing his true, violent nature, Lazarus abandons the group to destroy the city. Realizing his mistake, a panicked Simon urges the group to stop the impending massacre. Expecting carnage at the Obelisk's core, the group instead finds an eerie silence. After Shadow strikes down a guardian known as the [[Stalker]], Simon makes a shocking discovery: the Patriarch himself is trapped inside the central crystal. Realizing the conspiracy is far deeper than a simple rebellion, Simon's voice glitches and he fades from reality as a new entity emerges.

## Trivia\[]

- Simon is unique among the Underworld allies, as he is the only character confirmed to have directly created and programmed one of the bosses the player must fight (the Gatekeeper).
- During his initial encounter with the group, Simon remarks that Shadow's "face" is somehow familiar to him. This is a notable piece of lore, as it confirms that in-universe, Shadow possesses visible facial features despite being depicted as a featureless silhouette to the player. How Simon recognizes him across dimensions remains an unsolved mystery.
- Narratively, Simon serves as the anchor for a massive thematic shift in the Underworld. While previous tiers focused on ancient magic, demons, and primordial entities like Tenebris, Simon introduces a dystopian, sci-fi element to the lore, discussing calculations, programming, and cyborgs.
- Unlike Sensei, who acts as a wise and usually correct mentor, Simon is one of the few guide characters whose primary theories are proven disastrously wrong. He severely miscalculates Lazarus's intentions, inadvertently helping to unleash a ruthless killer rather than a heroic rebel.
