**Ninja Sword** is the first [[Gem|gem-priced]] [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] available in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 4 and has the Precision [[Enchantments|enchantment]]. It is used by the bodyguard [[Ghost]] and one of the ninja fought throughout Act [[Act I Hero Reborn|I]] and [[Act II Secret Path|II]]. It is also the weapon used by a young [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] and [[Harbinger]] during [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]].

## Trivia\[]

- Ninja sword is translated from [ninjatō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ninjat%C5%8D "wikipedia:Ninjatō") (Japanese: 忍者刀), ninjaken (Japanese: 忍者剣), or shinobigatana (Japanese: 忍刀). Ninjatō are allegedly one of the weapons that ninja of feudal Japan carried. It is also a multi-purpose tool that can be used for climbing, and its saya (sheath) is able to be used as a blowgun or a monocular without magnifying lenses and for eavesdropping.
  - Historically, there is no physical evidence for the existence of this "katana-like short sword legendarily used by ninja" before the 20th century, though it is believed that the designs demonstrated by alleged replicas are based on the design of [wakizashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakizashi "wikipedia:Wakizashi") or [chokutō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chokut%C5%8D "wikipedia:Chokutō") or the swords associated with ashigaru, the infantry employed by the samurai class of feudal Japan. Most people misunderstand that ninja frequently used katana or katana-length ninjatō as their primary weapons because of popular cultures. They, however, actually used many peasant-made or small weapons for stealth missions such as [kama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kama_\(tool\) "wikipedia:Kama (tool)"), [shuriken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuriken "wikipedia:Shuriken") or [kunai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunai "wikipedia:Kunai").
    - Considering its appearance, the Ninja Sword looks more like a longer chokutō than a shorter ninjatō.
