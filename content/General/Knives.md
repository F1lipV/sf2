**Knives** are the first pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] available to the player for purchase in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Players will be prompted to buy the Knives after defeating [[Kenji]], as a part of the tutorial. They are used by the fighters [[Fighters|Jester]] and [[Fighters|Vulture]], as well as the bodyguard [[Bird]].

## Tips\[]

- The Knives are the first weapon the player obtains and gets to use as part of the tutorial and features a fighting style largely similar to the unarmed style. Despite being a beginner's weapon, the Knives are surprisingly capable of keeping up with many of its opponents using its disjointed range and quick slashes. Its range may be expectedly lackluster, but it makes up for it with high head hit and combo potential, as well as the two main uses mentioned above. Overall, the Knives make for an excellent weapon for most of the early game. Although the Knives eventually get overshadowed as more powerful weapons show up later, it still remains a solid weapon against many opponents.

## Trivia\[]

- The Knives are based on the [tantō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tant%C5%8D "wikipedia:Tantō"), a Japanese short sword/dagger/knife with curved, single-edged blade, and with or without hand guard that were used by samurai as their secondary weapon and by ninja as one of their primary weapons in feudal Japan.
- They are the only weapons to have an identical weapon in the same Act (Act I) - [[Daggers]].
- They are the only weapons to be bought with only [[Currencies (SF2)#Gold|gold]]. All other shop weapons can be bought with [[Gem|gems]].
- All weapons of the Knives class have a bug where the value from the unarmed stat (the fists stat that comes from upgrading armors) is not calculated to their single slash. This causes the weapons to deal basically zero damage if the weapon damage stat is lower than the unarmed stat.
