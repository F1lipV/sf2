**Claws of Winged Darkness** is a Halloween 2019 [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It was obtainable during Halloween 2019 update, as a possible reward by purchasing the [[Equipment Sets (SF2)#Halloween 2019 Set|Chest of Nightmare]]. In order to purchase the chest, players needed 350 Raven tokens or 149 [[Gem|gems]]. Raven coins could be obtained by defeating the witch sisters, the Halloween 2019 special raid bosses. The scythe came enchanted with the [[Enchantments#Simple Recipe|Magic Recharge]] enchantment.
