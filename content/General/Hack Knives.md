**Hack Knives** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]] of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are the first pair of weapons available in Act VII, unlocked at level 44. Hack Knives are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Raptor]] and his [[Ninja|Looter]] counterpart.
