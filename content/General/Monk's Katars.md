**Monk's Katars** are a pair of special [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Monk's Katars are one of the possible rewards in the [[Underworld#Rewards|Raid chest]] at the end of each season. It is necessary to get this weapon, along with other equipment from the [[Equipment Sets|Monk's Set]], to unlock the mythical enchantment Tempest Rage. Monk's Katars work like the knuckles, with a different super slash from other knuckle-type weapons. Each hit from the super slash is strong, but it is difficult to hit the enemy with all the hits. This weapon is enchanted with Tempest Rage.

## Trivia\[]

- Similar to [[Katars]], low punch has different knockback animations for the opponent.
- In the previous versions of the game, Monk's Katars as well as regular Katars suffered a bug where you could only activate Simple and Medium enchantments by landing hits with super slash. This was later fixed, allowing the weapons to activate enchantments from any weapon attacks.
  - This was due to a coding error where every attack, except super slash, was registered as unarmed attacks. It also meant that these attacks were able to activate the Martial Spirit perk just like barehanded attacks.
