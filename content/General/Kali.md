**Kali** is a main character in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]]. She is the replacement of [[Sly (SF2)|Sly]]'s role for Act VII as assistant, where she helps [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] about weapons, money, and other things. Kali first appears when Shadow is ambushed by [[Shroud]]. Since Shadow is too weak for Shroud at that time, Kali comes into the fight and immobilizes Shroud, allowing Shadow to escape. From that point on, she accompanies Shadow in the Shadow World to help him to defeat [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]].

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**How does Kali assist Shadow in the Shadow World?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Kali aids Shadow in the Shadow World by providing him with weapons and financial support. When Shadow is attacked by Shroud, Kali steps in, paralyzing Shroud and enabling Shadow's escape. She continues to support Shadow in his quest to overcome Titan. Kali also guides Shadow to a covert hideout, introducing him to their leader, Cypher, and elucidating the menace Titan represents.

Provided by: Fandom

**What role does Kali play in Act VII?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Kali serves as Shadow's assistant in Act VII: Revelation, succeeding Sly. She aids Shadow with weapons, finances, and other essentials. Kali debuts when Shadow is seized at the Gates of Shadows' closure. She is a rebel against Titan, hiding in a sanctuary with Cypher, a cyborg with a role akin to Sensei's. Kali intervenes in a fight between Shadow and Shroud, using an energy ball to briefly incapacitate Shroud.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is Kali's first appearance in the Shadow fight series?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In the Shadow Fight series, Kali debuts during an ambush on Shadow by Shroud. Shadow, being too weak, is saved by Kali who uses her Energy Ball to stun Shroud, facilitating their escape. She then continues to assist Shadow in the Shadow World, particularly in his mission to overcome Titan. Although she appears in the initial factory fight with Shroud, Kali does not participate in the combat.

Provided by: Fandom

**How does Kali help Shadow defeat Shroud in their first encounter?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

During the initial encounter with Shroud in Shadow Fight 2, Kali aids Shadow by intervening in the fight and temporarily immobilizing Shroud, allowing them to escape. Kali, not wielding a weapon, uses an Energy Ball in this confrontation. Post-battle, Kali escorts Shadow to a clandestine hideout and remains his ally in the Shadow World, assisting him in his quest to vanquish Titan.

Provided by: Fandom

**What kind of weapons does Kali use in her fights?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Kali's primary weapon is a pair of triangular-bladed knives. She also employs Throwing Daggers for ranged attacks. Additionally, she uses an Energy Ball to immobilize opponents, as seen in her encounter with Shroud in Shadow Fight 2.

Provided by: Fandom

## Battle\[]

Kali appears in the first fight with Shroud at the factory, but she is not fought in battle. She does not use a weapon, but is equipped with [[Energy Ball]] which she uses to immobilize Shroud.

Once Shadow receives damage from Shroud (which decreases his healthbar to a very low degree) Kali immediately appears and throws the Energy Ball which stuns Shroud, giving Shadow and Kali a chance to escape. Shadow then wins the fight.

## Trivia\[]

- Kali is the one of the rare characters that are shown with pupils in her eyes. Most of characters' eyes are either hidden or completely white.
- Kali's name is a reference to Kali, also known as Escrima. Kali is also the name of a martial art focused on combat with batons, swords and daggers. Considering the nature of Shadow Fight 2 as a fighting game, her name is appropriate.
- Kali wears a Redshift and Assassin's Cowl as her armor and helm respectively, as shown when she intercepts Shadow's battle with Shroud.
