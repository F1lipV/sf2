**Ascension** is recurring event [[Underworld|raid]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It returns every Friday two weeks after the last Ascension if there are no ongoing seasonal events. This event is the rework to the original Ascension game mode.

## Story\[]

![[assets/Statue.jpg|Statue]]

The player was approached by a mysterious figure, known only by the alias [[Puppeteer]]. He declared Shadow an "interesting face" and invited him to become one of his stage actors. The story that the Puppeteer re-enacted is one that tells of a monk who once lived in a forest, protected wayfarers from ill-meaning spirits and criminals. The monk was highly valued by the grateful citizens. However, an envious king ordered the monk's execution.

The loss of the monk meant that the forest was alive once more, and evil could prosper again. A hero, played by Shadow, was needed, to wear the [[Equipment Sets (SF2)#Set of Monk|Monk's equipment]] and assume his role.

## Gameplay\[]

Ascension is split into 5 increasingly difficult raid battles. Each raid lasts for 3:30 minutes, with a downtime of 45 seconds max (1 second if you survived the previous round) instead of regular raids' 1m30s. Fight rules are similar but more difficult but they only start applying to the 4th and 5th raids. Also, a special universal rule is set to all battles so that Medium and Large Charges of Darkness's damage is reduced by 75%. Enemies also have [[Perk Tree|perks]] and begin using magic.

Ascension battles require tickets to be fought.

The mode stays in the Underworld for 3 days as a solo player raid, and players must obtain Bronze tickets from daily Underworld missions to enter the first tier's raid. Successfully completing a raid rewards players with a ticket for the next tier (except tier 5) alongside different rewards. The order of ticket tiers is as follows: Bronze (Brown) - Silver (Silver) - Gold (Gold) - Platinum (Violet) - Ultra (Red).

## Ascension Opponents\[]

![[assets/Ascension2_ninja_man_crescent_knives.png]]

![[assets/Ascension2_ninja_man_tonfa.png]]

![[assets/Ascension2_ninja_man_keris.png]]

![[assets/Ascension2_ninja_man_nunchaku.png]]

The opponents are divided into 5 raids, from easiest to hardest:

- Raid 1: Crescent Knives Ninja, has 350 Shield Points and he uses [[Fire Ball]]
- Raid 2: Tonfas Ninja, has 500 Shield Points and he uses [[Water Ball]]
- Raid 3: Krises Ninja, has 800 Shield Points, his weapon is enchanted with Frenzy and he uses a dark version of [[Force Wave]]
- Raid 4: Swords Ninja, has 1400 Shield Points. She has Avenger, Pain Rage and Steel Foot perks. Her armor and helmet are enchanted with Damage Absorption and she uses [[Fire Pillar]]
- Raid 5: Steel Nunchacku Ninja, has 2600 Shield Points. He has Pain Rage perk. His armor and weapon are enchanted with Frenzy, while his ranged weapon and magic: Ice Pillar are enchanted with Overheat.

From Swords Ninja onwards, raids begin to have random rules:

- No Double Strikes
- No Block
- Hidden Healthbar
- Shrinked Location with Thorny Walls
- Increased vulnerability within shown area

## Notes\[]

The difficulty of tier 1-3 of Ascension is designed to be suitable for average players without powerful enchantments combinations or mythical enchantments. Tier 4 acts as a challenge for skillful players with strong weapons and normal enchantments while tier 5 is meant to be challenging even for highly skilled players who may be using mythical enchantments.

If the player ends an Ascension raid then quits the game for any reason, a critical bug will occur that causes the game reset its progress to tier 1 if the player won or not reset if the player lost. This can be extremely detrimental to the player as it is possible to lose your progress and have a redundant tier 2/3/4/5 ticket or they can be permanently soft-locked from doing Ascension battles until the next Ascension event comes.
