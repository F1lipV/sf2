**Knuckles** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]], unlocked at Level 2. The Knuckles have an extremely limited range and its basic moves are the same as [[Characters (SF2)|characters]]' bare fists, but they are very quick and powerful. Their Super Slash, when used at the right time, is extremely lethal and is capable of dealing high damage to the enemies. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Madman]], the fighter [[Fighters|Iron]], their [[Ninja|ninja]] counterpart and by [[Cleric]], a character from the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]].

## Trivia\[]

- Most knuckle weapons do not feature double punches except for the [[Pneumo Fists]].
- Knuckles are also known as brass knuckles, knucks, brass knucks, knucklebusters, knuckledusters, knuckle daggers, English punches, iron fists, paperweights, and classics.
- This weapon has the exact same moveset as if the user is unarmed except the super slash.
