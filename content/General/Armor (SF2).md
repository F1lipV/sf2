**Armor** is one of the five types of [[Equipment (SF2)|equipment]] that are available in [[Shadow Fight 2]], along with [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]], [[Helm (SF2)|helm]], [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]] and [[Magic (SF2)|magic]]. Armor reduces the damage taken to the body, and also increases the damage of unarmed attacks (punches, kicks, and throws).

## List of Armors\[]

### Shop Armors\[]

There are two types of armors available in the shop: standard armors and super armors. Standard armors can be bought and upgraded with either [[Currencies (SF2)|coins]] or gems. They do not come with [[Enchantments|enchantments]]. Super armors can only be bought and upgraded only with gems, and they come with enchantments. When bought, the level and enchantment power of a super armor will automatically scale to the player's current level.

## Trivia\[]

![[assets/Armor_shaman_old.png|Old Shamanic Harness sprite]] ![[assets/Armor_shaman_old.png|Old Shamanic Harness sprite]]

![[assets/Armor_shaman_old.png]]

Old Shamanic Harness sprite

- There are bugs involving Space Gown and Alien Cloak armors.
  - The Space Gown will not scale with the player's level when purchased (it will always be at level 47), despite being a super armor.
  - Meanwhile, the opposite occurs to Alien Cloak, which will scale to the player's level when bought, despite being a standard armor.
- Shamanic Harness had a bug where the dangling part of the armor could stuck and cover the player's screen. Due to this, Shamanic Harness was removed from the shop, and made O-yoroi the only armor available for level 34. This bug was then circumvented in the 2.13.0 update by removing the dangling parts and the armor was re-added back to the shop. Due to the armor's temporary removal, some enemies in Act VI can be found without armor.
- Poison Petal temporarily became the only armor that provided head protection instead of body protection upon its release. The mishap was later fixed in a subsequent minor update.
