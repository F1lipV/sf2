**Claws of Perversity** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Set Weapons|special set weapon]] in Shadow Fight 2. It is the weapon of the Karcer Set, which comes with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments#Mythical Recipes|Predator's Fury]].

This weapon could be acquired by chance from the Ritual Chest during the Ritual Battles Event. Opening a chest costed 600 event coins.

## Karcer Set\[]

Read More [[Equipment Sets (SF2)]]

All the items of the Karcer Set bring **Predator's Fury** mythical enchantment. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock the enchantment. Once all the items of the set are collected, Predator's Fury will be available, and the player can enchant other equipment with this enchantment.
