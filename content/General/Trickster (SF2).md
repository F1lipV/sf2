**Trickster** is the first [[Challengers|challenger]] that appears in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He challenges the players to a fight once they reach level 5. Trickster appears as a talented fighter with silver hair and an arrogant figure. He says that he has heard of the player's skills, and offers a duel to test them. If the player wins, he will give them his weapon - a pair of [[Nunchacku]], along with 50 coins. If the players lose, then they can either:

- Leave
- Retry by giving +3 gems for each retry (with maximum of 30 gems which means after 11th try, it will always cost 30 gems)

## Personality\[]

Trickster appears to be quite arrogant and underestimating of his opponent, mocking Shadow by calling him a 'vulgar copycat' of himself, but does have respect for Shadow after his defeat. As stated by himself, he lacks self control.

## Appearance\[]

He is a muscular man with no clothes on his body and sports grey hair.

### Equipment\[]

- Trickster uses Nunchacku as his weapon.
- He wears Red Doublet as his armor.
- He wears Conical Hat as his helm.

## Enchantment\[]

- **Overheat**![[assets/EnchantmentOverheat.png|EnchantmentOverheat]]

Trickster's Nunchacku is enchanted with Overheat, giving a chance in each strike to put a buff on the user that makes his next strike inflict 200% more damage.
