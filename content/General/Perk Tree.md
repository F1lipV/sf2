**Perk Tree** allows users to get perks. There are two types of perks: Special Abilities and Offense Moves. The player unlocks (a) new perk(s) every time they level up. Offense moves are the only option when they are unlocked, while two perk options are available when the tier does not contain an offense move. They are either new perks to learn, or upgrades for previous chosen perks (if any).

## List of Move Skills\[]

There are a total of six different offense moves that can be learned by the player. Offense moves are perks which do not have alternate options. These moves are not present in the beginning of the game and thus cannot be performed by the player until they are learned. As the player progresses, increasingly damaging offensive moves will be made available for learning.

## Trivia\[]

- Enlightenment is the only perk that can work in [[Underworld]].
- Damage-boosting perks such as Desperate, Furious, Pain Rage, Mirror, and Disorientation are described as increasing damage for all types of attacks, but in reality, they only apply to melee attacks. Similarly, Cobra and Avenger also only work with melee attacks.
- Other than the player, only [[Shroud]], Titan's [[Bodyguards|bodyguards]], [[May (SF2)|May]], [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], and most of Raid bosses utilize perks.
- Although Eagle Eye describes its operation time as 3 seconds, it actually operates for 4 seconds.
- Although "Ricochet" is described as blocking all damage from ranged attacks, in reality, the damage from such an attack is reduced by a factor of 1,024.
- Enlightenment has a bug, whereas if it triggers as the player is hit with a Lifesteal-enchanted weapon before they are defeated, the perk would instead cause the enemy to lose health equal to the amount of health the player restored instead of healing up their health. This is most apparent during the [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]] fight.
