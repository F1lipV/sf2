**Steel Batons** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked at level 4 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked along with [[Ninja Sword (SF2)|Ninja Sword]]. The Steel Batons have high damage, medium range and fast attack speed. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Rogue]], the bodyguards [[Brick]], [[Bull]], and [[Irbis]] and their [[Ninja]] counterpart.

## Trivia\[]

- In real life, there are many [stick-fighting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stick-fighting "wikipedia:Stick-fighting") martial arts that have batons. [Arnis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnis "wikipedia:Arnis") is one of the best-known examples where its practitioners use [bastons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baston_\(weapon\) "wikipedia:Baston (weapon)") as one of their primary weapons.
- All [[Fighters|fighters]] with the Steel Batons are depicted as wielding wooden batons instead of steel ones on their portraits.
- The Steel Batons, along with the [[Swords]] and [[Sai (SF2)|Sai]] are wielded by the most enemies in the game, being wielded by 5 enemies.
