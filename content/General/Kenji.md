Kenji is the first opponent the players encounter in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Once [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] returns to his native village after opening the [[Gates of Shadows|Gates]] and losing his physical body, his [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] asks him to demonstrate a few moves to see whether he still possessed all his fighting prowess. Shadow is made to attack a punching bag to test his moves. Apparently satisfied with what he sees, Sensei next commands Shadow to fight an opponent who would be able to defend and counter-attack. To this end, he summons his other student - Kenji - to fight against Shadow. Defeating Kenji rewards the players with 70 [[Currencies (SF1)|coins]].

## Appearance and Characteristics\[]

Kenji has medium-length, flowing maroon hair and a well-built muscular body. He fights barehanded and was apparently under the the tutelage of Sensei before the latter left to search for the [[Gates of Shadows#Seals|seals]] with Shadow. He does not have any speaking role in the game.

## Trivia\[]

- During the first fight, Kenji can never deal the critical hit.
- In the first fight with Kenji, when the player health is low, Kenji will stop attacking and will be only dodging player attacks (Like [[Shin]]).
- During the first three versions of game, when the players updated the app, a huge amount of [[Experience Points (SF2)|XP]] would be required to reach next level. To solve this issue, Kenji would appear and ask the players for a re-match in order to exact vengeance for humiliating him in front of Sensei earlier. Defeating him would provide the players with the required experience boost.
- In Shades, he is Kanade and [[Dragon|Dragon/Ryuji']]s cousin (for Dragon is unconfirmed) and also (possibly) [[Irbis]]' nephew.
