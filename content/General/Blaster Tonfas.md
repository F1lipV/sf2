**Blaster Tonfas** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked at level 46 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are the last weapons unlocked at the chapter 1 of [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Revelation]], along with [[Fretsaw]]. Blaster Tonfas' super slash fires two energy projectiles, which works similar to most ranged weapons but has higher damage, and can be blocked. The super slash is not categorized as any [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]], [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]] or [[Magic|magic]]; thus, it does not follow any [[Enchantments|enchantments]] on any piece of equipment used. Blaster Tonfas are used by [[Cronos]] and [[May (SF2)|May]].

## Trivia\[]

- The Blaster Tonfas' super slash only does one hit, despite firing 2 projectiles.
- Blaster Tonfas are one of the five weapons in the game that shoot projectiles as super slash. The other weapons are [[Cobra's Tooth]], [[Demon Slayer]], [[Plasma Rifle]] and [[Fury of Austerlitz]].
