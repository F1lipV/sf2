**Blood Reaper** is a super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It has a decorated sickle and a barbed circle-shaped weight, connected with a chain. It is unlocked at level 6, along with [[Daggers]]. The Blood Reaper is the second weapon that is purchased through [[Gem|gems]] in the [[Shop (SF2)|store]], with the first one being the [[Ninja Sword (SF2)|Ninja Sword]]. The weapon has long range, slow attack speed and, as the name suggests, comes with the [[Enchantments|Bleeding]] enchantment. Despite having a slow attack speed, the Blood Reaper can easily inflict headshots and combos.

## Trivia\[]

- It is the first weapon enchanted by the Medium recipes.
- The Blood Reaper is based on the [kusarigama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kusarigama "wikipedia:Kusarigama"), a traditional Japanese weapon that consists of a kama (the Japanese equivalent of a sickle or billhook) on a kusari-fundo – a type of metal chain (kusari) with a heavy iron weight (fundo) at the end.
