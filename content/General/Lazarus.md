**Lazarus** is a prominent non-playable character encountered in the Immortals' World during Tier 4 of the [[Underworld]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is the son of the dimension's enigmatic ruler, the Patriarch. Initially presented by [[Simon]] as a wrongfully imprisoned rebel leader who is key to dismantling his father's oppressive regime, Lazarus's true nature is ultimately revealed to be far more ruthless, manipulative, and bloodthirsty than his allies anticipate.

## Story\[]

Before [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] arrives, Lazarus escapes Warden [[Saturn]]'s high-tech prison and hides in the treacherous marshes, abandoning his rebel followers. Believing Lazarus can dismantle the Obelisk, [[Simon]] leads Shadow, [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]], and [[May (SF2)|May]] into the swamps to find him. Lazarus warns that their human presence attracts a dark entity known as [[The Blackness]]. When Shadow engages and defeats The Blackness, he inadvertently restores a missing piece of Lazarus's spirit. With his power back, Lazarus drops his noble facade, revealing his true, violent nature. He abandons the group and sets off to destroy the city and the Patriarch. The group rushes to the city to stop the carnage but finds an eerie silence instead. The Patriarch is already imprisoned within the Obelisk. Realizing a son would not orchestrate his own father's downfall, Simon deduces Lazarus was not responsible, pointing to a much larger conspiracy.

## Trivia\[]

- Lazarus is the only character in the Tier 4 storyline whose physical appearance and clothing resemble traditional medieval fantasy/rogue designs, contrasting heavily with the futuristic, sci-fi aesthetics of Saturn, Simon, and the Gatekeeper.
- Despite Simon hyping him up as an incredibly dangerous force capable of razing an entire city to the ground, Lazarus is never actually fought by the player, serving strictly as a narrative catalyst to move the plot toward the Concentration Hall.
- Simon's dialogue at the Concentration Hall reveals that Lazarus is the Patriarch's son. This explains his deep connection to the Obelisk's politics and why he was imprisoned by Saturn rather than simply executed.
