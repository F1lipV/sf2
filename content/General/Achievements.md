**Achievements** are special objectives players come across throughout [[Shadow Fight 2]]. There are two types: those that can be finished in either Normal or [[Eclipse]] mode, and those which are exclusive to the latter. Completion of an achievement rewards the players with 1 gem, or 3 gems in case of certain Eclipse Achievements.

## Achievements That Can Be Missed\[]

There are some achievements which can only be obtained before completing a certain part of the story. These achievements cannot be obtained anymore once the player progresses past a certain point.

- All the achievements for overcoming Survival in Acts I through VI can only be obtained before closing the [[Gates of Shadows]]. Overcoming Survival after that will not reward the players with the respective achievements.
- The [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]] achievement "**Presumed Innocent**" (Overcome Survival at the Factory) can only be obtained before defeating [[Cypher]]. Overcoming the Factory Survival during Act VII: Chapter 2 will not reward the players with the achievement.
- "**The Inside Job**" achievement (Defeat all competitors at the Hideout) can only be obtained before completing the Incubator Tournament in Act VII: Chapter 2. After that, the Hideout will be hijacked by Shroud and later converted into the location for the Spaceship Challenge in Chapter 3, making it impossible to access.
- "**The Flashback**" achievement can only be obtained before defeating Shroud in Act VII: Chapter 2. After that, the Stone Grove Challenge cannot be accessed anymore because it is replaced by the Stone Grove Tournament.
- "**Shadow Fight 2 Birthday Celebration**" achievement can only be obtained during the Shadow Fight 2 anniversary period (between 18th to 30th September 2023). Beyond that, the players cannot earn this achievement.

## Trivia\[]

- Images for "Not a Chance", "Unstoppable", and "Three in a Row" achievements are similar to the [[Duel]] icon which shows [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] fighting [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]].
- The images for "Sunstroke" and "Desert Storm" achievements actually show the location of the Challenge (Flying Rocks), not the location from the Survival itself (Graveyard Ships).
- Aside from their respective achievements, Act VII mini-bosses ([[Cypher]], [[Ancient]] and [[Shroud]]) can also give the player achievements obtained from defeating the demon bosses without a single loss.
- There are two unused achievements, where the user must use Monk set and Sentinel set to obtain them.
- In new versions of the game, it is no longer possible to obtain enchantment achievements.
