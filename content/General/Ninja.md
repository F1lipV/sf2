Ninja are unnamed enemies fought in [[Duel]] and [[Survival|Survivals]] from [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] to [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI]]. Their successors, called Looters, are fought in Duel and Survival in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]].

They are used to earn little amounts of money and XP which can help when grinding to buy equipments.

([[Weapon (SF2)|Weapons]] written in _italic_ are unobtainable.)

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What role do the Ninja characters play in the Shadow Fight 2 game?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

The Ninja in Shadow Fight 2 is a customizable character who battles against an invader. The game allows players to equip their Ninja with weapons, armor, and magic. As players progress, the Ninja gains new levels and skills. There's also a multiplayer mode where the Ninja can team up with others to challenge formidable bosses.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the difference between Ninjas and Looters in the game?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Ninja and Looters are distinct enemy types. Ninja, encountered from Act I to Act VI, are unnamed adversaries in Duels and Survivals. Looters, on the other hand, are opponents in Duel and Survival in Act VII. Both enemy types offer players opportunities to earn money and XP for equipment purchases.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the significance of Ascension Ninjas in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Ascension was a distinct mode found in Act II: Secret Path. It became accessible after players defeated twelve foes in the Act II Tournament. Puppeteer, a mysterious figure, introduced this mode. Ninjas in Ascension used weapons such as Tonfas, Steel Nunchacku, Krises, Crescent Knives, and Swords. Unlike other modes, Ascension didn't consume energy and was available until the v1.9.8 - 2.5.1 update. The Ascension's fight scenario was later incorporated into battles with Lynx and his bodyguards in Old Wounds.

Provided by: Fandom

**Why were Ninjas replaced by Looters in Act 7?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In the Shadow Fight 2 game, Ninjas serve as adversaries in Duels and Survivals from Act I to Act VI. However, in Act VII, Looters replace the Ninjas. This shift provides players with a fresh challenge and a novel method to accumulate money and XP, essential for equipment purchases.

Provided by: Fandom

**What type of equipment do Ascension Ninjas wield in the game?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Ascension Ninjas in the Shadow Fight 2 game use a wide array of weapons such as Butterfly Swords, Golden Katana, Two-handed Cudgel, and Wasp's Naginata. They also wear various armors like the Emerald Breastplate, Radiant Platemail, and Sentinel Coat. Helmets like the Dawn Tiara and Silver Kabuto are also part of their gear. Additionally, they utilize tools like Weighted Shurikens and Dark Blast. However, they do not use magic.

Provided by: Fandom

## Trivia\[]

- The first version of the game did not have any ninja. Instead, players used to duel with [[Fighters]] in Survival.
- There is only one ninja that appears in 3 acts, that being the male ninja equipped with [[Swords]].
- All ninjas are equipped with Snake Mask. The male ninjas are equipped with Leather Jacket, while the female ninjas are equipped with Snake Harness.
  - Unlike ninjas, each looter has their own unique [[Armor (SF2)|armor]] and [[Helm (SF2)|helm]].
- No ninja equipment is enchanted, except for the [[Ascension]] ninjas.
  - Ninja equipped [[Staff]] is the only ninja who isn't one of Ascension ninjas although he also the ninja of [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]], because 5 Ascension ninjas are the ninjas from Act II and Act II has 6 ninjas.
    - Maybe each of Ascension ninjas randomly represented for each item of the Monk's Set because each set only has 5 items and 5 items of the Monk's Set randomly represent one of them. And for some reason ninja with Staff was not chosen as a ninja Ascension.
- The [[Knives]], [[Daggers]] and the [[Trident]] are the only standard and coin-priced [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from the first six acts that are not used by ninjas.
- The [[Ninja Sword]] and [[Katars]] are the only gem-priced [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] that are used by ninjas. Mowers is the only gem-priced [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] that is used by the looter.
- [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] introduces 10 ninjas to the player. From the conclusion of that act onwards, 6 new ninjas get introduced with each successive act, with the exception of [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI]], which introduces 8 new ninjas.
- There are ninjas who wield dual weapons, but where only a singular one appears in their avatars. They are the ninja with [[Unavailable Known Items (SF2)|Kunais]], the ninja with [[Unavailable Known Items (SF2)|Triangular Knives]] and the ninja with [[Heavy Nunchacku]].
- None of the ninjas have a speaking role of their own.
  - In older versions, the ninja equipped with [[Unavailable Known Items (SF2)|Triangular Knives]] (that was called Watchdog) had a small speaking role.
- - In [[Sparring]] (Nintendo Switch version), the looter girl with Reaver is seen wielding a [[Dissector of Hopes]] instead, and she is named Agony.
  - [[Swords]], [[Sai]] and [[Telescopic Staff]] are the only [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] which are wielded by two ninjas and looters respectively. One being male and the other female.
  - It is revealed in [[Shadow Fight Shades|Shades]] that the Ninjas are members of the order of assassins.
