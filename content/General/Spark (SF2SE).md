Spark is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. She is a mercenary working for [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]] and is the first enemy encountered when [[Sensei]] recounts his story during [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] in Act I. She, along with Lynx and [[Cleric]], was tasked with bringing the [[Prince]] back home. It is later revealed that their real task was in fact to eliminate the Prince.

Before her fight with Sensei she tries to intimidate him by showing her tattoos, which apparently depicted the foes she had defeated and which were awarded to the strongest warriors in her homeland.

She uses a pair of [[Unavailable Known Items (SF2)|triangular-bladed knives]] as her weapon and Throwing Daggers as her [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]] .
