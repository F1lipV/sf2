**Broomstick** is a Halloween 2014 [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It was available in the shop during Halloween 2014 update, and could only be bought for [[Gem|gems]]. Broomstick works like [[Staff]].
