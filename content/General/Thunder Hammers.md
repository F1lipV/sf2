**Thunder Hammers** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 3]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 51, along with the [[Plasma Rifle]]. Thunder Hammers function like [[Imhotep Pounders]]. However, they have a unique super slash which can cause an electric explosion that does not inflict damage to the opponent. Thunder Hammers are wielded by a [[Fighters|Scavenger]] and by [[Guru]], [[Titan (SF2)|Titan's]] third bodyguard.

## Trivia\[]

- This is the only baton [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] that takes the stance of the [[Axes|axe]].
- Unlike [[Plasma Rifle]], [[Shocker Claws]], [[Blaster Tonfas]] and [[Cobra's Tooth]], the special effect, in this case, is that the electric explosion does not affect the enemy.
