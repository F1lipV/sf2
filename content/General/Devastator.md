**Devastator** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the first two-handed weapon which can be bought with gems. It is unlocked at level 16, along with [[Steel Claws]]. Like the other two-handed weapons, Devastator is heavy and slow, but it is compensated by its high damage. It is enchanted with the Lifesteal enchantment. [[Assassin]] wields this weapon.

- It is the first heavy weapon unlocked in the shop.
- Even though [[Assassin]] wields a Devastator, in his avatar, he wields a normal sword or a katana.
  - Assassin's Devastator has a different enchantment from the regular Devastator.
