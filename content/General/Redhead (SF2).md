**[[Redhead]]** is the fourth [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]], and a member of his gang. He wields [[Tonfas]] as weapons. He is a vain, aggressive and confident person. Before the fight with him, Redhead reveals that Butcher himself killed the parents of Bull, "that worthless fool". After being defeated, he says that the cult was once fun, but now it has become way too serious. He is well built and has red hair tied in a bun.
