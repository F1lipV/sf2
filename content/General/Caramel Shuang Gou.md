**Caramel Shuang Gou** are the 2015 Christmas [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Defeating the challenger [[Festivus]] during the Christmas 2015 update rewarded the player with this weapon. Unlike its [[Candy Canes|2014 counterpart]], they are enchanted with [[Enchantments#Lifesteal|Lifesteal]] and feature a different stance.
