**Capra** is the third [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]]. He is one of the many men who have been charmed by Widow. He sports grey hair as well as facial hair, wields the [[Silver Spear]] and wears navy blue clothes as well as silver wrist guards and a belt with a Capra's head symbol on it.

## Trivia\[]

- Capra is the name of a species of goat, which justifies the Capra's head symbol on his belt.
- Like other bodyguards of Widow, he is named after an animal that resides in cold climates.
- He is one of the oldest bodyguards in [[Shadow Fight 2]], the other being [[Bosun]] - a bodyguard of [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]].
- In older versions of Capra (for example, in 1.6.1) used weapon _[[Unavailable Known Items (SF2)|Spear]]_, but later it was replaced by a [[Silver Spear]]. An identical Spear model is used by the [[Hand of Prairie]].
