**Loa Twins** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special event weapon]] exclusive to the Shadow of Eternity 2024 Halloween event in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be acquired as a random reward by opening a Spirit Trap, which costed 600 ![[assets/CoinsHalloween24.png|CoinsHalloween24]] Coins or 99 gems to open. This weapon is used by Whisper, the fortune teller.
