**Flame Clubs** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 3]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are the last weapon unlocked in the main story, at level 52. Unique in their usage, their heavy slash and super slash causes flames to erupt from the tips of the clubs. They are used by a [[Fighters|Scavenger]] and by the ghost [[Fighters|Wraith]]. They appear as flame throwers with the gas containers connected close to the handle.
