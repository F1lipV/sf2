**Katars** are a pair of [[Gem|gem]] [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 32, along with the [[Silver Spear]]. Katars have the same moveset as the [[Knuckles]]. However, they have a slightly higher range than the Knuckles, while inflicting slightly weaker damage comparatively. Katars are wielded by the fighter [[Fighters|Thief]] and his [[Ninja]] counterpart.

## Trivia\[]

- Katar is a type of push-dagger from the Indian subcontinent. It shows the status symbol of the owner. The Indian nobility often wore katar for self-defense and as a symbol of their social status.
- Its super slash and low punch have different knockback animations for the opponent.
- Despite being visibly slower than the Knuckles' counterpart, Katars' super slash's animation can be cancelled at the end, giving it functionally the same speed as the Knuckles' version.
- In the previous versions of the game, Katars as well as regular Monk's Katars suffered a bug where you could only activate Simple and Medium enchantments by landing hits with super slash. This was later fixed, allowing the weapons to activate enchantments from any weapon attacks.
  - This was due to a coding error where every attack, except super slash, was registered as unarmed attacks. It also meant that these attacks were able to activate the Martial Spirit perk just like barehanded attacks.
