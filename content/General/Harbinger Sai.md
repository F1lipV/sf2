**Harbinger Sai** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are the last weapon in [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V: The Greatest Temptation]], unlocked at level 30, along with the [[Battle Hammers]].
