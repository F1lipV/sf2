**The Priestess** is a side character that appears in every Ritual Battles event. She is a shamaness of the Cult; a group of people residing in the [[Underworld]] who has devoted themselves to the responsibility of monitoring the status of the Eternals. She is The Lamb's confidante and significant other as well.
