**Orbs of Pain** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked at level 50 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are one of the first weapons available in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 3]], along with the [[Cobra's Tooth]]. The Orbs of Pain are a pair of flails, working like the [[Nunchacku]]. The Orbs of Pain are used by a [[Fighters|Scavenger]] and the soldier ghost [[Fighters|Smother]].

## Trivia\[]

- In the Russian version, they have a completely different name - Длани душегуба (Hands of the murderer).
- While there are [[Morning Stars]] in the game, the Orbs of Pain and [[Maces]] are the weapons which are more similar to the real-life morning stars and flails.
- It is practically impossible to fully hit the opponent with the Super Slash attack, as the first hit of the flail only staggers the opponent in place, causing the second hit to miss or be interrupted.
