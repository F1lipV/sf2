**Wanderer's Staff** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Challenger Weapons|challenger weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Defeating [[Fisher]] in [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV: Pirate Throne]] rewards the player with this weapon. The staff has a unique super-slash compared to other staff-type weapons. It comes enchanted with the [[Enchantments|Weakness]] enchantment.

## Trivia\[]

- The characters in the game refer to this staff as spear.
- There is a text glitch in the in-game move list of Wanderer's Staff. The Super Slash name is written as .
- Wanderer's Staff resembles a Khakkhara, a Buddhist ringed staff used primarily in prayer or as a weapon. It is also known as monk staff or Shakujō in Japan.
