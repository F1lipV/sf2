**Widow's Fans** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|boss weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are obtained by defeating [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]] once again in [[Eclipse]] mode. The fans function like [[Swords]] from [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]], except for their super slash, even though they look nothing like Swords. Some of their attacks are also slightly stronger than any other swords. These fans have variable stats and enchantment power depending upon the player's level. They are enchanted with the Lifesteal [[Enchantments|Enchantment]]. The formula for the power of the Lifesteal enchantment is as follows:

## Trivia\[]

- Fans, along with Staff, are the only weapon types that lose contact with the wielder's hands during their super slash. Every other weapon type is always in the hands of its wielder (unless the user is shocked).
- Widow's Fans are based on Tessen fan, a type of Japanese war fan designed for use in warfare.
