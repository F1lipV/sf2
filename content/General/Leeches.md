**Leeches** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Interlude]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are one of the two first weapons available in the Interlude, unlocked at level 38 along with the [[Morning Stars]]. As the name suggest, Leeches are enchanted with the [[Enchantments|Lifesteal]] enchantment.
