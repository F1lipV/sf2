**Helm** is one of the five types of [[Equipment (SF2)|equipment]] available in [[Shadow Fight 2]], along with [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]], [[Armor (SF2)|armor]], [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]] and [[Magic (SF2)|magic]]. Helms reduce the damage dealt from head hits.

## List of Helms\[]

There are two types of helms available in the shop: standard helms and super helms. Standard helms can be bought with either [[Currencies (SF2)|coins]] or gems, and can be upgraded to a higher level using coins. They do not initially have [[Enchantments|enchantments]]. Super helms can only be bought with gems, and upgrading them to a higher level requires gems as well. Super helms come with enchantments. When bought, the level and enchantment power of a super helm will automatically scale to the player's current level.

## Trivia\[]

- There are bugs involving several helms in the game. Shown below are the helms along with the bugs affecting them.
  - Two-faced Helm and Samurai Helm: A long withstanding bug where fighters who are supposed to be wearing these helms appearing without it instead. Examples include: various [[Fighters]] in Act V-VI, the challengers [[Outcast]] and [[Ronin]], and Widow's bodyguard [[Irbis]].
  - Frost Beard (2016 version): A bug that causes this helm's image sprite to not be displayed correctly in the game, making it appear only as a white square or rectangle in the list of helms.
  - Face of Christmas and Antlers of the Forest Spirit: A fairly recent bug which caused the helms affected by it to completely disappear from the list of helms. These helms are still present within the game's files and players' progress saves, however the bug prevents players from being able to equip the affected helms due to its effects.
  - But the second and third bugs have been fixed since version 2.40.0+.

- GlitchMask is the only helmet in which its name is written together, and not separately, like other helmets.
