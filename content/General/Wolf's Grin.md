**Wolf's Grin** is a special weapon. It is part of the Creator of the Winds set. This item was obtainable during an event in 2022 through a battle pass. Wolf's Grin was wielded by Wolf Wind.

## Creator of the Winds Set\[]

Read More [[Equipment Sets (SF2)]]

All the items of the Creator of the Winds Set are enchanted with the mythical enchantment **Squall**. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock **Squall**. Once unlocked, the player can enchant other equipment with **Squall**. To use **Squall** in fights, the players must equip either the full Creator of the Winds Set or any items enchanted with the enchantment.
