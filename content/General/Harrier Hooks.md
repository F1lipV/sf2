**Harrier Hooks** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act II Secret Path|Act II: Secret Path]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 10, along with [[Krises (SF2)|Krises]]. Harrier Hooks have high damage, slow swing speed and medium range. They are used by [[Hoaxen]] and Adanti.
