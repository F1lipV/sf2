Ranged Weapons in [[Shadow Fight 2]] can be broadly classified into the following categories:

Ranged Weapons written in _italic_ are unobtainable.

### Trivia\[]

- Shuriken were unlikely to be used as deadly weapons, unless they were coated in some poison. Instead they were used as distraction weapons or for creating ad-hoc tripping hazards.

## Unique Ranged Weapons\[]

- Circular Saws: Unlike other ranged weapons, when thrown, Circular Saws travel horizontally across the surface of the ground. The only way to dodge them is by jumping. These are the fastest ranged weapons to deploy. They deal 7 damage.
- Incinerator: A blaster that shoots laser-like bullets. It deals 9 damage.
- Landmines: The mine is placed on the ground. It explodes when the enemy steps on it, dealing 22 damage. After a mine is set, the player will have to wait for a short while before placing another.
- _Titan's Extendable Arm_: A mechanical hand that is shot from Titan's arm, which hooks onto Shadow and pulls him towards Titan. He then impales Shadow with his sword and kicks him, sending him backwards. In case the player manages to dodge it, the hand still can hook onto them when it returns back to Titan.
