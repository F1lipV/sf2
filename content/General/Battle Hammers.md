**Battle Hammers** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 30, along with the [[Harbinger Sai]]. Battle Hammers function like the [[Steel Batons]], albeit with a different super slash. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Gypsy]] and her [[Ninja]] counterpart.
