**Caramel Boom** is a New Year 2018 and 2019 [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is a giant green lollipop with red spikes on it. The weapon could be obtained as a possible reward by purchasing the [[Equipment Sets (SF2)#New Year 2018 Set|Gingerbread Chest]] during New Year 2018. In order to purchase the chest, players needed 250 New Year coins or 199 [[Gem|gems]]. New Year coins could be obtained by defeating Candy, the New Year 2018 special raid boss. Caramel Boom was also obtainable during New Year 2019, as a possible first place reward for defeating [[Karcer]]. The weapon comes enchanted with the [[Enchantments#Simple Recipes|Poisoning]] enchantment. Caramel Boom functions like the [[Two-handed Mace]].

In the 2019 Christmas update, Caramel Boom came with a new appearance. It could be obtained after defeating Freeze or from the Gingerbread Chest.

The Caramel Boom can also be purchased as a regular weapon in the Nintendo Switch version of Shadow Fight 2.
