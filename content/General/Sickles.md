**Sickles** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are the first weapon available in [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V: The Greatest Temptation]], unlocked at level 25. Sickles function like the claws-type weapons, despite not being claws at all. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Talon]] and a [[Ninja]] counterpart.

## Trivia\[]

- In older versions, Sickles used to work like [[Knives]], using the same super slash.
  - In later versions, Sickles was then reworked into a Claws variation but used a slightly different version of today's super slash, where the user leaps into the air and strikes the enemy as the 5th attack.
- Despite the animation and the sound effects, Sickles super slash only hits 4 times, as the hitbox for the first slash does not exist. This is also the reason why the movelist shows that Sickles super slash has 4 hits.
- The Underworld boss Karcer uses a weapon that looks like Sickles, named _[[Unavailable Known Items (SF2)|Hunger Sickles]]_. However, its model is significantly larger and they function like Mowers instead.
