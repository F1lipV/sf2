**Shogun's Katana** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Boss Weapons|boss weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is used by [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]], the sixth [[Bosses (SF2)|demon boss]] in [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI: Iron Reign]]. It is obtained after beating him again in [[Eclipse|eclipse mode]]. The weapon is a combination of a [[Katana|katana]] and a wakizashi blade. The katana is used to perform normal attacks and inflicts [[Enchantments|enchantments]], while the wakizashi is only used during the last strikes of the double slash and the super slash. It is enchanted with the Overheat enchantment. The formula for the power of the Overheat enchantment is as follows:

## Trivia\[]

- Although it is called Katana, it is actually a [[Daisho]] set.
- It is the second boss weapon to be enchanted with a simple recipe, the first one being [[Wasp's Naginata]].
  - However, if the Time Bomb enchantment on [[Lynx's Claws]] is taken into consideration, then it will be the third.
- Its Double Slash can be used in "No double strikes" rule.
- Unlike the katana, the wakizashi cannot inflict enchantments i.e. the player will get the Overheat enchantment buff and inflict the extra damage only when they strike and hit enemy with the katana part of the weapon.
- The word Daisho is a combination of the terms Daito and Shoto. Daito is the Japanese term for long sword, and Shoto is the Japanese term for short sword.
