**Chinese Dragon Staff** is the 2018 Chinese New Year [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It was obtainable by purchasing the [[Equipment Sets#Valentine's and Chinese New Year 2018 Set|Dragon's Chest]] for 250 Coins of Love or 149 [[Gem|gems]]. Coin of Love was the special currency for the 2018 Valentine's Day and the Chinese New Year update, which could be obtained by defeating raid bosses, with stronger bosses giving more Coins of Love. Chinese Dragon Staff works like the [[Staff]] and comes enchanted with [[Enchantments|Overheat]].
