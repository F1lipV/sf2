This is a list of soundtracks that are included in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. The entire soundtrack was composed by **Lind Erebros**.

The soundtrack can be found in multiple locations online such as iTunes, Google Play Music, and Spotify. In early 2018, a compiled soundtrack featuring most of the songs from the three Shadow Fight games was made available for download. Having a duration of 1 hour and 43 minutes, it features a total of 78 tracks, of which 51 belong to Shadow Fight 2.

## Trivia

<https://shadowfight.fandom.com/wiki/File:Burning_town_origins.ogg>

![[assets/Burning_town_origins.ogg]]

- Burning Town by Lind Erebros has its origins in an earlier composition that appeared in 2005 game Carjacker: Hotwired and Gone. This original version was later revised and reused as Burning Town in Shadow Fight games.
