**Northern Hammer** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the first weapon to be unlocked in [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI]], when the player reaches level 31. It is used by the fighter [[Fighters|Savage]] and a [[Ninja]] counterpart.

  - In the Unity engine update of the game, this text bug still persists, causing the text to become .
- Its Double Slash can be used in "No double strikes" rule.
- The Upper Slash can hit enemies from behind.
