**Ornamental Sabers** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked in [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI]] at level 37, after defeating [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]] and unlocking the [[Gates of Shadows]]. They are unlocked along with [[Ceremonial Shuang Gou]] and the [[Daisho]]. Ornamental Sabers function like the [[Oriental Sabers]]. The [[Underworld|Eternal]] [[Volcano]] wields this weapon.
