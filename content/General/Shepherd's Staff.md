**Shepherd's Staff** is a special weapon in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the weapon of the [[Equipment Sets (SF2)|Warlock's Set]]. This weapon was obtainable through the Battle Pass during Halloween 2021 event, by purchasing Premium Pass to unlock VIP rewards. Shepherd's Staff has a nearly identical design to the [[Wanderer's Staff]], as well as having the same super slash.

## Warlock's Set\[]

Read More [[Equipment Sets (SF2)]]

![[assets/Crimson_Corruption_Set.jpg|Crimson Corruption Set]]

All the items of the Warlock's Set are enchanted with the mythical enchantment Crimson Corruption. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock Crimson Corruption. Once unlocked, the player can enchant other equipment with Crimson Corruption. To use the Crimson Corruption in fights, the players must equip either the full Warlock's Set or any items enchanted with the enchantment.
