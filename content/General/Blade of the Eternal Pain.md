**Blade of the Eternal Pain** are a Halloween 2019 [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They were obtainable during Halloween 2019 update, as a possible reward by purchasing the [[Equipment Sets (SF2)#Halloween 2019 Set|Chest of Nightmares]]. In order to purchase the chest, players needed 350 Raven tokens or 149 [[Gem|gems]]. Raven coins could be obtained by defeating the witch sisters, the Halloween 2019 special raid bosses. These sickles came enchanted with the [[Enchantments#Simple Recipe|Poisoning]] enchantment.
