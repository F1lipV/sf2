**Steel Nunchacku** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is one of the last weapons of [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]], unlocked at level 12, along with [[The Sting]] and [[Grim Scythe]]. The nunchaku's moves are useful for countering enemy attacks, and they are also very effective at dealing a lot of damage. Its moves are fast, but it is hard to hit the full combo. It functions the same as the [[Nunchacku|Nunchaku]], a weapon that can be obtained by defeating the challenger [[Trickster]] in [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]]. This weapon is used by the fighter [[Fighters|Soothsayer]] and a ninja counterpart.
