**Yari** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the last coin weapon in Act III, unlocked at level 18 along with the [[Sapphire Fang]]. Yari functions the same as [[The Sting]] from Act II, it is used by the fighter [[Fighters|Mongoose]] (although he wields a different type of Yari in his avatar) and his [[Ninja]] counterpart. It is also used by [[Aster]], an enemy from the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]].
