**Difficulties** are the ratings of the power of opponents relative to the player's power in the Modes of Play in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They range from Easy to Impossible. Some Modes of Play have a fixed difficulty like [[Duel]] or varying difficulty depending on the person's [[Equipment (SF2)|equipment]] status and skill level. [[Challengers|Challenger]] fights are always at first presented at Insane (or impossible) difficulty, however the player can reduce this by equipping superior gear. The difficulties of all Modes of Play can be lowered to easy except that of [[Survival]], which depends heavily on both skill level and equipment status. Most Modes of Play display the difficulty on the panel on the map except:

- [[Survival]]
- [[Bosses (SF2)|Boss fights]] and [[Mini-bosses (SF2)|Mini-boss fights]]
- Storyline Fights of [[Interlude]]
- [[Gates of Shadows]]
- General's Mercenaries
- Raid fights ([[Underworld]])
- [[Festivus]] and [[Lilith]] fight

There are five difficulties in Shadow Fight 2:

- **Easy** ranked fights indicate that either the player's equipment is far more advanced than necessary, or that the challenge can be beaten with basic skills and moves. The filled section of the bar will be colored green.
- **Normal** ranked fights indicate that the player's equipment is sufficient, or the challenge can be beaten with a small number of skills and expertise. The filled section of the bar will be colored yellow.
- **Hard** ranked fights indicate that the player's equipment is adequate, but requires a decent number of skills to beat. The filled section of the bar will be colored orange.
- **Insane** ranked fights typically indicate that the player's equipment is not sufficient, and that the challenge requires a high level of skills and good utilization of combined moves. The filled section of the bar will be colored red.
- **Impossible** ranked fights indicate that either your equipment is very poor and the enemy in that fight can easily defeat the player with one hit, or that the challenge requires an expert level of skill and combined moves. It is still technically possible to emerge victorious in an Impossible ranked battle, however it will be very difficult. The whole bar will be colored purple.
