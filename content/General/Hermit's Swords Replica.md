**Hermit's Swords Replica** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This weapon is available exclusively from an offer via in-game purchase. Hermit's Swords Replica is enchanted with [[Enchantments|Enfeeble]].
