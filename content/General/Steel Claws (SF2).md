**Steel Claws** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 16, along with the [[Devastator]]. Steel Claws are the first claws weapon that can be bought with [[Currencies (SF2)|coins]]. They have high damage, moderate range, and fast speed. The claws appear to have hook shapes. Steel Claws are used by the bodyguard [[Tiger]], the fighter [[Fighters|Raven]] and a [[Ninja]] counterpart.
