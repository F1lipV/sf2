**Whaler** is the fifth and final [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]. He is one of Wasp's henchmen. Whaler wields a [[Trident]]. He wears black pants and a black sleeveless jacket, both with red trimmings and a red bandana. He also has bronze wrist guards and black fingerless gloves. Whaler appears to sit outside of [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]'s direct crew, seeming to resemble a friend of the late Pirate King rather than a servant.
