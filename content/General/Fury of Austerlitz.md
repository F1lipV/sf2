**Fury of Austerlitz** is an exclusive weapon in [[Shadow Fight 2]] for the New Year 2024. It is a musket from the 1700s British army, with gold patterns that are visible on it. Despite its appearance, the Fury of Austerlitz is mostly used as a polearm with its bayonet, rather than a ranged weapon. When performing the super slash, it fires a bullet that is weaker than ranged weapons, and can be blocked. The bullets deal weapon damage but are not categorized as weapons, ranged weapons, or magic attack, thus, they do not activate nor benefit from any enchantments/perks.

## Trivia\[]

- Being a copy-paste of the Plasma Rifle shot, the Fury of Austerlitz shot only deals 6 damage when hit, which is very weak. Remembering that the Fury of Austerlitz only fires its bullets once, not three times like the Plasma Rifle does.
- In the move list, the super slash's name is displayed as "" due to the developers not giving the attack a name in the codes like other super slashes.
- The texture of the musket bullet is a smaller and recolored version of [[Dark Blast]] magic texture.
  - The hit effect texture is also a smaller and recolored version of [[Sloe Thorn|Dark Force Wave]] hit effect.
- A [musket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musket "wikipedia:Musket") is a muzzle-loaded long gun that appeared as a smoothbore weapon in the early 16th century.
- Austerlitz (current name is Slavkov u Brna) is a name of a town in Vyškov District in the South Moravian Region of the Czech Republic.
  - The name Austerlitz is also referred to the [Battle of Austerlitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Austerlitz "wikipedia:Battle of Austerlitz").
- Fury of Austerlitz's super slash cannot deal critical hits except when the wielder uses it from point-blank. This also applies to Plasma Rifle.
