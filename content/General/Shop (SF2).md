**Shop** is the place where [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] and other [[Equipment (SF2)|equipment]] items are purchased, upgraded, demonstrated, and [[Enchantments|enchanted]] as well as [[Currencies (SF2)|currencies]] are bought. It is the only place where numerical statistics of equipment and shadow orbs can be found. It allows the player to inspect the damage and defense ratings of various equipment as well as unarmed damage.

The Shop is accessible during all stages of the game, and new content is added during special events or when a new Act is reached. It can be accessed using the roll-down menu in the left corner of the screen.

## Shop Sections\[]

### Weapons\[]

![[assets/SF2WeaponIcon.png|SF2WeaponIcon]]

Numerous weapons are available for purchase at the Shop. There are two types of weapons: standard and super weapons. Standard weapons cost either coins or gems and do not have initial enchantments. Super weapons cost varying amounts of gems (both for purchase and for level upgrades) and come pre-enchanted. There is a weapon for every level in the game, and at least one or more weapons are unlocked when the players level up. Weapons can be upgraded for an amount of coins or gems. Upgrades increase the damage output of weapons. Weapons have their own statistics which indicate the damage dealt by them.

### Armors\[]

![[assets/SF2ArmorIcon.png|SF2ArmorIcon]] ![[assets/SF2ArmorIcon.png|SF2ArmorIcon]]

Numerous armors are available for purchase at the Shop. Armors reduce the damage dealt to the players' body and increase the damage of unarmed attacks. There is an armor for every level in the game, and at least one or more armors are unlocked when the players level up. Armors can be upgraded for an amount of coins or gems. Upgrades increase the defense and unarmed damage output of armors. There are two types of armors; standard and super armors. Standard armors cost either coins or gems and do not have initial enchantments. Super armors cost varying amounts of gems (both for purchase and for level upgrades) and come pre-enchanted.

### Helms\[]

![[assets/SF2HelmIcon.png|SF2HelmIcon]] ![[assets/SF2HelmIcon.png|SF2HelmIcon]]

Numerous helms are available for purchase at the Shop. Helms reduce the damage dealt to the players' head. There is a helm for every level in the game (except for level 51), and at least one or more helms are unlocked when the players level up. Helms can be upgraded for an amount of coins or gems. Upgrades increase the head defense of helms. There are two types of helms; standard and super helms. Standard helms cost either coins or gems and do not have initial enchantments. Super helms cost varying amounts of gems (both for purchase and for level upgrades) and come pre-enchanted.

### Ranged Weapons\[]

![[assets/SF2RangedWpnIcon.png|SF2RangedWpnIcon]] ![[assets/SF2RangedWpnIcon.png|SF2RangedWpnIcon]]

Ranged weapons are unlocked after defeating [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]. Ranged weapons have unlimited usage - players can use as many ranged attacks as they want without waiting for any kind of recharge/restore time in between, with the exception of one particular ranged weapon (Landmines). Ranged weapons can be upgraded for an amount of coins or gems. There are two types of ranged weapons: standard and super ranged weapons. Standard ranged weapons cost either coins or gems and do not have initial enchantments. Super ranged weapons cost varying amounts of gems (both for purchase and for level upgrades) and come pre-enchanted.

### Magic Amulets\[]

![[assets/SF2MagicIcon.png|SF2MagicIcon]] ![[assets/SF2MagicIcon.png|SF2MagicIcon]]

Amulets are used to deploy magic attacks. These type of attacks are the most powerful attacks in the player's arsenal. Magic Amulets are unlocked after defeating [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]]. Unlike ranged weapons, magic has a meter which needs to be recharged before it can be used in fights. Magic amulets can be upgraded for an amount of coins or gems. There are two types of magic amulets: standard and premium magics. Standard magic costs either coins or gems and does not have initial enchantments. Premium magic costs varying amounts of gems (both for purchase and for level upgrades) and comes pre-enchanted.

### Currency\[]

![[assets/ShopButtons2.Payment.png|ShopButtons2.Payment]] ![[assets/ShopButtons2.Payment.png|ShopButtons2.Payment]]

In the Currency section of the Shop, players can purchase the game's primary virtual currencies—Coins and Gems—using real-world money through in-app purchases. This section offers various denominations of each currency, typically ranging from small "piles" or "handfuls" to large "chests" or "mountains," with higher-value bundles often providing a better price-to-currency ratio. While Coins are the standard currency used for basic equipment and upgrades, Gems serve as the premium currency required for purchasing "Super" equipment, instant upgrades, and rare raid items. During special events or promotional periods, this section may also feature limited-time offers or bonus packs that provide additional value for players looking to accelerate their progress.

### Raids\[]

![[assets/ShopButtons2.meshok_2.png|ShopButtons2.meshok 2]] ![[assets/ShopButtons2.meshok_2.png|ShopButtons2.meshok 2]]

![[assets/ShopButtons2.meshok_2.png]]

The Raids section of the Shop provides essential supplies for players venturing into the Underworld to face the Eternals. This specialized category offers a variety of tactical items, including Charges of Darkness (Minor, Medium, and Large) to deal direct burst damage to bosses, and a range of potent Elixirs like the Magic Source, Steel Hedgehog, and Phoenix to enhance combat performance through health regeneration, damage reflection, or faster magic recharge. Additionally, players can purchase Raid Keys to access specific boss battles and various premium chests—such as the Chest of the Forest Mystery or Chest of Souls Keeper—which contain high-tier consumables and exclusive pieces of the Monk, Sentinel, or Neo-Wanderer equipment sets. These items are primarily purchased with Gems or Raid Shards and are vital for climbing the leaderboards and overcoming the increasing difficulty of higher-tier raids.

### Free\[]

![[assets/ShopButtons2.Free.png|ShopButtons2.Free]] ![[assets/ShopButtons2.Free.png|ShopButtons2.Free]]

![[assets/ShopButtons2.Free.png]]

The Free section of the Shop provides players with alternative methods to acquire Gems without spending real money. This section primarily features the "Earn Gems" option, where players can watch short video advertisements to receive a small reward of 3 Gems per video (limited to a daily maximum). For those looking to earn larger quantities, the section also integrates Tapjoy offers, allowing players to complete various tasks—such as downloading other apps, reaching specific milestones in games, or participating in surveys—in exchange for significant amounts of premium currency. This serves as a vital resource for free-to-play users aiming to unlock premium equipment, sets, and raid consumables over time.
