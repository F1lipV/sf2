**Composite Sword** is a super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 49 in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 2]], along with the [[Shocker Claws]]. This weapon consists of nine segmented blades, with each segment connected by a stretchable thread. When swung hard, the segments are disconnected from each other, making the Composite Sword work like a whip instead of a sword. Due to how it functions, the Composite Sword has insanely long range with a reasonably quick attack speed but is subpar in close combat compared to usual sword-type weapons, and much longer end lag due to having to wait for the segments to connect after each swing. It is wielded by [[Ancient]], the ghost Whiplash and [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]'s second bodyguard, [[Master]].

## Trivia\[]

- The Composite Sword is one of the most expensive gem weapons in the game. The others are the [[Daisho]], the [[Emerald Cutters]], and the [[Plasma Rifle]].
- The Composite Sword bears a close resemblance to [Renji Abarai](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Renji_Abarai "w:c:bleach:Renji Abarai")'s [Zabimaru](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Renji_Abarai%23Zanpakut%C5%8D "w:c:bleach:Renji Abarai") in its Shikai form from [Bleach](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Bleach "w:c:bleach:Bleach").
- The Composite Sword made a return in [[Shadow Fight 3]], but with 8 segments.
- The Composite Sword is one of the two weapons with a (slightly) unique winning stance alongside Titan's Desolator where the user sweeps the sword a little bit before lowering it down.
