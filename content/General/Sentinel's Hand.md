**Sentinel's Hand** is a special [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the weapon of the Sentinel's Set. This weapon is obtainable only by purchasing the Chest of Souls Keeper in the [[Underworld|raid shop]] for 1199 gems. Upon purchase, the players may receive the Sentinel's Hand, along with some Charges of Darkness. Sentinel's Hand is enchanted with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments|Typhoon of Spirits]]. It works like the standard Spears, namely [[The Sting]], [[Yari]], and [[Silver Spear]]. Sentinel's Hand, however, has a blade with twice the length of other spears, making it the spear with the longest reach, as well as one of the weapons with the longest range in the game.

## Trivia\[]

- Sentinel's Hand's design resembles the guandao, a type of Chinese pole weapon that is used in some forms of Chinese martial arts, used quite frequently in the part of contemporary Wushu derived from the Shaolin or Wudangquan form of martial arts in modern times.
  - Furthermore, the Legendary Guandao from [[Shadow Fight 3]] named [[Emerald Claw]] shares many resemblances with Sentinel's Hand appearance-wise.
