**Guardian's Wrath** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Set Weapons|special set weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the weapon of the Spore Soul Set, which comes with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments#Mythical Recipes|Cordyceps]].

This weapon could be acquired by chance from opening the Ritual Chest during the Ritual Battles Event. Opening a chest costed 600 event coins.

## Spore Soul Set\[]

Read More [[Equipment Sets (SF2)]]

All the items of the Spore Soul Set bring **Cordyceps** mythical enchantment. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock the enchantment. Once all the items of the set are collected, Cordyceps will be available, and the player can enchant other equipment with this enchantment.

## Trivia\[]

- Guardian's Wrath, as well as other equipment of the Spore Soul has references in its design from that of **Devil's Tooth** (_Hydnellum peckii)_, a fungus that is infamous for its white fruit body that oozes bright red droplets of viscous liquid which earns it one of its common names, the **bleeding tooth fungus**.
- The weapon's sprite is displayed in a way that is more akin to a [[Weapon (SF2)#Two-handed Weapon|two-handed weapon]], instead of the way how all other [[Weapon (SF2)#Batons|batons]] are presented.
