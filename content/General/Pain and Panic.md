**Pain and Panic** are a 2016 Halloween [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They were obtainable during Halloween 2016 update either as a drop by defeating Morgana, the Halloween 2016 special raid boss, or by buying the [[Equipment Sets#Halloween 2016 Set|Spider Chest]] for 149 [[Gem|gems]]. Morgana herself also wields this weapon. In the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]], Pain and Panic are available in the [[Shop (SF2)|shop]] to be bought. The weapons come enchanted with the [[Enchantments#Bleeding|Bleeding]] enchantment. They function similar to the [[Sickles]].
