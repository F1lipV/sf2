**Bosun** is the fourth [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]. He uses a [[Glaive]]. Bosun wears a brown, sleeveless shirt and pants and golden wrist guards. Bosun is one of Wasp's henchmen and appears to be a form of messenger & executive to [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]] dealing with [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] directly throughout dialogue. After he saw [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] dealing with [[Kraken]], he offers a deal with Shadow to work together with Wasp to deal with the New Blood. However, after Wasp found out that Shadow is an ally of [[Sly (SF2)|Sly]], Bosun immediately turns off the deal and attempts to attack Sly. After he is stopped by Shadow, Bosun says that it is because of people like Sly that they are losing the war against New Blood.

## Trivia\[]

- He is one of the three bodyguards in the game to use a glaive-type weapon. The second one is [[General]], who wields a unique type of glaive, and the third one is [[Emperor (SF2)|Emperor]], who wields [[Cobra's Tooth]].
- He appears to be one of the oldest bodyguards in the game, the other one being [[Capra]] from [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V]].
