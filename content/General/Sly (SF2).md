**Sly** is a character in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. In the beginning, he is a cowardly and malicious merchant who often takes advantage of unknowing victims, tricking them for his own benefit. He meets [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]], [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] and [[May (SF2)|May]] in [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]] and tries to cheat them out of their money. Sly rarely benefits the group - if at all - often causing trouble for the other three and then expecting them to protect him from the people he offends.

## Story\[]

Sly makes his debut after [[Buffalo]], [[Hermit|Hermit's]] second bodyguard, is defeated. He offers to reveal Hermit's secret power to [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] for 450 [[Currencies (SF2)|Coins]]. If the players accept his offer, he takes the money and disappears without divulging the said information.

He then re-appears during [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]] blaming Shadow for destroying his "business". Because of Shadow's fight with Hermit, everyone now knows the latter's secret power and Sly cannot deceive people anymore, which implies that Shadow is not the first person he was trying to trick. He later agrees to reluctantly let the trio join him when they catch up to him as he is about to leave on a boat at the end of Act III.

Upon reaching the harbor where the story of [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV: Pirate Throne]] unfolds, Sly explains to the group that the harbor was governed by a Pirate King who disappeared under mysterious circumstances a while back, and that now his daughter [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]] has succeeded him. Many of her crew members are displeased with this and have decided to rebel against her by forming a group called the "New Blood". Later, [[Bosun]] reveals that Sly has been taking advantage of the conflict by selling weapons to the New Blood.

During [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V]], upon encountering [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]], Sly is immediately bewitched by her and sets out to buy expensive gifts in order to impress her. Her hold over him is broken only after Shadow defeats her, for which Sly thanks him profusely. During the events of [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI]], when [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] is mistaken as the [[Prince]], it is Sly only who suggests that [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] play along with the ruse.

Sly replaces [[May (SF2)|May]]'s role as [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]]'s assistant during the [[Interlude]] after May is captured during the closing of the [[Gates of Shadows]]. He also introduces the player to a new type of [[Currencies (SF2)|currency]] - the [[Currencies (SF2)|Platinum]] coins. He also replaces [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]]'s role in describing the rules for [[Challenge|Challenges]]. At the end of the Interlude, Sly does not follow Shadow through the reopened Gates. He bids his farewell, claiming that it was an honor to travel with him.

## Trivia\[]

- His name is descriptive of his cunning and deceitful personality. However, although he starts out with the intent of tricking Shadow and company, even then by the end of their adventure, he becomes a friend to them.
- During Christmas 2016 update, Sly is attacked by Freeze when he is trying to loot something from the [[Underworld]]. He is later found by [[Volcano]] and [[Vortex]].
- During New Year 2018 update, when Candy appears and offers the [[Shadow|player]] some sweets, Sly comes just in time to warn that the sweets have been poisoned. Angry at Sly for interrupting her, Candy freezes him with a blizzard of sugar, blinding him. Then Sly gives the player some Elixir to help them deal with Candy.
