**Ronin** is the sixth [[Challengers|challenger]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]], encountered during [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI: Iron Reign]]. He miscognizes [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] as the [[Prince]] who killed his master and wants to take revenge. If the player wins, he says that since they defeated him without cheating, the battle which killed his master must have been a fair one too. Defeating him rewards the player with his [[Ronin's Dadao|Dadao]], along with 125,000 coins. If the player loses, there will be two options:

- Leave
- Retry by giving +3 gems for each retry (with maximum of 30 gems which means after 11th try it will always cost 30 gems)

## Equipment\[]

- Ronin uses Ronin's Dadao as his weapon.
- He wears Riveted Jacket as his armor.
- He doesn't wear helm.
- For [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|Ranged Weapon]], he uses Kunai of the Wind.
- [[Water Ball]] is his magic.

## Enchantment\[]

- **Bloodrage**![[assets/EnchantmentBloodrage.png|EnchantmentBloodrage]]

Ronin's Dadao is enchanted with Bloodrage, giving a chance to deal 200% more damage on his hit, but would also lose 10% of damage dealt in health. This enchantment won't work if it will cause Ronin to lose.

## Trivia\[]

- Ronin is supposed to wear the Two-faced Helm. But, due to a bug, the helm is not displayed and he appears without helm instead.
- A rōnin (浪人, "drifter" or "wanderer") was a samurai without a lord or master during the feudal period (1185–1868) of Japan. A samurai became masterless upon the death of his master or after the loss of his master's favor or privilege.
