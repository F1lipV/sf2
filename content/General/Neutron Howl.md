**Neutron Howl** is a special [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the weapon of the New-wanderer Set. This weapon was obtainable only through purchasing the Neo-wanderer Chest in the [[Underworld|raid shop]] for 399 gems or 8500 shards. Upon purchase, the players could receive the Neutron Howl or other Neo-wanderer item, along with some Charges of Darkness, elixirs or Mystical Coupon. Neutron Howl is enchanted with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments|Arcane Martial Art]].

## Neo-wanderer Set\[]

![[assets/Img_neo_wanderer.png|Img neo wanderer]] ![[assets/Img_neo_wanderer.png|Img neo wanderer]]

![[assets/Img_neo_wanderer.png]]

All the items of the Neo-wanderer Set bring **Arcane Martial Art** mythical enchantment. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock the enchantment. Once all the items of the set are collected, Arcane Martial Art will be available, and the player can enchant other equipment with this enchantment.
