**Candy Rage** are a 2016 Christmas [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They were one of the possible rewards which could be won by defeating Freeze, the 2016 Christmas special raid boss. They were also obtainable from the [[Equipment Sets#Christmas 2016 Set|Crystal Ice Chest]], which could be bought from the [[Shop (SF2)|shop]] during the Christmas 2016 update. Candy Rage comes enchanted with the [[Enchantments|Stun enchantment]].

In the 2019 Christmas update, Candy Rage came with a new appearance. It could be obtained after defeating Freeze or from the Gingerbread Chest.
