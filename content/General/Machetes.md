**Machetes** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]] that are unlocked at level 5. The Machetes are neither fast nor slow; their moves determine their efficiency in different situations. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Fiend]] and the [[Ninja]] counterpart, although their avatar shows some different type of the machetes.
