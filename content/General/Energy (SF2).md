**Energy** in [[Shadow Fight 2]] is needed to fight. There is a maximum of 5 energy units, and the player uses 1 energy unit to enter any fight (except for [[Ascension]], [[Underworld|Raids]] and [[Challengers|Challenger]] fights). Fighting General's Mercenaries requires one energy unit. The player will gain 1 energy every 10 minutes, but they can gain energy quickly by either watching a video to gain 1 unit. The player can also purchase unlimited energy, which comes at a price of \$19.99.

In [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition]], the player has unlimited energy. The energy bar is replaced with the "Unlimited Energy" icon (picture below).
