**Cougar** is a supporting character who appeared in the American event of [[Shadow Fight 2]].

## Story\[]

Cougar did not think that the past of her people would follow her and said she ran to protect them. When she was a child, a shaman told her about a spirit that howls, waves and tears off leaves. She did not think the story would come true. She warned that Wolf Wind is coming here to drive them away with his cry until they fall lifeless just for fun. She said that shaman explained that in the myths, the foliage turned into birds, beautiful and free. They just have to wait for them to come home.

## Trivia\[]

- She is named after cougar (Puma concolor), a large cat native to the Americans.
