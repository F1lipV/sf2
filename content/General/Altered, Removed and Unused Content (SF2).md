[[Shadow Fight 2]] has changed greatly since its first release. Some characters and perks have been removed, some characters, weapons and acts have been **renamed**, some of the characters have undergone a change of **appearance**. Some of the characters have had both their **names and appearances** changed. Fighting moves have also changed over time. Also, the old storyline was completely removed and replaced with a new one.

## [[Weapon (SF2)|Weapons]]\[]

### Set Weapons\[]

- Dragon Knives: The weapon of the unused Dragon Set. It functions exactly like the Knives. The weapon [[Snowpiercer]] uses a modified model of this weapon.

#### Weapons with both images and models\[]

- Desolator: A weapon that appears to be a smaller version of Titan's Desolator. It has a white blade and black handles instead of metallic navy blue like Titan's. In the 2018 New Year's dojo, a Desolator wrapped in a purple ribbon can be seen on the left.

- Super Knives: Knives with handles and blades decoratively covered in blue glaze. These knives slightly resemble [[Meteorite Knives]] visually.

- Poleaxe: A large golden axe with four heads. It could've possibly functioned like the [[Two-handed Mace]].

- Club: A large two-handed club covered with spikes on its surface. It could've possibly functioned like the [[Two-handed Cudgel]].

- Batons: A weapon very similar to [[Steel Batons]], albeit it is made out of wood and with handles wrapped with black cloth. It is usually depicted in the avatars of all the enemies in the game who are known to actually wield Steel Batons.

- Imhotep Axes: A pair of golden axes with a triangular hole in its cheeks and ribbons attached to their knobs. It could've possibly functioned like regular [[Axes]].

- Fans: A pair of gray Japanese gunsen fans with an emblem emblazoned on them. It could've possibly functioned like other [[Weapon (SF2)Weapon Classes#Swords|fans]] in the game.

- Chinese Swords: A pair of Chinese swords with red hilts and ribbons attached to it with the same color. This weapon is modeled identically after [[Hermit's Swords]], however it has an unused unique super slash move.

- Moon Fans: A pair of fans that has blade-like spokes extending beyond its edges. It visually resembles [[Emerald Cutters]]. This weapon can be seen in an unused [character](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/shadowfight/images/9/9a/Girl_fan_1.png/revision/latest?cb=20190503161319 "Girl fan 1.png"), known as Ilex in Shadow Fight: Shades.

#### Weapons with images only\[]

- Unused Yari: An unused design for a yari that was previously found on the Google Play and App Store preview images. It is identical to the yari design in the game albeit with a blue shaft.

- Unused Nunchacku: An unused design for a nunchacku that was previously found on the Google Play and App Store preview images. It appears to be a nunchacku made out of steel with blue handles.

- Whip: Judging by one unrealized [character](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/shadowfight/images/b/b1/Girl_whip.png/revision/latest?cb=20150914214634 "Girl whip.png"), it was planned to add whips to the game, but for unknown reasons they did not. Also, in the concepts of [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]], it can be seen that the [[Master]] could have a whip, not a [[Composite Sword]].

- One-Handed Axe: Was found in the preview image of [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Shadow Fight 2: Special Edition]] in Google Play and App Store. In the image, you can see that [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] is holding one of the [[Labryses (SF2)|Labryses]] on one hand.

- Cool Katana: A katana that has a similar appearance to a normal [[Katana]], with a single ornamental ribbon on its hilt.

- Super Poleaxe: A huge two-handed axe covered in spikes, with the largest spike found on the poll/butt of the axe. It is very likely that this weapons isn't the same as the weapon similarly named Poleaxe, since this weapon's model doesn't correspond to the sprite of the Poleaxe.

- Jaguar Claws: A pair of gloves with three thin blades attached to it. It visually resembles the Three-pronged Claws wielded by a Mercenary, albeit longer.

- Kelt Axes: A pair of axes that are very similar to the Spiked Axes wielded by a Mercenary, however the heads of these axes are much larger.

- One-Handed Sword: A weapon that is a very reduced version of [[Devotion]]. It is most likely a prototype version of all weapons under the [[Weapon (SF2)Weapon Classes#One-handed Sword|One-handed Sword]] class.

- Caramel Fervor: A weapon that has the same model and moveset as with [[Candy Rage]]. This weapon was supposed to appear in the Summer 2021 event.

- Unfinished Nunchacku: A weapon appears to be a diminished version of [[Nunchacku]].

- Unfinished Tonfas: A weapon that appears to be a diminished version of [[Tonfas]].

- Unfinished Crescent Knives: A weapon with a model identical to [[Crescent Knives]], but functions like Knives instead.

- Unfinished Shuang Gou: A weapon that has model identical to [[Shuang Gou]], but functions like [[Steel Batons]] instead.

- Unfinished Glaive: A weapon with a model that appears to be a very thin handle, making it nearly invisible. The weapon itself is signed in the files as a glaive and possesses a move set same as with a [[Glaive]].

- Unfinished Kusarigama: A weapon that resembles an ordinary [[Kusarigama]], but with a smaller sickle.

- Unfinished Silver Glaive: A weapon that appears to a diminished version of [[Silver Glaive]]. For some reason it works like [[Glaive]].

## Ascension\[]

Originally introduced in Act II as an optional series of single-round fights against magic-less ninjas for a chance to win the Monk's Set via a randomized prize wheel, the Ascension mode was removed in update 2.4.2 and later completely reworked upon its return in update 2.39.0. In its modern iteration, Ascension has been transformed into a recurring, time-limited solo Underworld raid event featuring five increasingly difficult tiers. Unlike the original version's flat three-ticket entry cost and fluctuating difficulty based on player losses, the reworked mode requires ascending tiers of colored tickets (starting with Bronze tickets earned from daily missions) to progress through each stage. Furthermore, enemies in the new Ascension possess shield points, unique perks, and magical abilities, with random fight rules now reserved only for the final two tiers. The reward system has also been overhauled, shifting away from random equipment spins in favor of granting Battle Pass points, Underworld rating points, and Charges of Darkness.

## Trivia\[]

- Shadow Fight 2 once planned to add whips, as seen in one of the unused characters. However, it was scrapped.
- On version 2.36.5, Earth Strike (magic) was removed, and on version 2.37.0, the Normal Size Desolator (weapon) was removed. Perhaps these items were removed temporarily for refinement, since the animation of the heavy and super slash attacks of the Normal Size Desolator is unfinished, and the Earth Strike may be used in another set.
- [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]] is the only boss to have his avatar unchanged.
- In Shades, Morel's avatar reused Serpent's old avatar's pose.
- Although changed in appearance, the old design of six demons (except for Shogun) can still be seen in the game intro.
- Two of the unused fighters' avatars resemble Son of Heaven and [[Fighters|Golem]]'s avatars, respectively.
