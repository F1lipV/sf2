**Plasma Rifle** is a super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 51 in the third chapter of [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Revelation]], along with the [[Thunder Hammers]].

Despite its appearance, the Plasma Rifle is mostly used as a polearm with its bayonet, rather than a ranged weapon. Its Super Slash works similar to the [[Blaster Tonfas]], although unlike the former, which fires a single energy bullet from each Blaster Tonfa, the Plasma Rifle fires three energy bullets in a three-round burst. Each bullet is individually weaker than [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapons]], and can be blocked. The bullets that deal weapon damage are not categorized as [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]], ranged weapons, and [[Magic|magic]] attack. Thus, they do not follow any [[Enchantments|enchantments]] on these [[Equipment (SF2)|equipment]]. The Plasma Rifle is wielded by some enemies in the [[Challenge|Spaceship Challenge]] mode and the [[Tournament (SF2)|Stone Grove Tournament]] mode. It is also the weapon wielded by [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]'s fourth [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]], the [[Corsair]].

## Trivia\[]

- The Plasma Rifle is one of the four weapons that shoot projectiles as the Super Slash move. The other similar weapons are the [[Blaster Tonfas]], the [[Cobra's Tooth]] and Fury of Austerlitz, with the Plasma Rifle being the most expensive out of the three.
- The Plasma Rifle is one of the most expensive [[Gem|gem]] weapon in the game along with the [[Daisho]], the [[Emerald Cutters]], and the [[Composite Sword]].
- Plasma Rifle's super slash cannot deal critical hits except when the wielder uses it from point-blank. This also applies to Fury of Austerlitz.
