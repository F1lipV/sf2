**Labryses** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked in [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV: Pirate Throne]] at level 20 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They work like the [[Axes]]. Labryses are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Lumberjack]] and his [[Ninja]] counterpart.
