**Matter Splitter** is a special paid [[Weapon (SF2)#Others|weapon]] that was introduced in the New Year 2024 update of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It can be acquired by purchasing the Cyber-Dragon Pack for \$7.99, which also includes the **Dragon's Thirst** helm and the **[[Isotope Reactor]]** magic. The weapon and the bundle were initially available to all players upon the release of the New Year 2024 update. It is now only available to players who have reached level 5 or to returning players who were not there at its initial release.

This kusarigama has a futuristic appearance, featuring a sickle with a handle that has high-tech looking ornaments, an elastic rope and tied to it, a barbed weight that resembles a circular saw.

## Trivia\[]

![[assets/Wpn_kusarigama_01_04.png|Blood Reaper from Shadow Fight 3]] ![[assets/Wpn_kusarigama_01_04.png|Blood Reaper from Shadow Fight 3]]

![[assets/Wpn_kusarigama_01_04.png]]Blood Reaper from Shadow Fight 3

- The design of Matter Splitter resembles [[Blood Reaper (SF3)|Blood Reaper]] from [[Shadow Fight 3]]. Both weapons have the same blade shape and weight, with the only differences being the handle and chain.
- Along with [[Vulture's Feather]], it is one of the only two premium weapons that does not come with an enchantment.
