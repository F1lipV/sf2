**Hand of Prairie** is the 2021 Independence Day [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be obtained by purchasing the Casket of Anchestors for 600 feather coins or 219 gems. Feather coins was the special currency for the Independence Day update, which could be obtained by defeating Adanti. Hand of Prairie is enchanted with [[Enchantments|Lifesteal]].
