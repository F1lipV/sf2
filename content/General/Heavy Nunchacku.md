**Heavy Nunchacku** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 33 in [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI: Iron Reign]]. It is used by the fighter [[Fighters|Whirlwind]] and his [[Ninja]] counterpart.

## Trivia\[]

- Despite being named Heavy Nunchacku, this weapon is not heavier than the other Nunchaku, and has the exactly same attack speed as them.
- Nunchaku is a traditional Okinawan martial arts weapon, most widely used in martial arts such as Okinawan kobudō and karate.
