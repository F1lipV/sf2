**Fretsaw** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 1]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is one of the last weapons available in Chapter 1, unlocked at level 46 along with the [[Blaster Tonfas]]. Fretsaw has the same moves like the [[Sapphire Fang]] and [[Silver Glaive]]; it is thus the first non-gem Glaive to have the unique triple-hits Super Slash. It is used by the fighter [[Fighters|Aquaris]] and her [[Ninja|Looter]] counterpart.
