**Energy** in [[Shadow Fight Shades|Shadow Fight: Shades]] is needed to fight. There is a maximum of 20 ![[assets/Icons.EnergySmall.png|Icons.EnergySmall]] energy units but the player can have more than this as much as they wish, and the player uses 5 energy units to enter any fight. The player will gain 1 energy every 10 minutes, but they can gain energy quickly by either watching a video to gain 5 units, up to 4 times per day or they can also purchase 10 energy units by purchasing them for ![[assets/Icons.GemsGreen.png|Icons.GemsGreen]] 30. Also, the player receives energy rewards for completing every even-numbered round in chapters. Energy can also be used to unlock new comic pages in the [[Shadow Theater]] - every 10 Energy Units spent unlocks one page (spending Energy Units in event fights also count). Alternatively, the full collection can be unlocked instantly via an in-app purchase.
