**Sapphire Fang** is a super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 18, along with the [[Yari]]. It comes enchanted with [[Enchantments#Time Bomb|Time Bomb]]. Sapphire Fang has a somewhat high damage, moderate range and somewhat fast speed.
