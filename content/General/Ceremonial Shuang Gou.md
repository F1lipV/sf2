**Ceremonial Shuang Gou** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They become available along with the [[Ornamental Sabers]] and the [[Daisho]] at level 37, after defeating [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]] and unlocking the [[Gates of Shadows]]. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Fury]] (despite her avatar showing her wielding regular [[Shuang Gou]]) and her [[Ninja]] counterpart. They are not used by Son of Heaven in battle, though his avatar suggests otherwise; he uses regular Shuang Gou instead.

## Trivia\[]

- Ceremonial Shuang Gou, along with [[Moon Sabers]], are the swords-type weapons with the second longest reach. The first are the [[Unavailable Known Items (SF2)|War Swords]] which are used by [[Drakaina]].
- Shuang Gou (双钩, _shuāng gōu_, Dual Hooks) is a Chinese traditional weapon. It is also known as Hook Sword.
