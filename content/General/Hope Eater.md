**Hope Eater** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special event weapon]] exclusive to the Shadow of Eternity 2024 event in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be acquired as a random reward from opening a Distorted Prophecy, which costed 600 ![[assets/CoinsFall24.png|CoinsFall24]] Coins or 249 gems to open.
