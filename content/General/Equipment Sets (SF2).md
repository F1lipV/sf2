**Equipment Sets** are pairs of [[Equipment (SF2)|equipment]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. When completing a set, the set's mystical [[Enchantments|Enchantment]] will be unlocked and available in the forge for the player.

## Set of Monk\[]

_Obtained from: [[Underworld]], Chest of the Forest Mystery, Chest of Eternals\` Treasures_

![[assets/Monk_chest.png|Monk chest]]

Players obtain the Set of Monk by playing Raids. At the end of each Raid season, the players in the 1st-5th place at the League will have a chance to get a random item from the Set. The item obtained this way will not come with the default simple enchantment. Additionally, Monk's Set can be obtained by purchasing the Chest of the Forest Mystery from the raid shop for 329 gems or 7500 ![[assets/RaidKeys2-MonkShard.png|RaidKeys2-MonkShard]] shards. Upon purchase, the players will receive an item from the Monk's Set or a lot of raid consumables. In addition to the usual enchantments, equipping the entire set of Monk will activate the Tempest Rage mythical enchantment.
