**Lost Desire** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special event weapon]] exclusive to the New Year 2025 event in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be purchased in a special paid offer for real money.

## Trivia\[]

- Lost Desire currently has a bug where the user's hurtbox is extended to the weapon's model. This means that any attack that hits the sword will also hit the user. A similar bug was recorded in the past for [[Hero's Will]], although it has since been fixed.
