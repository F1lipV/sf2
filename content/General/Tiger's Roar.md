**Tiger's Roar** is the Chinese New Year 2022 [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be obtained by purchasing the Dragon's Chest with patterned coins or gems. Patterned coins was the special currency for the Chinese New Year 2022 update, which could be obtained by defeating Son of Heaven.
