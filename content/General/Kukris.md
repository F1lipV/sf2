**Kukris** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked in [[Interlude]] at level 41 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked along with the [[Flayer Claws]]. Kukris function like the [[Swords]] from [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]].
