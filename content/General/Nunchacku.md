**Nunchacku** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|challenger weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Defeating the challenger [[Trickster (SF2)|Trickster]] in [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] rewards the player with this weapon.

## Trivia\[]

- Nunchaku is a traditional Okinawan martial arts weapon, most widely used in martial arts such as Okinawan kobudō and karate.
  - Similar weapons were also used in China and Medieval Europe.
