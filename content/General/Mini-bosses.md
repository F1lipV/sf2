In [[Shadow Fight 2]], some enemies fought at the conclusion of a major chapter in the story, yet are not bosses, are called mini-bosses. There are five mini-bosses in the game: Cypher, Cronos, Ancient, Shroud And May. Each mini-boss is encountered in a different mode of play in Act VII. Defeating mini-bosses will unlock their achievements.

## Trivia\[]

- Aside from their respective achievements, mini-bosses also give the player achievements obtained from defeating the demon bosses without a single loss (**Not a Chance**, **Unstoppable**, and **Three in a Row**).
