**Stilettos** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are the first weapon available in [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]], unlocked at level 13. Stilettos are a part of the [[Weapon (SF2)Weapon Classes#Sai|Sai]] class; they function the same as the [[Sai]] from [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I: Hero Reborn]]. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Pickpocket]] and a [[Ninja]] counterpart.

## Trivia\[]

- Stilettos are slender, rodlike daggers that originated in Italy in the 15th century and were mainly used as a secondary weapon for knights.
