< [[Weapon (SF2)]]

In [[Shadow Fight 2]], there are dozens of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] that are merely visual variants of previous ones, functionally differing only in their Super Slashes or not at all. A minority of weapons, especially the exotic hybrid or technologically advanced weapons encountered in Act VII, may additionally differ in a few other attacks or basic properties while still sharing many of the same moves with other weapons.

Therefore, most weapons in Shadow Fight 2 can be classified into **classes** based on their attack styles and their weight. To see a list of Shadow Fight 2's weapons ordered by when they are obtained, see [[Weapon (SF2)]].

Weapon classes names are given by the in-game demonstration of their moves, whereas sub-classes are named after a description of the distinct Super Slashes among the weapon class.

Weapons that are written in _italic_ cannot be obtained.

## Knives\[]

![[assets/Knives_SF2.png|Knives SF2]]

**Knives** are short blades wielded in pairs. Their attacks deal slightly more damage than average, have fast speed but short range, and are performed with minimal movement; in addition, all but two attacks are aimed directly at the head.

Knives can be categorized into **two sub-classes** based on their Super Slashes.

## Knuckles\[]

![[assets/Knuckles.png|Knuckles]]

**Knuckles** are low-profile fist-fitted weapons wielded in pairs that are designed to augment the damage of normal punches. Their attacks deal very high damage, have very fast speed but very short range, and, with the exception of their super slashes, are performed with minimal movement.

**Technical note**: knuckles weapons has nearly the same attacks to the barehanded moveset but grant a Super Slash, and they therefore can trigger the Martial Spirit perk and lack a Double Punch. The exception to both is the Pneumo Fists, which grants a Double Punch and cannot trigger the perk.

Knuckles can be categorized into **three sub-classes plus one special sub-class** based on their Double Punches and Super Slashes.

## Swords\[]

![[assets/Swords_SF2.png|Swords SF2]]

**Swords** is an umbrella term for many moderate-length blades wielded in pairs whose appearances share nothing in common yet function identically in all standard attacks. Their attacks deal slightly less damage than average and have moderate speed, but they have moderately long range and their attacks are accompanied by long lunges forward. All non-Super attacks other than their Strong Slash are lateral swings, making Swords weapons well-suited to precisely head-hitting at different heights at the cost of being more easily dodged.

Swords can be categorized into **six sub-classes** based on their Super Slashes.

## Kusarigama\[]

![[assets/Weapon_kusarigama.png|Weapon kusarigama]] ![[assets/Weapon_kusarigama.png|Weapon kusarigama]]

![[assets/Weapon_kusarigama.png]]

**Kusarigama** consist of a sickle tethered to a weight by a very long cord. Their attacks have slow speed but extremely long range, some of which are performed with minimal movement while others are performed with moderate movement. Their attacks deal moderately high damage per hit with more multi-hit attack maneuvers than average that in total deal very high damage. While all attacks except their Spinning Slash have at least one strike aimed directly at the head, some multi-hit attacks are less reliable in head-hitting if their first hit is not a headward strike due to the knockback/knockdown of the initial strike.

All Kusarigama employ the same triple-hit spinning Super Slash.

## Unique weapons\[]

These weapons are unique and do not show any similarities with other weapons.

### Unarmed\[]

**Fists** are the default "weapons" in Shadow Fight 2 used by barehanded fighters. In the absence of a weapon's added attack strength, they deal low damage at very low range with minimal mobility but have very fast attack speed.

Barehanded attacks can trigger the Martial Spirit [[Perk Tree|perk]] and lack a ![[assets/ComboButtons.icon_punch.png|ComboButtons.icon punch]]![[assets/ComboButtons.icon_punch.png|ComboButtons.icon punch]] attack; Knuckles weapons (other than the Pneumo Fists) share these properties.

### Composite Spear\[]

![[assets/Weapon_composite_spear.png|Weapon composite spear]] ![[assets/Weapon_composite_spear.png|Weapon composite spear]]

![[assets/Weapon_composite_spear.png]]

The [[Thruster]], referred to as **Composite Spear** in the moves menu, is a fictional futuristic spear with sturdy construction and head-extending piston that enable it to be used for both thrusting and bludgeoning. Its attacks deal very low damage (excluding its Upper Slash, which deals the highest single-hit damage out of all weapons' attacks) and are slightly slower than average, but have long range, though its attacks are performed with moderately small movement.

The Composite Spear's highly disjointed thrusts perform similarly to Spears' while strikes with its shaft are similar to those of Staves.

### Giant Sword\[]

![[assets/Weapon_boss_giant_sword.png|Weapon boss giant sword]] ![[assets/Weapon_boss_giant_sword.png|Weapon boss giant sword]]

![[assets/Weapon_boss_giant_sword.png]]

A **Giant Sword** is a massive, heavy blade that is lifted via a sturdy hilt and a handle positioned halfway up the edge of the blade. The sole in-game Giant Sword, Titan's Desolator, is only wielded (let alone lifted) by [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]: he thrusts the end of the sword at opponents in the default grip, but with herculean strength, he can switch to a sword-like two-handed grip at the hilt to gain additional range. At the cost of very slow attack speed, its attacks deal extremely high damage at very long range, and Titan performs attacks with such a weapon with aggressively long forward movements.

The attributes of the Giant Sword are most similar to, but are much more extreme than, the standard Two-handed Weapon class, though the Giant Sword's thrusting attacks are more reminiscent of other classes.
