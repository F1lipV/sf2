**Quenched Sai** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked in [[Interlude]] at level 42 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They function the same as [[Sai]], [[Stilettos]] and [[Harbinger Sai]] from the previous 6 Acts.
