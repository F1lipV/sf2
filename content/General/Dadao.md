**Dadao** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the second two-handed weapon which can be bought with [[Currencies (SF2)|coins]], the first one being the [[Two-handed Mace]]. Like the other two-handed weapons, Dadao is heavy and slow, but it is compensated by its high damage. It functions the same as the Devastator from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]]. It is used by the fighter [[Fighters|Backsword]] and his [[Ninja]] counterpart. It is also used by [[Ermine]], an enemy from the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]].

## Trivia\[]

- Its Double Slash can be used in "No double strikes" rule.
- Dadao is based on a real-life Chinese weapon with the same name.
  - In actual fact, this hardly resembles the real-life Dadao. The actual Dadao has a signature ring pommel, and a slightly curved blade. The other contrasting feature is that the real-life Dadao is light enough to be held with one hand, whereas the Dadao in game is somehow ridiculously heavy and completely restricted as a two-handed weapon.
- In Chinese characters, Dadao is written as "大刀", and roughly translates to "Broadsword".
- It has a challenger counterpart, called [[Ronin's Dadao]]. It has rings on the blade's spine.
