**Razor** is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. He is the second and final bodyguard of [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]], encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] during [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]].

He wields the Machetes as his weapon and uses the Throwing Daggers as his [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]].
