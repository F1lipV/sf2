**Two-handed Cudgel** is a [[Gem|gem]] [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 22, along with the [[Maces]]. The Two-handed Cudgel functions like most two-handed weapons, except for its super slash, which consists of three slow, yet powerful strikes. The Eternal [[Fungus]] wields this weapon.
