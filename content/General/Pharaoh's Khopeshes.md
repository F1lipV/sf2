**Pharaoh's Khopeshes** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 21, along with the [[Golden Katana]]. Pharaoh's Khopeshes function like the [[Big Swords]] from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]].

## Trivia\[]

- The khopesh is an ancient Egyptian sickle-shaped sword that was originally designed to loop around the enemy’s weapon and pull it out of the enemy’s possession. It was designed in ancient Egypt for the pharaoh’s royal guard.
