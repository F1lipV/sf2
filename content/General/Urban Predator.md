**Urban Predator** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special event weapon]] exclusive to the Trial of the God 2025 event in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be acquired as a random reward from opening a [[Ermine#Creator's Archive|Creator's Archive]], which costed 600 ![[assets/SummerFestCoin25.png|SummerFestCoin25]] Coins.
