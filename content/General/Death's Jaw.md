**Death's Jaw** are a pair of Halloween 2017 [[Weapon (SF2)|special weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They were obtainable during the Halloween 2017 update, as a possible reward by purchasing the [[Equipment Sets#Halloween 2017 Set|Mystical Chest]]. In order to purchase the chest, players needed 250 bone coins or 199 [[Gem|gems]]. Bone coins could be obtained by defeating Whisper, the Halloween 2017 special raid boss. Whisper herself wields these weapons. These weapons came enchanted with the [[Enchantments#Medium Recipes|Bleeding]] enchantment. Death's Jaw has the same mechanics as the [[Hack Knives]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]]. This weapon is also available in the Nintendo Switch version.
