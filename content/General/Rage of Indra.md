**Rage Of Indra** is a special [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the weapon of the Skanda Set. This weapon is obtainable through the Battle Pass during Bihu festival event 2022, by purchasing Premium Pass to unlock VIP rewards. **Rage Of Indra** was wielded by Rakshasa, main boss of the event.

## Skanda's Set\[]

Read More [[Equipment Sets (SF2)]]

![[assets/Skanda_set_karma.jpg|Skanda set karma]]

All the items of the Skanda's Set are enchanted with the mythical enchantment **Karma**. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock **Karma**. Once unlocked, the player can enchant other equipment with **Karma**. To use **Karma** in fights, the players must equip either the full Skanda's Set or any items enchanted with the enchantment.
