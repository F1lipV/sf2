**Weapon** is one of the five types of [[Equipment (SF2)|equipment]] that are available in [[Shadow Fight 2]], along with [[Armor (SF2)|armor]], [[Helm (SF2)|helm]], [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]] and [[Magic (SF2)|magic]]. Weapons increase the damage done by the player through attacking with the "punch" button, as well as having different fighting animations depending on the weapon being used. Majority of the weapons are bought from the shop with either [[Currencies (SF2)#Primary Currencies|coins]] or [[Currencies (SF2)#Secondary Currency|gems]], but some weapons are only obtainable outside of the shop such as [[Challengers|challenger]] weapons, [[Bosses (SF2)|boss]] weapons, and special event weapons.

For detailed description on each weapon class, see [[Weapon (SF2)Weapon Classes|Weapon Classes]] subpage.

## List of Weapons\[]

There are two types of weapons available in the shop: **standard weapons** and **super weapons**. Standard weapons can be bought and upgraded with either [[Currencies (SF2)|coins]] or [[Gem|gems]], and also do not come with [[Enchantments|enchantments]]. On the other hand, super weapons can only be bought and upgraded to higher levels with gems. Super weapons comes with enchantments. When bought, the level and the enchantment power of a super weapon will automatically scale to the player's current level.

In the Nintendo Switch version of the game, nearly every weapon from the shop comes with enchantments whether it be a standard or a super weapon. However, unlike super weapons, the enchantments' power on standard weapons do not scale to the player's current level when bought.

## List of Special Weapons\[]

Special event weapons must be bought or obtained during their respective events. Boss weapons are won after the player defeats their original owners in [[Eclipse|eclipse]] replays (except [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]). Set weapons can be obtained from the [[Underworld]], either from opening certain chests or during special events.

## Trivia\[]

- Weapon is the only type of equipment with at least 1 item per player level.
- Weapon is the only type of equipment with an item for Level 1 (the knives).
- Imhotep Pounders are the only shop weapon that comes with the Stun enchantment.
- The Snowpiercers from the Chronos Set uses a modified design of the Dragon Knives from the unused Dragon Set.
- Daisho and Hero's Will are the only weapons that come with two non-mythical enchantments (Precision and Poisoning, and Lifesteal and Frenzy).
- Trident is possibly the only standard coin-obtainable weapon that has no Fighters or Ninjas wielding it, with the only known user of it being [[Whaler]].
- Broomstick, Vulture's Feather, and Matter Splitter are the only gem/paid weapons that do not come with an enchantment.
- Level 37 and Level 12 are the only levels in which 3 weapons are unlocked upon reaching. The other levels unlock a maximum of 2 weapons.
- In the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]], the 2016 Halloween and 2017 Valentine's Day special weapons are available in the shop.
- In the Nintendo Switch version, all shop weapons (with the exceptions of Knives, Knuckles, Sai, and Steel Batons) come with enchantments, regardless of being either coin or gem weapons. However, this feature is present in order to compensate for the absence of the forge and equipment enchanting in this version.
- Previously, there was this picture in Google Play and App Store, where there were two unused icons for the Nunchacku and Yari present.
