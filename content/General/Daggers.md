**Daggers** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked in [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They have the same function as the [[Knives]]. The Daggers are the last weapon that can be bought with [[Currencies (SF2)#Gold|gold]] in the [[Act I Hero Reborn|first Act]], unlocked at level 6 along with the [[Blood Reaper (SF2)|Blood Reaper]]. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Seer]].
