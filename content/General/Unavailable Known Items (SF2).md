**Unavailable Known Items** are pieces of equipment, as well perks or abilities in Shadow Fight 2 that cannot be legitimately obtained or utilized by the player, most of which are exclusively used by enemies.

## [[Weapon (SF2)|Weapons]]\[]

### Used by [[Fighters]] and [[Ninja|Ninjas]]\[]

- **Triangular Knives**: A pair of knives with long triangle-shaped blades that can be encountered in [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] (and occasionally in [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]]). They have the same moves as [[Knives]], but with a different appearance. Thorn, [[Spark]] and a ninja wield this weapon.

- **Crusher**: The Crusher is a two-handed weapon with a moveset similar to that of a [[Dadao]]. It resembles the [[Two-handed Cudgel]] from [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV]], albeit with spikes instead of studs and a distinct super slash from the Cudgel. A ninja from [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI]] and Bonebreaker from [[Interlude]] wield this weapon. Crusher is also one of the random weapons [[Duel|duels]] offer during and after the Interlude.

- **Sawblade**: A unique weapon with its stance and moves completely different from any known weapons. It has a long range, very fast movements and high mobility, as well as a signature sound effect. This weapon is wielded by the ghost Mephisto and the soldier Emerald from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]]. Later, two weapons with identical stances and movesets were added - [[Devotion]] and [[Neutron Howl]].

### Used by [[Bodyguards]]\[]

#### [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx's]] Bodyguard\[]

- **Kunais**: [[Shin]] wields a pair of lengthened [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|Kunai]] that function like the [[Knives]]. They are also used by a ninja from Act I and Act II.

#### [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp's]] Bodyguard\[]

- [[Kraken|Kraken's]] (& Ermine's) **Two-Handed Sword**: Both wield a Two-handed Sword that appears to be identical to a [[Dadao]] in its appearance and moveset. However it is coded separately from Dadao within the files, technically making it a distinct weapon.

#### [[Widow|Widow's]] Bodyguards\[]

- [[Capra|Capra's]] **Spear**: Capra wielded a Spear with a small simplistic spearhead up until version 1.6.1, which was then replaced by the [[Silver Spear]]. Its model was later re-used for the [[Hand of Prairie]]. The Spear functions the same as a [[Yari]].

- [[Bear|Bear's]] **Heavy Hammer**: Bear wields a large two-handed hammer that visually resembles the [[Northern Hammer]], albeit without the ornamental feathers. Contrary to its appearance, the Heavy Hammer functions more like a Dadao instead.

- [[Puma|Puma's]] **Sentinel Kusarigama**: Puma's Kusarigama is a variant of the [[Kusarigama]] that is depicted in Puma's avatar with a notched sickle that sports a toothed blade, a chain in place of rope, and a claw for its weight. However, the sickle and chain are only realistic to Puma's avatar as in the weapon's actual in-game model, her kusarigama appears to be just like the normal variant only with the claw attached.

#### [[Shogun|Shogun's]] Bodyguards\[]

- [[Corporal|Corporal's]] **Jumonji Yari**: Corporal wields a yari with a pair of curved protrusions at the sides of its central blade. It functions the same as a regular [[Yari]].

- [[Captain|Captain's]] **Nodachi**: Captain wields a two-handed sword that resembles a very long [[Katana]], but it functions akin to other two-handed weapons in the game, particularly to the [[Dadao]] with its super slash.

- [[Major|Major's]] **Wakizashis**: Major wields a pair of short swords with slightly curved blades that function the same as [[Swords]]. The shorter secondary sword of the [[Daisho]] shares a similar appearance to the wakizashis.

- [[Colonel|Colonel's]] **Bisento**: Colonel wields a polearm with a large blade that resembles that of a naginata, albeit the size difference. It functions the same as a regular [[Naginata]].

- [[General|General's]] **Glaive**: General uses a glaive that has the appearance of two swords conjoined at the ends of their handles (essentially making it a double-bladed sword). It functions the same as other glaives in the game, particular to the [[Sapphire Fang]] with its super slash.

- The mercenaries:
  - The third mercenary uses the same Wakizashis as [[Major]].
  - The fourth mercenary uses Three-pronged Claws which function identically to [[Lynx's Claws]]. They are thinner and shorter in appearance.
  - The fifth mercenary uses Spiked Axes which function identically to every other [[Weapon (SF2)Weapon Classes#Axes|axe]] in the game.

### Used by Bosses\[]

- Titan's Desolator: A giant two-handed sword used by [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]. Its massive size makes it such that Titan can only wield it and moreover, only with sluggish movements. Though it may be slow, it has a long reach and can deal very powerful blows which can even break through blocks.

- **Hunger Sickles**: A pair of sickles that have the appearance of enlarged [[Sickles]] but with the moveset of [[Mowers]] and with a greater reach. It is used by [[Karcer]].

- **War Shuang Gou**: A pair of large fearsome hook swords with two prongs and serrated blades. It has the same moveset as [[Shuang Gou]] albeit with a much longer range. It is used by [[Drakaina]] and Architect.

- **Electric Batons**: A pair of two spiked clubs with plugs on their ends. It has the same moveset as [[Flame Clubs]], but its strong slash and super slash moves eject blasts of electricity instead of flames. The second blast of electricity in its super slash move delivers the same electrocution effect as the [[Shocker Claws]]. It is used by [[Saturn]].

- **Sharp Claws**: This weapon has the appearance of very sharp overgrown fingernails, which has a moveset same as that of [[Steel Claws (SF2)|Steel Claws]]. It also has a unique starting stance. It is used by [[The Blackness]].

- **Insectile Claws**: This weapon consists of curved, flat claws. Starting from version 2.32.0 (when this weapon first appeared) and ending with version 2.36.5, these weapons worked like [[Lynx's Claws]], however, in version 2.37.0, these weapons work like [[Lynx's Claws|Claws]] and [[Machetes]] (only the starting stance and low punch retained from the Claws, the rest of the blows are like Machetes), however, the super slash of Insectile Claws is slightly different from the super slash of the Machetes. It is used by [[Stalker]].

- **Ravana's Fists**: These fists resemble [[Panda Cuddles]], but have a larger appearance.

- **Architect's Sickles**: A pair of sickles with an appearance identical to Hunger Sickles, however it has the moveset of regular [[Sickles]] instead of [[Mowers]] as with its original counterpart.

- **Witch's Fangs**: It is a duplicate of Hunger Sickles in that of its model and moveset, however it is coded separately within the files, technically making it a distinct weapon. It is used by Banshee.

## Armors and Helms\[]

[[Bosses (SF2)|Bosses]], certain fighters and bodyguards equip themselves with various custom armors and helms. Some of them even bear their unique models. Examples are:

- Enemies that look muscular in their avatar usually wear bulkier and enlarged versions of Centurion Mail, Riveted Jacket, Spike Breastplate, and Beast Shell.

- Female enemies (fighters, ninjas and bodyguards) always wear slimmer and more feminine versions of Barbarian Armor, Ronin Doublet and Snake Harness (in Acts I to VI locations); and of Alloy Armor, Redshift, and Luminosity Plate (in Act VII locations).

- Lynx wears a modified Light Helm with an additional bandana, and a customized Leather Jacket with a denser appearance and a belt attached to it.

- Lynx's bodyguard Shin wears a signature armor/clothing that is reflective of his own avatar. The overhanging half sleeves and bottom linings are visually seen during battle against him.

- Hermit wears a wider Conical Hat with visible hair under the hat and a beard and moustache.

- Lynx's bodyguard [[Ghost]], Butcher's bodyguard [[Reaper]] and Hermit's Old Wounds bodyguard [[Lotus]], all wear a modified Snake Mask with an added lower extension that resembles the shoulder pads of a Kendo Mask.

- Wasp and Widow both seem to wear highly-stylized feminine Snake Harnesses. Wasp's has added cuffed long sleeves and trousers, while Widow's has draping sleeves and a skirt.

- Shogun wears a type of Centurion Mail, which is bigger in size, has visible pants without the hanging plates, and a wakizashi sheath.

- [[Shroud]] wears an armor that resembles an enlarged version of Half-Life Armor.

- [[Ancient]] wears a masculine version of Wasp's custom Snake Harness.

- Candy wears a Fur-Ear Flap helm with strips attached made to resemble long hair.

- Nrityu wears a Crown of Night helm with strips attached made to resemble long hair.

- [[Corsair]] wears a western-style hat that is indicative of his pirate character.

#### Armor with model only\[]

- Big Redshift: An enlarged version of the Redshift armor that was supposed to be worn by some male enemies, though no one wears it.

## [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|Ranged Weapons]]\[]

### Used by Bosses\[]

- Titan's Harpoon: Titan uses his extendable hand as his ranged weapon. A mechanical hand shoots out from Titan's arm like a harpoon, which hooks onto [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] and pulls him towards Titan. He then impales Shadow with his sword and kicks him, knocking him backward. In case the player manages to dodge it, the hand still can hook onto them when it is returning back to Titan.

- Fishes: Big sharp-toothed fishes that will follow a straight line when thrown, similar to a dart. Used by [[Vortex]]. On lower-end devices, the initial animation makes the game lag slightly.

- War Axes: Functions similarly to Needles; when thrown, three small axes follow a straight line spreading apart from each other the further they go, making it almost impossible to dodge at a certain distance. The axes themselves are identical to Bat Wings appearance-wise. Used by [[Drakaina]].

## [[Magic]]\[]

### Used by [[Bodyguards]] and Bosses\[]

- Ice Ball: The user creates a spiky ice ball in both hands, then launches it towards the opponent where it shatters on impact. It functions similarly to all straight-path thrown magics like [[Fire Ball]]. Later, the magic became available as [[Shard of Eternal Ice]] and [[Continuum]] in special events, which are just functionally identical. Used by [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]] and [[Corporal]].

- Ice Pillar: Despite the name, it does not function similarly to Fire Pillar. Instead, it resembles [[Dark Implosion]], albeit it takes longer for the user to strike the conjuration into the ground and has taller area of effect. The user smashes an ice crystal into the ground to trigger a straight chain of sharp ice stalagmites and upon hitting the opponent, forms a larger stalagmite underneath causing damage and then crumbling back to the ground. It is hard to dodge without an extremely well timed jump, like all magics with a similar function. Used by [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]], [[Megalith]] and [[Ascension|Ninja]].

- Invisibility: The user becomes invisible after throwing a smoke bomb to the ground near them and stays the same until the player lands a powerful blow on them, damages them in time, defeats them or are defeated themselves. Used by [[Lynx]] (after being defeated in a round), and in [[Gates of Shadows]].

- [[Mind Throw]]: Titan forms a blue energy orb in his hand, then sends another towards the player while holding the original. On impact, Shadow is choked and becomes immobile. Titan then uses the orb to lift Shadow, hold him slightly and slam him back onto the ground.

- Dark Wave: A magic that looks like [[Force Wave]], but is black in color instead of white. The magic then became available to the player as [[Sloe Thorn]]. Used by [[Fatum]], [[Karcer]], [[The Blackness]], Morgana, Banshee, [[Puppeteer]], Faradeya and [[Ascension|Ninja]].

- Medium Charge of Darkness Magic: The Medium Charge of Darkness as magic is used in the same way as any other magic thrown along a straight path (especially the [[Energy Ball]]). Used by [[Tenebris]].

- Minor Charge of Darkness Magic: The Minor Charge of Darkness as magic is used in the same way as any other magic thrown along a straight path (especially the [[Fire Ball]]). Used by [[Prince|Haunted Prince]].

## [[Enchantments]]\[]

### Used by Bosses\[]

- Critical Protection: A 20% chance to reduce damage from an incoming critical hit. Extent of this reduction depends on the power aspect of this enchantment. Several raid bosses use this enchantment.

- Shocking Strike: Chance to cause Shock with a Head Hit is increased. This chance depends on the power of the enchantment. This enchantment is only used by [[Tenebris]], Banshee, [[Gatekeeper]], and Power Mode [[Megalith]].

- Good Sharpening : +15% critical chance for your weapon. It is now removed but is still available in the game's source code.

### Set Enchantments\[]

- Revival Heal: The mythical enchantment of the Revival Set. This enchantment can be added to an item in the game via files, but it does not have any function. In the new version, this enchantment has a white square instead of an avatar.

- Stone Fetters: The mythical enchantment of the Dragon Set. This enchantment can be added to an item in the game via files, but it does not have any function. In the new version, this enchantment has a white square instead of an avatar.

- Searing Luminary: The mythical enchantment of the Heaven Set. This enchantment can be added to an item in the game via files, but it does not have any function. In the new version, this enchantment has a white square instead of an avatar.

## Trivia\[]

- Bear's Hammer attack moves could possibly be a developer's oversight, since before an update, all blunt two-handed weapons share identical moves to that of the bladed two-handed weapons.
- Corporal's Jumonji Yari shares similar characteristics to the [[Magari Yari]]. In fact, it is simply another variation of blade designs. Despite this, it works similarly to the regular [[Yari]] instead.
- Crusher is the only weapon that can still be equipped by the players despite being impossible to purchase or acquire into the equipment list legitimately. It is available as one of the random weapons for duels during and after Interlude.
- Ice Pins was temporarily made available for players to use during the Halloween 2022 event, through Architect where it is one of the random magics available for usage.
- Nrityu's helm and ability are the only unobtainable helm and ability that can be equipped by players through some way despite being impossible to own legitimately, through the _Fight as Nrityu_ random fight rule in [[Dandy]] or Faradeya raids during the Destiny in Red 2024 event.
- The Mind Throw magic amulet has the Frenzy enchantment icon on it, despite having Enfeeble (and Weakness afterwards) enchantments when used by Titan.
- Critical Protection is not available in the in-game enchantment list, therefore it cannot be obtained through enchanting. This enchantment is currently available to only two helms: both versions of Fur-Ear Flap helm from New Year 2018 and X-mas/New Year 2021.
  - The helm Iron Mask which was obtainable from the Starter Pack prior to version 1.6.1 was also initially enchanted with Critical Protection, however the enchantment will disappear from the helm if a player updates their game to a newer version
