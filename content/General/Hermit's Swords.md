**Hermit's Swords** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|boss weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are the weapons of [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]], the second demon boss. They are obtained by defeating Hermit in [[Eclipse|eclipse mode]], after completing [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]]. Though Hermit's Swords are referred to as swords, they are part of the Sai class. They have the same movesets, as the other Sai class weapons, with the Super Slash being the only different attack. They are longer than any other weapon in the Sai class. Hermit's Swords generally perform medium to long range attacks. Their attacks are rapid and fast, albeit the damage is high unlike other sai-class weapons. Hermit's Swords are enchanted with the [[Enchantments|Enfeeble]] enchantment. The formula for the power of the Enfeeble enchantment is as follows:

## Trivia\[]

![[assets/Weapon_boss_swords_old.png|The original Hermit's Swords sprite]] ![[assets/Weapon_boss_swords_old.png|The original Hermit's Swords sprite]]

![[assets/Weapon_boss_swords_old.png]]The original Hermit's Swords sprite

- Hermit's Swords are based on the Chinese double-edged one-handed sword, Jian. It is known as one of the four major weapons in Chinese martial arts, along with the the gun (stick or staff), qiang (spear), and dao (sabre). In Chinese folklore, the Jian is known as "The Gentleman of Weapons".
- The Hermit's Swords sprite image was updated from white-bladed swords with black handles to metallic blades with red handles featuring a yellow dragon ornament. The original sprite was reassigned to its replica weapon.
