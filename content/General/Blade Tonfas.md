**Blade Tonfas** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked at level 36 in [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI: Iron Reign]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Despite being named 'Blade Tonfas', it functions exactly the same as the [[Tonfas]] from [[Act II Secret Path|Act II: Secret Path]]. It is used by the fighter [[Fighters|Nomad]], a mercenary, and their [[Ninja]] counterpart.
