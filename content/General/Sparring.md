**Sparring** is a real-time PvP mode exclusive to the Nintendo Switch version of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. In this mode, the player can fight with characters that are not playable in the main storyline. In this mode, there are 32 characters, 7 of which appear upon the completion of the game. And there are also 39 weapons that you can play with, and just like with the characters, we will be able to unlock 7 weapons as you progress through the game.
