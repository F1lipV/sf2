**Rose** is the third [[Challengers|challenger]] that appears in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. She challenges the players when they reach level 14-17. She is [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher's]] "favorite" wife and wants to protect her master.. If the player wins, then Rose gives her Katana (with Overheat enchantment) to the player, along with 1,250 coins. If the players lose, then they can either :

- Leave
- Retry by giving +3 gems for each retry (with maximum of 30 gems which means after 11th, try it always costs 30 gems)

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What level must players reach to challenge Rose in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Rose, the third challenger in Shadow Fight 2, can be challenged by players who have reached levels 14 to 17. The difficulty of the fight can be mitigated by equipping better gear. Victories against Rose yield rewards including coins, gems, and possible items, special moves, or perks.

Provided by: Fandom

**What does Rose give to the player if they win the fight?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Victory in the fight against Rose results in the player receiving her Overheat-enchanted Katana. In case of defeat, the player can retry by sacrificing +3 gems each time, up to a limit of 30 gems.

Provided by: Fandom

**How is Rose related to the character Butcher in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Rose is Butcher's wife and is introduced when players reach levels 14-17. Known as Butcher's preferred spouse, Rose is committed to his protection. She proposes a challenge to Shadow, offering her katana if he can surpass her in a fight.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the maximum number of gems a player can give for a retry against Rose?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Upon losing a match against Rose, a player can opt to retry. This requires an incremental payment of 3 gems per retry. The maximum gem payment for a retry is 30 gems, which becomes the fixed cost after the 10th retry.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the enchantment on Rose's Katana in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Rose's Katana carries the Overheat enchantment. This bonus on melee weapons offers players an edge during battles. Players receive this weapon after defeating Rose in Act III.

Provided by: Fandom

## Appearance\[]

She wears a short top and long leggings, sports red hair and has a strong fit or toned slim body physique.

## Equipments\[]

- Rose uses Katana as her weapon of choice.
- She wears Snake Harness as her armor.
- She wears Steel Helm as her helm.
- For ranged weapon, she uses Hunter's Knives.

## Enchantment\[]

- **Overheat**![[assets/EnchantmentOverheat.png|EnchantmentOverheat]]

Rose's Katana is enchanted with Overheat, giving a chance in each strike to put a buff that makes her next strike inflict 200% more damage.
