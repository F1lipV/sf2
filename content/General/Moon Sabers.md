**Moon Sabers** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]], unlocked at level 26 in [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V]]. They function exactly the same as [[Swords]]. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Warlord]] and his [[Ninja]] counterpart. They have decent damage, very good range, low mobility and moderate attack speed.

## Trivia\[]

- Moon Sabers, along with [[Ceremonial Shuang Gou]], are the swords-type weapons with the second longest reach. The first are the _War Swords_ which are used by [[Drakaina]].
