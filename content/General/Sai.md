**Sai** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked at level 3 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. The Sai's moves are medium-ranged and generally fast, but have low damage. They are used by the fighter [[Fighters|Velvet]], bodyguard [[Needle]], the 1st mercenary and two [[Ninja]] counterparts.

## Trivia\[]

- The [sai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_\(weapon\) "wikipedia:Sai (weapon)") (Japanese: 釵) is a traditional Okinawan stabbing weapon for stabbing and striking. It is primarily used in ninjutsu and kobudo, as well as in southern Chinese martial arts. The basic form of the weapon is that of a sharp metal prong with two sharp and curved side prongs (yoku) projecting from the handle (tsuka). There are many different types of sai with varying prongs for trapping and blocking. It is based on the Indian [trishula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trishula "wikipedia:Trishula") (Hindi: त्रिशूल).
- The Sai, along with the [[Steel Batons]] and [[Swords]] are wielded by the most of the enemies in the game.
