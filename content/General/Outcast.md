Outcast is the fifth [[Challengers|challenger]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He challenges the players to a fight when they reach level 26-29. Outcast has a rather maniacal personality - he asks the player if they want him to split their heads with his kusarigama, and talks about giving "knuckle sandwiches" to local blunderers. If the player wins, he rewards them with his weapon - [[Heavy Kusarigama]], along with 25,000 coins. If the players lose, then they can either:

- Leave
- Retry by giving +3 gems for each retry (with maximum of 30 gems which means after 10th try it will always cost 30 gems)

## Equipment\[]

- Outcast uses Heavy Kusarigama as his weapon of choice.
- He wears Gilded Platemail as his armor.
- He does not wear helm.
- Ghostly Kunai is his ranged weapon of choice.
- [[Lightning Arrow]] is his magic.

## Enchantment\[]

- **Precision**![[assets/EnchantmentCriticalChance.png|EnchantmentCriticalChance]]

Outcast's Kusarigama is enchanted with Precision, giving a chance to make a Critical Hit with increased damage.
