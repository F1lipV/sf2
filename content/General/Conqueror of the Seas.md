**Conqueror of the Seas** is the 2021 Summer event [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be obtained by purchasing the Treasury of Cold for 600 shells or 219 gems. Shells are the special currency for the event update, which could be obtained by defeating Freeze Jr.. Freeze Jr himself wields these weapons. Conqueror of the Seas is enchanted with [[Enchantments|Enfeeble]].
