**Beach Punisher** is the 2021 Summer event [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This weapon is available exclusively from an offer for real money via in-game purchase. Beach Punisher is enchanted with [[Enchantments|Frenzy]].
