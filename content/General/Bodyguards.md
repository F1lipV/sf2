**Bodyguards** are fighters who protect the [[Bosses (SF2)|demon bosses]]. All demons have five bodyguards. Before one can actually battle the demon, they have to beat their five bodyguards. Bodyguards have their own speaking roles (except Shin).

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**Who is the leader of the group of assassins known as 'The Order'?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

The demon boss Lynx initially leads 'The Order', a covert group comprising his five bodyguards. However, after a defeat by the Shadow from another dimension, leadership of 'The Order' changes hands.

Provided by: Fandom

**How many bodyguards does each demon have in the game?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Each demon boss in the game is guarded by five bodyguards. Players are required to defeat these bodyguards in fights that can span up to three rounds. Winning a minimum of two rounds is necessary to advance to the boss fight.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the role of bodyguards in the game?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Bodyguards in the game serve to protect demon bosses. Each demon boss is guarded by five bodyguards, and players must defeat these bodyguards before they can engage the demon boss. Bodyguards have distinct speaking roles and utilize various fighting styles and weapons. For instance, Widow's bodyguards are mainly charmed men who assist her in fulfilling her ambitions. Upon defeat by the player character, Shadow, they are freed from Widow's enchantment.

Provided by: Fandom

**What competition did Hermit hold upon building his school?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Upon constructing his school, Hermit organized a contest to find a deserving student to learn his secret power. The contestants were Hermit's 2 bodyguards. Four of these bodyguards aimed to uncover and exploit Hermit's concealed power for personal gain, while one sought to annihilate it. The contest drew hopeful warriors striving to earn a spot at Hermit's academy, with the victor learning Hermit's secret power.

Provided by: Fandom

**Are all of Lynx's bodyguards members of 'The Order'?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Indeed, every bodyguard of Lynx is a member of 'The Order', a clandestine organization he leads. The bodyguards, namely Shin, Brick, Needle, Ghost, and Dandy, are loyal to Lynx and carry different weapons including Kunai, Steel Batons, Sais, and a Ninja Sword.

Provided by: Fandom

## Trivia\[]

- Most bodyguards have their names based upon their personality or [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]].
- No demon has more than one female bodyguard.
- Hermit, Shogun and Titan do not have any female bodyguards.
- Shogun is the only demon that doesn't have an [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] bodyguard, as he is one himself.
- Shin is the only bodyguard with no speaking role.
- Hermit, Widow, Shogun, & Titan's bodyguards sport unique names; Hermit's bodyguards being named after martial art styles, Widow's being winter animals, Shogun's being military ranks, & Titan's being every male [[Demons|demon]], plus Wasp, who Shadow defeated.
- Crane is the only bodyguard who fights barehanded. This is a reference to an actual martial arts style, the Fujian White Crane.
- Corporal is the only bodyguard who uses an unavailable [[Magic]], Ice Ball. As of Christmas 2016 update, players can however, obtain the [[Shard of Eternal Ice]] magic which functions in a similar manner.
- General is the only bodyguard who has his own mercenaries.
  - Mercenaries do not use any ranged weapons or magic.
  - Unlike all other characters from the storyline (except [[Shin]], [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx's]] first bodyguard), none of the mercenaries have a speaking role of their own.
  - The fight with the mercenaries is the first survival type fight in the storyline. Second is during the battle of Gates of Shadows, third is in the interlude where all main story fights are of that nature, and fourth is the fight with Cronos which is guarded by Looters ([[Act VII Revelation|Act VII]]).
  - Mercenaries do not reappear in [[Interlude]] or [[Eclipse]] mode.
- Shin, Kraken, Bear, Puma, General's last three mercenaries and all of Shogun's bodyguards wield unobtainable weapons.
- Ghost, Cleaver, and all of Titan's bodyguards (except Guru) wield gem weapons.
- Only Titan's bodyguards utilize [[Perk Tree|perks]] and [[Enchantments|enchantments]] on their equipment.
- Whaler is the only enemy in the game to use the Trident.
- Titan's bodyguards are the only bodyguards to have perks and enchantments on their equipments.
- Titan admits that he believed that his bodyguards were as powerful as Shadow - he never imagined that Shadow could defeat them.
- All of Titan's bodyguards' names represent their profession/designation.
- All of Titan's bodyguards (except Assassin) wield weapons from Act VII.
- Titan is the only boss who has six bodyguards.
- Titan's bodyguards have smaller level gaps compared to other bodyguards. They have 3 level difference instead of the usual 6.
  - Despite this, they also have the highest power boost if they are fought when the [[Eclipse]] mode is activated.
- All of Titan’s bodyguards have names and appearances that resemble their respective demons they defeated.
  - Assassin resembles a Ninja, along with his name, much like Lynx.
  - Master’s appearance and his name mimic that of Hermit’s.
  - Guru’s name means an influential teacher or popular expert, much like how Butcher’s gang looks up to him.
  - Corsair has an outfit that resembles a pirate and his name meaning is similar to pirate, when Wasp is pirate.
  - Emperor’s appearance and name are similar to the Prince and Shogun, both were rulers of Ivory City. He may have also believed that he was the Prince due to being called such for so long after being corrupted.
  - There is no counterpart for Widow, but it can be argued that May can be one since she is a female and also mocks Shadow for desiring women and power.
