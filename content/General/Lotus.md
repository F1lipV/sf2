Lotus is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. She is the first bodyguard of [[Hermit]], encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] during Act II. She is a disciple of Hermit's and claims to have traveled far in order to learn from him.

She has short, dark hair and wears warpaint on her face. Akin to her name, she has a tattoo of a lotus on her thigh. One of her hands is mechanical. She also wears a CheongSam/Qipao in her avatar.

She wields the [[Emerald Cutters]] as her weapon, and uses Throwing Daggers as her [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]].
