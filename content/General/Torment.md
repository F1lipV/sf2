**Torment** is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. She is the first bodyguard of [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]], encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] during Act III.

She wields the [[Sickles]] as her weapon, and uses Throwing Daggers as her [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]].
