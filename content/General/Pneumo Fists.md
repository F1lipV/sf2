**Pneumo Fists** are a pair of [[Gem|gem]] [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 2]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 48, along with the [[Thruster]]. The Pneumo Fists are a pair of gauntlets with pistons and rams built-in above the wearer's fists, working like the [[Knuckles]] with some unique differences. They have the Double Punch combo unlike other knuckles, and their range is slightly higher thanks to their pistons shooting the rams forward. The Super Slash can also cause an earthquake, similar to [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]]'s Earthquake ability, but with half the power and limited range. They are used by the hero ghost [[Fighters|Golem]] and the soldier [[Fighters|Topaz]]. They are also used by the eleventh underworld boss Gatekeeper.

## Trivia\[]

- The Pneumo Fists are the only knuckles that offer Double Punch.
  - Their Double Punch can be used even in fights with the "no double strikes" challenge rule.
- They are inspired by the [Fallout 3](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Fallout_3 "w:c:fallout:Fallout 3") version of the [Power Fist](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Power_fist_\(Fallout_3\) "w:c:fallout:Power fist (Fallout 3)").
- The earthquake strike of the Pneumo Fists super slash bypasses blocks entirely.
