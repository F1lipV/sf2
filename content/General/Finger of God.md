**Finger of God** is a special weapon in [[Shadow Fight 2]] which was offered during the Ritual event 2022. It could be obtained from the Ritual Chest for 150 Face of Darkness coins or 119 gems. Face of Darkness coins was the special currency for the Ritual event, which could be obtained by defeating The Lamb. Finger of God is enchanted with Magic Recharge.
