**Cougar** is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. She is the second and final bodyguard of [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]], encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] during Act IV.

Before her fight with Sensei, she tells Sensei that she was once a victim of thralldom before she was saved by Wasp. After Wasp saved her, she became loyal to her (which implies she thinks that they tried to threaten Wasp) and then she threatens Sensei (as she says "let the spirits be kind to you").

She wields the [[Kusarigama]] as her weapon and uses the Throwing Daggers as her [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapon]].
