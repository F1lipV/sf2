**Don't cross the director!** is a special weapon in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the weapon of the Director Set. Two-handed Blunt is heavy and slow, but its attacks deal high damage, and has medium range. This weapon wielded by Architect, the main boss of the event.

This weapon was obtainable through the Battle Pass during Halloween event 2022, by purchasing Premium Pass to unlock VIP rewards.
