**Reaver** is a [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 1]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 45, along with the [[Mowers]]. The Reaver is a large, long, heavy hybrid of a dane axe and a scythe with a foldable blade, which will fold out when the user performs a certain attack. It functions like the two-handed weapons, albeit with some difference. The Reaver is used by the mini-boss [[Shroud]] and a [[Ninja|Looter]] from the Factory.
