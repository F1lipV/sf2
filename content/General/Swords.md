**Swords** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 11, along with [[Shuang Gou]]. Swords are neither fast nor slow; their moves determine their efficiency in different situations. They are used by the [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] [[Dandy]], the bodyguard [[Wolf]], the fighter [[Fighters|Hellcat]], and some [[Ninja]] counterparts.

## Trivia\[]

- The swords are possibly based on ninjatō (忍者刀), ninjaken (忍者剣), or shinobigatana (忍刀). Ninjatō are allegedly the preferred weapon that the Shinobi of feudal Japan carried.
- The Swords, along with the [[Steel Batons]] and [[Sai (SF2)| Sai]], are wielded by the most enemies in the game.
