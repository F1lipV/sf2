**Shadow Fight: Battle of Demons** (Russian: _Shadow Fight. Битва демонов_) is a dynamic, fast-paced card-based board game published by **Hobby World** in collaboration with [[Nekki]]. The game is set in the immediate aftermath of the defeat of [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]], exploring the power vacuum left behind in the Shadow World.

## Premise\[]

After the fall of Titan, the conqueror of worlds and formidable tyrant, the Gates of Shadows were briefly closed. However, with the throne of the ruler now empty, twelve of Titan's strongest minions—demons from various worlds—have unleashed a fierce civil war. Players take on the role of these demons to fight for the right to be named the new ruler of the universe and the rightful heir to Titan's power.

## Gameplay\[]

The game is designed for quick sessions, typically lasting about 10 minutes. It is an elimination-style battle where the last demon standing wins a victory point.

### Preparation\[]

- Each player is dealt two **Demon cards** and must choose one to play as.
- Seven **Tactic cards** are placed in the center of the table.
- Decks for **Knockout cards**, **Battlefield cards**, and **Trick (Move) cards** are placed nearby.
- Each player starts with a hand of 7 Move cards.

### Round Structure\[]

A battle consists of several rounds. Each round has three turns, divided into four phases:

1. **Selecting a Card:** Players simultaneously pick a move card from their hand and place it face down.
2. **Performing an Action:** Starting with the first player, everyone opens their card and performs one of two available actions.
3. **Checking the Battle Status:** If only one demon remains undefeated, the battle ends.
4. **Checking the Round Status:** If all players have completed 3 moves, the current "series of tricks" is discarded, and a new round begins.

### The "Series of Tricks" Rule\[]

The core mechanic is building a combo chain. Move cards are numbered 1 to 5. Each subsequent card added to a player's chain must have a higher number than the previous one. A player is eliminated if:

- They lose all their energy.
- A "Knockout" card effect is applied to them.
- They play a card with a number lower than the previous card in their chain.
- They are unable to play any card on their turn.

## Victory Condition\[]

The first player to win three full battles (earning 3 victory points) is declared the new ruler of the universe.

## Trivia\[]

- Despite the martial arts theme, the game has a very low entry age (4+), making it accessible for younger fans of the franchise.
- The game mechanics emphasize "combos" to mimic the feel of the mobile game's combat system.
- It is one of the few pieces of media that explicitly states what happened to Titan's subordinates after the ending of _Shadow Fight 2_.
