**The Sting** is a super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 12, along with [[Steel Nunchacku]] and the [[Grim Scythe]]. It is a spear with high attack speed and long range. The Sting is wielded by the Eternal [[Arkhos]]. The Sting works like every normal spear in the game.
