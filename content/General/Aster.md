**Aster** is a character who appears in the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]] of Shadow Fight 2. She is the first bodyguard of [[Widow]], encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]] during Act V.

She wields the [[Yari]] as her weapon, and uses Throwing Daggers as her ranged weapon.

She is dressed in a purple dress and has purple colored makeup on her face, namely the eyes and mouth. She also seems to have a leg brace affixed onto her right thigh, possibly an injury she attained in a fight. In addition to that, she is also the last female bodyguard encountered in [[Old Wounds (SF2SE)|Old Wounds]].
