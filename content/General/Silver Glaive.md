**Silver Glaive** is a [[Gem|gem]] [[Weapon (SF2)|weapon]] from [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 28, along with the [[Magari Yari]]. Like the [[Sapphire Fang]] from [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]], Silver Glaive functions like the [[Glaive]], except for its super slash, which consists of three-combo spinning slashes.
It is wielded by the Eternal [[Vortex]].
