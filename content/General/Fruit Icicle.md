**Fruit Icicle** are a New Year 2021 [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They were obtainable during New Year 2021 update, as a possible reward by purchasing the Gingerbread Chest. In order to purchase the chest, players needed 500 Mint coins or 199 [[Gem|Gems]]. Mint coins could be obtained by defeating Candy. The weapons come [[Enchantments|enchanted]] with Magic Recharge enchantment.
