**Fight Styles**are an aspect of gameplay in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Fighters can enhance their fight abilities in a round through Fight Styles (named simply "Styles" by the game). They describe how well fighters are doing in a round and in turn help in doing better. In combat, allocated to these styles is a meter located beneath the health bar. This style bar can be filled by dealing hits to the enemy. There are six styles, which are (in ascending order): Passive, Hard, Brutal, Aggressive, Crazy and Fantastic.

## Advancing the Style Meter\[]

Fighters start on Passive and can progress through Styles by utilizing different moves and techniques to cause damage to the opponent. Each successful non-repeated attack helps in filling up the Style Bar by a small amount. However, if the fighter does not damage the enemy, hits are blocked for a long time, or the fighter uses the same moves repeatedly, the Style Bar progress will decrease. Once a Style is reached, the fighter cannot go back to the preceding Style(s) during that round. When the Style bar fills up, the next style, if any, is reached, and the bar progress is reset. Reaching higher Styles will reward the player with bonus coins at the end of fights. Style will not persist through rounds, and will revert to Passive on the beginning of each round.

## Available Styles\[]

1. **Passive**: All rounds begin at this Style. It is indicated with the white color, and the game does not name it below the Style bar like other styles; but it does display its name at the end of the fight when presenting rewards.
2. **Hard**: It is depicted with a yellow color.
3. **Brutal**: It is shown in an orange-yellow color.
4. **Aggressive**: It is shown with an orange-red color, and reaching this style for the first time awards the player the [[Achievements|achievement]] "Aggressive".
5. **Crazy**: Colored in light green, reaching this Style for the first time awards the player with the achievement "Crazy".
6. **Fantastic**: Blue in color. Reaching this Style for the first time awards the player with the achievement "Fantastic".

## Examples\[]

- The player begins on Passive mode.
- The player executes a successful Double Sweep; this brings the meter halfway to Hard.
- The player executes a High Kick, bringing the meter to Hard.
- The player executes a High Kick and brings the meter halfway to Brutal.
- The player executes another Double Sweep and brings the meter 2/3 of the way to Brutal.
- The player executes more Double Sweeps, but this does not fill the meter.
- The player executes a Double Jump Kick and reaches Brutal.
