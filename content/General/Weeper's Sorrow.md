**Weeper's Sorrow** is a special event [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapons|weapon]] exclusively dedicated for International Women's Day 2024 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It can be obtained by purchasing the Women's Day special paid offer for \$8.99 USD, which also comes with the armor - Poison Petal, and magic - [[Shivering Earth]]. The offer was available for a week starting from March 8, 2024.
