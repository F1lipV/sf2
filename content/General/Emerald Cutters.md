**Emerald Cutters** are a pair of super [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] unlocked at level 43 in [[Shadow Fight 2]] and the last weapon in the [[Interlude]]. They are unlocked along with the [[Keen Katana (SF2)|Keen Katana]]. They are used by [[Lotus]], a character from the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]].

## Trivia\[]

- Emerald Cutters are one of the most expensive gem weapons in the game along with the [[Daisho]], [[Composite Sword]], and [[Plasma Rifle]].
- They are one of the four fan weapons in the game, the others being [[Festive Fans]], [[Widow's Fans]] and [[Fans Of Passion]].
