**Ronin's Dadao** is a [[Weapon (SF2)#Challenger Weapons|challenger weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Defeating [[Ronin]] in [[Act VI]] rewards the player with this weapon. It functions just like the regular Dadao and [[Devastator]] in Act V.

## Trivia\[]

- The Bloodrage enchantment on Ronin's Dadao has a different mechanic from the usual Bloodrage; the player will lose health by 10% of the damage dealt instead of 30%. [[Naginata]] from Act II shares a similar feature.
- However, when player reaches new level, its Bloodrage will be reset to normal usage, making its user lose 30% health. The [[Naginata]] from Act II also resets to normal Bloodrage when level up.
  - There is a glitch which causes the player to lose 30% health even when they have not leveled up yet. If you restart the game by closing and reopening the app, the special Bloodrage effect will reset to normal Bloodrage.
- Its Double Slash can be used in "No double strikes" rule.
- [[Dadao]] is based on a real-life Chinese weapon with the same name.
- In Chinese characters, Dadao is written as "大刀", and roughly translates to "big blade".
- Ronin's Dadao and [[Dragon's Roar]] are a bit similar in terms of their designs.
