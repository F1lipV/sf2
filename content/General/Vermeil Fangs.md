**Vermeil Fangs** are a 2017 Valentine's Day [[Weapon (SF2)#Special Event Weapon|special weapon]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They were obtainable during the Valentine's Day 2017, 2020, 2021 and 2023 updates, as a first-place reward by defeating raid bosses ([[Vortex]], [[Fatum]], [[Arkhos]], or [[Hoaxen]]), or by purchasing the Casket of Fates ![[assets/Chest_val17.png|Chest val17]] and Ruby Treasure Chest ![[assets/Val20Chest.png|Val20Chest]]. Vermeil Fangs are enchanted with [[Enchantments|Magic Recharge]]. In the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]], they are available to be bought in the shop.

## Trivia\[]

- Sai originated in India and spread to other Hindu and Buddhist states including Japan in the 15th century.
- This is one of the five weapons that can be obtained in the amount of two pieces. The other weapons are : [[Fans of Passion]], [[Fatemaker]], [[Panda Cuddles]] and [[Chinese Dragon Staff]].
