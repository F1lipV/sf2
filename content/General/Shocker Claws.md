**Shocker Claws** are a pair of [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 2]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. They are unlocked at level 49, along with the [[Composite Sword]]. They are used by the hero ghost [[Fighters|Harpy]] and the soldier Beryl. Shocker Claws has a unique super slash.

## Trivia\[]

- The burst of electricity at the end of the Electric Shock move ignores blocks and makes the opponent shake and fall when the electric shock hits. It is also capable of dealing a Head hit.
- Shocker Claws only have the electric shock strike vision in its super slashes which none of the other slashes do.
- It is practically impossible to fully hit the opponent with the super slash attack as they always get ragdolled on second hit, causing the Electric Shock attack to miss.
