**Hoaxen** is the seventh Eternal of the [[Underworld]]. He is one of the three bosses in the second floor (along with [[Fatum]] and [[Arkhos]]), and is the last boss of the tier 2. Hoaxen has a shield of 12,639 points, which has to be destroyed in 7 minutes and 50 seconds by six players before they can finish him. Players must reach [[Dan (SF2)|Dan]] 6 before they can challenge him. He wields [[Harrier Hooks]] as his weapon and fights with him carry random rules.

The players can obtain the keys to fight him by defeating Arkhos. If the players do not have any Hoaxen keys, they can buy one key for 25 [[Gem|gems]].

In battle, Hoaxen is very aggressive, just like every raid boss. He has extreme offensive power, making it possible for him to instantly defeat any player with just one strike as his weapon is an axes-type weapon and has enchanted overheat support. He is immune to the [[Enchantments#Medium Recipes|Stun]] enchantment.

After being defeated, Hoaxen mentions the one who rules over the [[Bosses (SF2)|Eternals]], saying that the Father is waiting for the players.

## Story\[]

Within a fractured, gravity-defying realm of floating ruins, the multi-headed monstrosity known as Hoaxen confronted Shadow, taunting the mortal with the terrifying claim that his entire life was merely an illusion crafted by the Eternals. Scoffing at those who relied on simple brute force, fire, water, or disease, the bizarre entity threatened to instead shatter the warrior's mind and drive him completely mad. When the human seemingly showed defiance, Hoaxen questioned if Shadow was laughing at them, revealing that his inner "brother" suggested sending mortal souls to an abyss of eternal misery so their world would peacefully yield without shedding a single drop of blood. Defying the horrific psychological onslaught, Shadow engaged the illusionist and decisively struck him down in combat. Defeated and disoriented, Hoaxen bizarrely claimed that his own boundless mercy would have led him to kill the warrior, but stated his less tolerant brother was allowing Shadow to leave—provided the mortal didn't lose his wits on the way out. However, the crushing defeat suddenly severed the dark psychic connection between the entity's fractured minds, prompting a panicked Hoaxen to cry out for his now-silent brother. Lamenting that true isolation was far worse than any physical fire or blade, the despairing, newly alone creature finally dismissed the victorious warrior, ominously warning him to proceed because the supreme Father was waiting.

## Special Ability\[]

- **Soul Extract**

![[assets/HoaxenSoulExtract.gif|HoaxenSoulExtract]]

Hoaxen can extend the tentacles from his back to pierce through the player, which have the ability to make their soul come out from their body. If the player gets hit, they will take 25 damage (75 if buffed with Overheat) and become incapacitated for a moment before they can move again. The player can easily avoid his tentacles by rolling or jumping, or they can prevent it by always staying close to Hoaxen.

## Trivia\[]

- Hoaxen personifies the supernatural principles of the world as his appearances and the domain where he resides manifest the concept of the Unexplainable or Incomprehensible. He furthermore represents humanity's madness and insanity that are evoked when one is exposed to things that are beyond understanding.
  - In the background, a structure is distorted into a shape of a _Penrose Triangle_ with multiple faces and eyes emerging on each side, this triangle is widely recognized as a popular element of optical illusion.
  - Hoaxen has multiple personalities; he refers to himself as "we" or "us", and he refers to his other-self as "brother".
- Hoaxen is the only Eternal to reappear in Shades.
