**Dandy** is [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]]'s fifth and final [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is an arrogant fighter who sees The Order as merely an opportunity to enrich his skills, and does not take it seriously. He wields [[Swords]] as his weapon.

Dandy returned as a raid boss in the Destiny in Red 2024 event where he was encountered on the first floor of the Underworld, along with Faradeya.

## Storyline\[]

### Act I\[]

When he meets [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] in [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]], Dandy tries to intimidate Shadow by telling him about his success, "_...already impressive sword skills_" and strength. However, when defeated, Dandy says that he plans on leaving the Order to "_...return to my true place, my seat of high power_", and that Lynx will not be as easily defeated as he was. Dandy returns as Lynx's bodyguard in the fight to break the [[Gates of Shadows#Seals|seals]] in the [[Interlude]].

### Destiny in Red Event\[]

In the Destiny in Red event storyline, he appeared as a character in the [[Puppeteer]]'s theatre play - _The Legend of Nrityu_. He first appeared to be demanding an old man to be allowed inside their abode and threatening that he will set the place on fire if he is refused shelter. It is then revealed that Dandy was being chased by Nrityu, a grim reaper who has come to take away his life as a punishment for murdering innocent people. He attempts to resist and fight back the grim reaper but was easily overpowered, and that is where he meets his end.

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

- **Lightning Strike**

Dandy can summon four lightning bolts in front of him, firing off successively away from him. Lightning bolts will knock the player down and deal considerable damage to them. Due to the distance of each lightning strike from another, the player may get hit consecutively if they remain lying down after getting struck by a lightning bolt. The player can interrupt Dandy from activating this ability by hitting him.

- **Cobra** ![[assets/IconCobra.png|IconCobra]]

A chance that during the first 3 seconds of a round that Dandy's First Strike would reduce the player's health by 10%.

- **Avenger** ![[assets/IconAvenger.png|IconAvenger]]

A chance to trigger Avenger, which allows Dandy to deal a Critical Hit with increased damage for 5 seconds after receiving a Critical Hit.

- **Helm Breaker** ![[assets/IconHelmBreaker.png|IconHelmBreaker]]

A chance to trigger Helm Breaker, which allows Dandy to increase his Head Hit damage for 5 seconds after dealing a head hit to the player.

- **Steel Foot** ![[assets/IconStunningFoot.png|IconStunningFoot]]

A chance for Dandy to knock the player down with a kick. The chance is doubled when he's in the air.

- **Weakness** ![[assets/EnchantmentWeakness.png|EnchantmentWeakness]]

A chance to allow Dandy to weaken the player's attacks by 50% for 5 seconds upon dealing a successful hit with his weapon.

- **Magic Recharge** ![[assets/EnchantmentMagicRecharge.png|EnchantmentMagicRecharge]]

A chance to allow Dandy to gain increased magic recharge after taking body hits.

- **Precision** ![[assets/EnchantmentCriticalChance.png|EnchantmentCriticalChance]]

A chance for Dandy's ranged weapons to deal a Critical Hit with increased damage.

- **Stun** ![[assets/EnchantmentStun.png|EnchantmentStun]]

A chance for Dandy's magic to stun the player for 5 seconds upon being hit.
