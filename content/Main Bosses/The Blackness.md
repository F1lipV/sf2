**The Blackness** is the third Boss of the Immortal's World encountered in [[Underworld]] Tier 4, along with [[Gatekeeper]], [[Saturn]] and [[Stalker]]. Fight with Blackness requires 4 players and carries random rules.

The Blackness is an entity residing in the forests of the ancient swamps, at the Immortals' World. She preys on any sentient being by consuming their mind and memories.

**What tier is The Blackness encountered in the Immortal's World?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In the Immortal's World of the Underworld, The Blackness is a boss encountered in the fourth tier. This dark spirit is one of three bosses in this tier, the others being Gatekeeper and Saturn. ## Story\[]

Deep within the treacherous marshes, Shadow and his companions were confronted by a terrifying phantom known as The Blackness, which had disturbingly taken the exact physical form of May. The eerie entity taunted Shadow, asking if he was sick before delivering a chilling sequence of cryptic prophecies involving sacrifice, butchery, and spilled blood, threatening to physically show him this painful future. Unshaken by the psychological warfare, Shadow engaged the dark mimic and successfully struck it down. In the immediate aftermath, a deeply unsettled May shivered at the entity's ghostly resemblance to her and its intense, specific focus on Shadow. However, their hard-won victory inadvertently triggered a devastating twist: defeating The Blackness somehow restored a missing piece to the fugitive Lazarus. Revealing his true, ruthlessly violent nature, Lazarus thanked the group for returning a part of himself before abruptly abandoning them to enact a bloody revenge, completely ignoring Simon's desperate pleas to spare unnecessary victims. Realizing they had been manipulated into unleashing a monster, a furious Sensei berated Simon for lying to them and condemning the world. Finally admitting a grave mistake in his calculations, a panicked Simon urged the group that they must immediately rush to the city to stop Lazarus before he could raze it to the ground and massacre the innocent alongside the Patriarch.

## Requirements\[]

In order to challenge The Blackness, players require:

- 1![[assets/Key12.png|Key12]] The Blackness key

The keys can be obtained as a reward after successfully defeating [[Saturn]], or by purchasing it for 37 gems.

## Possible Rules\[]

The possible rules during the fights with The Blackness are:

- Hot ground (12 seconds)
- Light in the darkness
- No double strikes
- Passing burning effect (Has no effect on blackness)
- Your health regenerates

## Special Ability\[]

- **Embrace of Darkness**

![[assets/Blacknessability.gif|Blacknessability]]

The Blackness can conjure a wave of dark energy in the form of a giant hand from behind the player, which will reduce their health by 65% upon getting hit. The wave may be dodged by jumping over it, or by quickly dashing forward to get out of its range. The Blackness is invulnerable to melee attacks while activating this ability.

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

- **Crag** ![[assets/Potion_rock.png|Potion rock]]

Blackness can hardly be knocked down.

- **Furious** ![[assets/IconFurious.png|IconFurious]]

A chance for The Blackness to get 30% more damage after dealing a successful hit to the player. The effect wears off if she receives a hit herself.

- **Full Power** ![[assets/IconEnergyRush.png|IconEnergyRush]]

A chance for The Blackness' magic damage to increase by 50% for 4 seconds after her magic gets fully charged.

- **Block Breaker** ![[assets/IconBlockBreaker.png|IconBlockBreaker]]

A chance for The Blackness to bypass the player's block.

- **Martial Spirit** ![[assets/IconMartialSpirit.png|IconMartialSpirit]]

A chance to increase The Blackness' magic recharge by 100% after successfully dealing an unarmed hit.

- **Frenzy (Weapon)** ![[assets/Frenzy.png|Frenzy]]

A chance to increase The Blackness' melee damage by 150% for 5 seconds after making a successful weapon hit.

- **Bleeding (Weapon)** ![[assets/EnchantmentBleeding.png|EnchantmentBleeding]]

A chance to inflict Bleeding to the player after getting hit by The Blackness' weapon, which depletes their health by 6% for 5 seconds.

- **Overheat (Weapon)** ![[assets/EnchantmentOverheat.png|EnchantmentOverheat]]

Each hit of The Blackness' weapon has a chance to activate a buff that allows her next strike to inflict 200% more damage. The buff wears off after 5 seconds if no strikes has been made after its activation.

- **Bloodrage (Weapon)** ![[assets/EnchantmentBloodrage.png|EnchantmentBloodrage]]

A chance for The Blackness to deal 200% more damage on upon hit.

- **Magic Recharge (Weapon)** ![[assets/EnchantmentMagicRecharge.png|EnchantmentMagicRecharge]]

A chance for The Blackness to increase magic recharge by 300% upon dealing a successful hit.

- **Weakness (Magic and Special Ability)** ![[assets/EnchantmentWeakness.png|EnchantmentWeakness]]

A chance to allow The Blackness to weaken the player's attacks by 75% for 5 seconds after a dealing a successful magic or special ability hit.

## Trivia\[]

- Out of all enemies and raid bosses that have been introduced, The Blackness has the most number of enchantments on one type of gear, having 5 enchantments on her weapon alone (Bloodrage, Overheat, Magic Recharge, Bleeding and Frenzy). Due to the many enchantments that increase the power of weapons, blackness can kill the player with a single unblocked hit.
- Blackness's bloodrage enchancement does not cause her to lose health.

![[assets/Girl_May.png|Girl May]] ![[assets/Girl_May.png|Girl May]]

![[assets/Girl_May.png]]

- The Blackness takes the physical form of [[May (SF3)|future May]], specifically mimicking her tactical outfit from [[Shadow Fight 3]]. Coupled with its cryptic dialogue regarding an "offspring", "the mind" and a "sphere", this entity serves as a direct foreshadowing of the Descendant, the Shadow Mind, and the overarching plot of the sequel.
  - When The Blackness talks about an "offspring", "butchery" and "spilling blood", it is actually spoiling the plot of Shadow Fight 3. It is warning Shadow that he and May will eventually have a baby (the Descendant), but that May will tragically die when the baby is born because Shadow will kill her.
  - Within the lore, The Blackness functions less as a simple shapeshifter and more as a temporal oracle. By appearing as May from the future and predicting a "great sacrifice" and "spilled blood", it forces Shadow to confront a manifestation of the tragic fate that ultimately awaits him.
  - Although The Blackness heavily foreshadows the plot of Shadow Fight 3 by mimicking May's tactical outfit and prophesying about her "offspring" and a "great sacrifice", this boss was actually added to Shadow Fight 2 the year after the main storyline of Shadow Fight 3 had already concluded.
- There is a bug where the Blackness has extended hitbox.
