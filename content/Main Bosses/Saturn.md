**Saturn** is the second Boss of the Immortal's World encountered in [[Underworld]] Tier 4, along with [[Gatekeeper]], [[The Blackness]] and [[Stalker]]. Fights with her require 4 players and carry random rules.

To enter Saturn's raid, the player requires one Saturn key, which is obtainable by defeating Gatekeeper or purchasing it with 37 gems.

## Story\[]

Upon arriving at a formidable, high-tech prison facility, Shadow and his companions were intercepted by Saturn, a ruthless, cybernetically enhanced warden who mocked their willing intrusion and threatened them with execution on her scaffold. However, Shadow met her fierce challenge and decisively struck her down in combat, bypassing her lethal sentence. Defeated and bitter, Saturn revealed that a fugitive named Lazarus had escaped her confines and sought refuge in the treacherous marshes, daring the group to pursue him if they too wished to perish. While Simon mocked the warden's failure and May astutely pointed out that the marshes must hold terrifying dangers if a loyal warrior like Saturn was too afraid to enter them, Saturn defensively rationalized her cowardice by claiming her life was more valuable than chasing a leaderless rabble. Taking in the exchange, Sensei decisively concluded that their path inevitably led into those dangerous marshes, hoping that finding Lazarus would finally uncover how Shadow was mysteriously connected to the Obelisk and why the enigmatic Patriarch had condemned this new world to death.

## Special Ability\[]

- **Mechanical Blaster**

![[assets/SaturnAbility1.gif|SaturnAbility1]] ![[assets/SaturnAbility1.gif|SaturnAbility1]]

![[assets/SaturnAbility1.gif]]

Saturn releases a mechanical blaster that shoots 2 fireball projectiles, one of it moving down, dealing 35% damage on impact. The player can dodge the projectiles by rolling, ducking, or jumping within the right time. The player can also interrupt Saturn from using her ability by attacking her with melee attacks, ranged weapons, magic attacks, and charges of darkness.

![[assets/SaturnAbility2.gif|If the player is close to Saturn when she fires her blaster.]] ![[assets/SaturnAbility2.gif|If the player is close to Saturn when she fires her blaster.]]

![[assets/SaturnAbility2.gif]]If the player is close to Saturn when she fires her blaster.

Saturn's mechanical blaster ability has an additional feature: if the player is close to her while she fires, she will use the blaster's fiery plasma bayonet, swinging it to strike the player. This attack deals 20% damage (40% on a head hit) and induces a bleeding effect. Saturn is immune to damage while using her blaster in melee form.

## Trivia\[]

- [Saturn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn "wikipedia:Saturn") is the name of the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest in the Solar System, after Jupiter.
- Saturn's Mechanical Blaster ability looks a little similar to the [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|Incinerator]] animation when grabbing it.
- Saturn is the second non-event raid boss that is not an Eternal.
- Saturn's electric batons work exactly like [[Flame Clubs]].

![[assets/Boss_saturn_hard.png|Boss saturn hard]] ![[assets/Boss_saturn_hard.png|Boss saturn hard]]

![[assets/Boss_saturn_hard.png]]

- Although she does not have a Power Mode like the other Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 bosses, Saturn has an unused avatar of her Power Mode in the game files. This can possibly be an indication that her Power Mode might be added in the future.
