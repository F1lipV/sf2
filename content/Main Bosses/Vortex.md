**Vortex** is the fourth Eternal encountered in [[Underworld]], the multiplayer mode of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is one of the four bosses of the first floor (Along with [[Volcano]], [[Megalith]], and [[Fungus]]), and the last boss of the first floor. Vortex has a shield of 11,531 points which has to be destroyed in 12 minutes and 40 seconds by eight (7 in Power Mode) players before they can finish him. He wields [[Silver Glaive]] as weapon and uses fishes as ranged weapons. The players need to reach [[Dan (SF2)|Dan 3]] before they can challenge him.

The players can obtain the keys to fight him by defeating [[Fungus]]. One particular [[Underworld#Daily Quest|daily quest]] also will reward the players with Vortex key upon completed. If the players do not have any Vortex keys, they can buy one key for 20 [[Gem|gems]].

In battle, Vortex is extremely aggressive and his moves are very fast compared to standard enemies. He is immune to the [[Enchantments#Stun|Stun]] enchantment.

After he is defeated, Vortex will mention the one that rules over the Eternals. Vortex states that he has no mercy and has no desire to understand humans.

## Story\[]

Deep within a flooded subterranean cavern, the aquatic Eternal known as Vortex confronted Shadow, fiercely declaring that dark waters would inevitably engulf the earth and the Gates of Shadows, ensuring their secrets would be lost to the abyss with no survivors left on the surface to tell the tale. However, defying the monstrous entity's apocalyptic prophecy, Shadow engaged Vortex and decisively struck him down in combat. Astonished by his defeat, the fallen Eternal admitted that a human had won for the first time in a thousand years, grudgingly admiring the mortal's incredibly strong will to live. Despite this unprecedented victory, Vortex ominously insisted that Shadow's ultimate fate was already decided and that he would inevitably die, possessing only the illusion of choosing how his life would end. The aquatic creature revealed that this final, fatal decision belonged to an overarching, merciless ruler; and while the Eternals had interacted with humanity long enough to understand them, their supreme leader harbored no such desire for comprehension—a terrifying reality that Vortex promised Shadow would very soon face.

## Trivia\[]

- Vortex represents all the water of the world including the oceans, and may be responsible for causing tsunamis and floods as well as storms and rain.
- Vortex is one of the three enemies that use an unobtainable ranged weapon, the other being [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]] and [[Drakaina]].
- Vortex is the second boss to not have a special ability. The first is [[Megalith]].
