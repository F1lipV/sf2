**Volcano** is the first Eternal encountered in the [[Underworld]], the multiplayer mode of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is one of the four bosses of the first floor of Underworld (along with [[Megalith]], [[Fungus]], and [[Vortex]]). He has a shield of 1757 points which has to be destroyed within 5 minutes and 20 seconds by 4 players before they can finish him. He wields the [[Ornamental Sabers]] as his weapon.
Five keys to fight with Volcano are given after the players enters the Underworld for the first time. If players have less than five Volcano keys at the end of a season, the stock will be refilled to five at the beginning of a new season. If players have five or more Volcano keys, the number of keys will not be changed. Some daily quests also can reward the players with Volcano keys upon completed. They can also buy one key for 5 [[Gem|gems]].

In battle, Volcano is very aggressive and his reactions are faster as compared to standard enemies. He is immune to the [[Enchantments#Stun|Stun]] enchantment.

## Story\[]

### Prelude\[]

Deep within his molten domain, the ancient Eternal known as Volcano confronts Shadow, arrogantly taunting the warrior for foolishly attempting to correct their grave mistake of opening the Gates of Shadows. Declaring that only the divine Eternals possessed the true strength to eliminate the resulting Shadow contagion, Volcano dismisses his opponent as a weak mortal who would inevitably perish like all the rest.

### Volcano Raid\[]

The fiery behemoth boasts that he was the very first of his kind to awaken, prophesying that the magma flowing through his veins would soon blanket the land to cleanse it for new life, and that countless fighters would fall by his hand as he completed his destructive mission. However, despite his grand decrees, Volcano is ultimately defeated in the ensuing clash. Humbled by the loss, the fallen Eternal formally concedes the battle, but he leaves the victorious Shadow with a chilling final warning: their triumph had inadvertently roused his brother from slumber, a dangerous new foe whose outward appearance masked a deceptively quick and deadly nature.

## Special Ability\[]

- **Teleportation**

Volcano has the ability to teleport by which he disappears in the cyan beam coming from his sabers and shows up attacking from the back, dealing 25 damage. It can be easily dodged by a back-handspring or a well-timed roll. The players also can interrupt the attack by attacking Volcano before he finishes his teleportation.

## Perk and Enchantments (Power Mode only)\[]

- **Shielding**![[assets/EnchantmentShielding.png|EnchantmentShielding]]

Volcano has a chance to reduce incoming damage by 75% for 7 seconds after receiving a Hit.

- **Overheat**![[assets/EnchantmentOverheat.png|EnchantmentOverheat]]

Volcano has a chance to put a buff on him that makes his next strike inflict 200% more damage after he receives a body hit or successfully lands a hit.

## Trivia\[]

- Given his name, Volcano represents fire, and may be responsible for volcanic eruptions and possibly even natural fires.
- He is the third boss to use Teleportation as a perk after [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]] and [[May (SF2)|May]].
- Volcano's name is misspelled as Vulcan on the [Shadow Fight 2: 10th Anniversary](https://youtu.be/Ocm_F3BshDY?si=HCNsvqA-3-lPpTbl) video.
