**Drakaina** is the ninth Eternal and the second boss encountered in [[Underworld]] Tier 3. She is one of the three bosses of the third floor (Along with [[Karcer]] and [[Tenebris]]). Drakaina has a shield of 12,513 points, which has to be destroyed in 7 minutes by six players before they can finish her. Players must reach [[Dan (SF2)|Dan]] 9 before they can challenge her. Drakaina wields a pair of huge, menacing hook swords that look like oversized [[Shuang Gou]] as her weapons and a type of throwing axes as her ranged weapons, which she is capable of throwing three at once. These three axes travel in different directions and are impossible to be dodged at a certain range. Fights with her carry random rules.

The players can obtain the keys to fight her by defeating [[Karcer]]. If the players do not have any Drakaina keys, they can buy one key for 32 gems.

In battle, Drakaina is very aggressive. She is a deadly adversary who has dangerous attacks that can easily take away the player's health. Drakaina is immune to the Stun [[Enchantments|enchantment]].

## Story\[]

Amidst a blood-red wasteland overshadowed by a looming, monolithic skull, the war-hungry Eternal known as Drakaina enthusiastically confronted Shadow, reveling in the atmosphere and declaring it a lovely day for the approaching storm of battle. Sensing the untold fury of marching legions, the crimson warrior sadistically proclaimed that all combatants were merely gifts meant for her bloody altar. Questioning if the thrill of violent combat was entirely foreign to the mortal, Drakaina demanded that he demonstrate his fire, passion, and tempestuous power, issuing a stark ultimatum to either entertain her in single combat or perish. However, Shadow met her tempestuous bloodlust with cold, calculated precision, decisively striking down the battle-crazed Eternal in the ensuing clash. Stunned by her defeat and his seemingly emotionless execution, a frustrated Drakaina branded the victorious warrior an imposter, demanding to know how he could possibly fight so effectively without tapping into unbridled fury. Refusing to accept the end of their clash, the fallen warrior desperately pleaded for another dance of blades, insisting with absolute certainty that the true spirit of war lived deep within his soul.

## Special Ability\[]

- **Eternal Fury**

![[assets/DrakainaWarStorm.gif|DrakainaWarStorm]]

![[assets/DrakainaWarStormDodge.gif|Drakaina's ability can be stopped by non-melee attacks.]] ![[assets/DrakainaWarStormDodge.gif|Drakaina's ability can be stopped by non-melee attacks.]]

![[assets/DrakainaWarStormDodge.gif]]

Drakaina's ability can be stopped by non-melee attacks.

Drakaina emits a fiery gust of red tornado around her, then levitates and telekinetically spins her swords horizontally around her. She then charges forward at high speed while spinning horizontally with the tornado swirling around her. The player will take 55 damage if they come in contact with the flying swords. Drakaina herself will become immune to melee attacks when inside the tornado. The players can interrupt it by throwing any ranged weapon, using magic or Charge of Darkness while she is spinning the tornado around her or they can avoid it by staying away from the tornado.

## Trivia\[]

![[assets/DrakainaSwords.gif|DrakainaSwords]]

- Drakaina's name is from the archaic Greek word **δράκαινα** (drákaina), which means "she-dragon/female dragon".
- Drakaina’s furious passion for war and battles may mean that she represents fights, war, and anger.
- The War Shuang Gou that Drakaina wields possibly has longest reach out of all swords-type weapons in the game, surpassing [[Ceremonial Shuang Gou]] and [[Moon Sabers]] with its length.
- Drakaina is one of the only three enemies that uses an unobtainable ranged weapon, the others being [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]] and [[Vortex]].
- On the left side of the battlefield, a [[Skullgrinder]], which is a weapon from [[Shadow Fight 3]] can be spotted.
