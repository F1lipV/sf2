**Fungus** is the third Eternal encountered in [[Underworld]], the multiplayer mode of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is one of the four bosses encountered in the first floor along with [[Volcano]], [[Megalith]], and [[Vortex]]. He has a shield of 10,095 points which has to be destroyed in 12 minutes and 40 seconds by six (also 6 in Power Mode) players before they can finish him. He wields [[Two-handed Cudgel]] as his weapon.

The players can obtain the keys to fight him by defeating Megalith or from different raid rewards (season end, quests, etc.). If the players do not have any Fungus keys, they can buy one key for 15 [[Gem|gems]].

Compared to other Eternals, Fungus' attacks are slower. But, his attack range is fairly high and he can easily deal high damage with his attacks. Like every raid bosses, he is immune to the Stun enchantment.

## Story\[]

Deep within a decaying, bioluminescent grotto, the grotesque Eternal known as Fungus confronted Shadow, accusing the warrior of poisoning the land and causing his precious mushrooms to wither and die. The monstrous entity declared a grim fate for the human, threatening that his fungal spores would absorb Shadow's mind until they became one, effectively bringing a permanent end to the mortal's story. However, Shadow proved too formidable a foe, overcoming the toxic adversary in the ensuing battle and forcing the creature to yield. Defeated but still fiercely defiant, Fungus scoffed at the weakness of his fellow Eternals, claiming that his brethren had forgotten themselves by playing at being gods and that receiving mortal worship had only made them soft. With a final, bitter condemnation of his conqueror, the fallen entity asserted that destruction was fundamentally ingrained in humanity's nature, maintaining his resolute belief that the world would ultimately be much better off without them.

The possible rules during the fights with Fungus in Power Mode are:

- You lose health in shown area + No blocks
- You lose health in shown area + No double strikes

## Special Ability\[]

- **Earthquake**

Fungus can stomp the ground to cause a devastating quake that can damage much if players are on the ground, causing them to take 40 damage and fall. It is counted as a magic attack and therefore does not deal Head hit or Critical damage.

## Perk and Enchantments (Power Mode only)\[]

- **Shielding**![[assets/EnchantmentShielding.png|EnchantmentShielding]]

A chance to reduce incoming damage by 75% for 7 seconds after receiving a Hit or Head Hit.

## Trivia\[]

- As it can be inferred from his name, Fungus represents the various types of fungi that grow.
- After [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]], Fungus is the second boss to use the Earthquake perk.
- Fungus has a set and mystical enchantment based on him, the Spore Soul and Cordyceps.
