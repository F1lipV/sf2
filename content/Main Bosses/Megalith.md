**Megalith** is the second Eternal encountered in [[Underworld]], the multiplayer mode of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. He is one of the four bosses of the first floor (along with [[Volcano]], [[Fungus]], and [[Vortex]]). He has a shield of 5,121 points which needs to be destroyed within 10 minutes and 50 seconds by six (four in Power Mode) players before they can finish him. He wields [[Coral Prickles]] as his weapon.

The players can obtain the keys to fight him by defeating Volcano. Some daily quests also can reward the players with Megalith keys upon completed. If the players do not have any Megalith keys, they can buy one for 10 [[Gem|gems]].

Megalith is the only Eternal that does not have any [[Perk Tree|perks]] or [[Enchantments|enchantments]]. But, this is easily compensated by his extremely fast moves and aggressive attacks as compared to standard enemies. He is immune to the Stun enchantment.

## Story\[]

Within the crystalline depths of a sacred mine, the ancient earth elemental known as Megalith intercepted Shadow, demanding to know why the mortal dared to trespass in his domain. The towering, rock-armored giant commanded the warrior to leave and meet his destiny with honor, ominously promising that if he stayed, the very last sound he would ever hear would be the earth trembling beneath his feet. Despite the crushing weight of the eternal being's threats, Shadow stood his ground and ultimately shattered the titan's defenses, forcing the behemoth into submission. Brought low by the decisive defeat, a furious Megalith cursed his damnation, agonizing over the bitter prospect of having to wait for thousands of years in slumber once again. The fallen Eternal fiercely recounted his ancient, godlike might, boasting that he had once moved entire continents, and deeply lamented that his divine, now-unfulfilled role had been to completely cleanse the earth.

## Possible Rules (Power Mode)\[]

The possible rules during the fights with Megalith in Power Mode are:

- No double strikes + Inverted movement
- No double strikes + Hidden healthbar
- No double strikes + No jumps

## Perk and Enchantments (Power Mode only)\[]

- **Crag ![[assets/Potion_rock.png|Potion rock]]**

Critical hits cannot knock Megalith off.

- **Stun ![[assets/EnchantmentStun.png|EnchantmentStun]]**

A chance to stun the player for 3 seconds after a successful hit.

- **Damage Absorption ![[assets/EnchantmentBodyProtection.png|EnchantmentBodyProtection]]**

A chance to absorb all incoming body hit by a single strike.

- _Shocking Strike ![[assets/EnchantmentShock.png|EnchantmentShock]]_

Chance to cause a Shock with a Head Hit is increased significantly.

## Trivia\[]

- Megalith is responsible for the movement of the Earth’s continents and tectonic plates, as well as being responsible for causing earthquakes.
- He is the only raid boss who does not use any perks (excluding Power Mode) and the first boss who does not have any special ability during raid. But this however, makes him more aggressive and deadly than other bosses.
