**Gatekeeper** is the first Boss of the Immortal's World encountered in [[Underworld]] Tier 4, along with [[Saturn]], [[The Blackness]] and [[Stalker]]. Fight with Gatekeeper requires 4 players and carries random rules.

Gatekeeper has no legs, so it hovers using a thruster underneath. Therefore, Gatekeeper is unable to perform jumps, and any attacks that land downwards cannot deal any damage to Gatekeeper. The player also cannot throw Gatekeeper. In contrast, Gatekeeper can throw the player.

To enter Gatekeeper raid, the player requires one Gatekeeper key, which is obtainable by defeating [[Tenebris]] or can be purchased using 35 gems. The key is also obtainable from various packs, chests or offers.

## Story\[]

Having made the fateful decision to brave the cosmic unknown, Shadow plunged into the terrifying abyss of Tenebris's portal, eventually emerging alongside his companions in a stark, technologically advanced new dimension. However, their arrival immediately triggered the defenses of the rickety gateway they stepped out of. An erratic robotic sentry known as the Gatekeeper intercepted them, frantically babbling about the "strange air" and repetitively declaring its intense unhappiness before launching a programmed assault. After Shadow swiftly dismantled the malfunctioning automaton in combat, a mysterious man named Simon stepped forward. Apologizing for his bot's cold welcome, Simon explained that he had expected catastrophic danger to emerge from the other side of the portal, not a human hero. Intrigued by the travelers, Simon observed that Shadow's arrival had inadvertently strengthened an oppressive local structure called the "Obelisk" and mused aloud that the warrior's face was strangely familiar. When an exasperated May demanded to know if this immediate new trouble was just a sick joke, Simon’s demeanor grew grave. He assured them it was no laughing matter, revealing that despite surviving their journey through the portal, they were now facing the absolute end of the world, and he urged the weary companions to follow him to learn the grim truth.

## Special Abilities\[]

- **Powerfield**

![[assets/GatekeeperPowerfield.gif|GatekeeperPowerfield]]

Gatekeeper can unleash a powerfield around itself, electrocuting the player and dealing 50% damage on hit. The player can evade this ability by keeping a distance from Gatekeeper. The player also can interrupt it.

- **Kinetic Defense**

After getting hit from ranged weapon, Gatekeeper activates a kinetic shield that can wipe out any ranged attacks. The shield lasts for 5 seconds.

## Trivia\[]

- Gatekeeper is the first non-event raid boss that is not an Eternal.
- The choice given after defeating Tenebris directly impacts gameplay. Choosing the alternate "Turn Back" ending prevents the player from fighting the Gatekeeper or any other Tier 4 bosses until Tenebris is defeated again and the choice to enter the portal is made.
- Initially, Gatekeeper is fought alone or single player.
- The Gatekeeper has no head so it cannot take head hit.
- Gatekeeper is one of the characters whose names in their dialogues are not fully capitalized. The others being Freeze Jr. and Simon. This is most likely a developer oversight, and does not apply to his name in the raid menu or during the fight.
- Gatekeeper is the only raid boss who cannot be thrown or suplexed by the player, but only he can do so.
