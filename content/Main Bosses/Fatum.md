**Fatum** is the 5th Eternal in the [[Underworld]], and the first boss of the tier 2 of Underworld. She is one of the three bosses in the second tier (along with [[Arkhos]] and [[Hoaxen]]). Fatum has a shield of 8,473 points which must be destroyed in 8 minutes and 40 seconds by 6 players before they can finish her. The players need to reach [[Dan (SF2)|Dan]] 4 before they can defeat her. Battles with her carry 2 random rules. She wields the [[Grim Scythe]] as her weapon.

The players can obtain the keys to fight her by defeating [[Vortex]]. If the player's Dan is 7, Fatum keys will be given at the end of a season by reaching the 1st-5th place in the League. If the players do not have any Fatum keys, they can buy one key for 23 [[Gem|gems]].

Compared to other Eternals, Fatum's attacks are slower. But she has a very wide attack range and she can deal enormous damage with ease. She is immune to the [[Enchantments#Stun|Stun]] enchantment.

After she is defeated, Fatum mentions the one who rules over the Eternals, saying that the Maker is testing human's kin and wanting a decent rival.

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What is the significance of Fatum in the Underworld tier 2 in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2's Underworld mode, Fatum is a significant boss in the second tier. She is one of three bosses, with the others being Arkhos and Hoaxen. Fatum possesses a shield with 8,471 points that must be destroyed within a time limit of 8 minutes and 30 seconds. Players are required to attain Dan 4 level to challenge her. Fatum's weapon of choice is the Grim Scythe, and the keys to combat her are acquired by defeating Vortex.

Provided by: Fandom

**What is the unique feature of Fatum's shield in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, Fatum's shield possesses a unique characteristic. It fully recharges after any attack, irrespective of whether it hits the player or not. The player can only inflict damage once the shield is entirely depleted. If Fatum avoids being hit for a duration of 5 seconds, the shield restores to its full capacity.

Provided by: Fandom

**What weapon does Fatum wield in her battles?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In the game, Fatum, an Eternal and boss character, employs the Grim Scythe as her main weapon. Additionally, she utilizes triangular-bladed knives and Throwing Daggers for ranged attacks.

Provided by: Fandom

**What are the prerequisites for players to defeat Fatum?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

To challenge Fatum, players must attain Dan 4. A team of six is required to destroy Fatum's 8,471-point shield within 8 minutes and 30 seconds. Keys to fight Fatum are obtained by defeating Vortex or by reaching 1st-5th place in the League at Dan 7. A Fatum key can also be bought for 60 gems. Fatum's slower attacks, wide attack range, and immunity to the Stun enchantment should be noted.

Provided by: Fandom

**How can players obtain the keys to fight Fatum in Shadow Fight 2?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In Shadow Fight 2, keys are necessary to challenge Fatum. Players can secure these keys by triumphing over previous bosses in the Underworld. They can also buy keys with gems or find them in a Mystery Box. Note that keys reset when a season concludes.

Provided by: Fandom

## Story\[]

Amidst the eerie, mist-shrouded ruins, the ethereal weaver of destinies known as Fatum confronted Shadow, mockingly declaring that his mortal courage was matched only by his folly. Anticipating his arrival, the spectral entity proclaimed that they were each other’s inevitable fate and vowed to permanently rewrite his destiny. With a taunting sneer, she commanded him to keep his companions in check and boasted absolute omniscience over his true identity and future path, cruelly teasing whether she should reveal who would ultimately perish at the end of his journey. Defying her deterministic threats, Shadow engaged the phantom in combat and shattered her ominous prophecies by decisively striking her down. Unfazed by her defeat, a cynical Fatum reflected on her profound knowledge of mankind, asserting that humanity was forever doomed to walk the same inescapable spiral of self-destruction and withering. Yielding to a higher cosmic power, she ominously revealed that a supreme entity known as the Maker was far more skilled at weaving the threads of fate than she was, and that he was actively testing humanity in search of a worthy rival, finally dismissing the victorious warrior to go forth and suffer the Maker's inescapable ire.

## Trivia\[]

- Fatum’s name may be based on the word ‘fate’, hence she may represent fate and potentially death.
  - Her appearance and weapon of choice mimic that of the Grim Reaper.
- She is the third boss to use Invisibility as a perk after [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]] and [[May (SF2)|May]].
- Fatum is the creator of the [[Equipment Sets (SF2)#Fatum's Artifact|Valentine artifacts]].
- She is the only Underworld boss that does not wear a mask in the Halloween 2017 update.
- She is the first raid boss from whom fights with random rules start being applied.
