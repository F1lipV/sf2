**Stalker** is the final Boss of the Immortal's World encountered in [[Underworld]] Tier 4, along with [[Gatekeeper]], [[Saturn]], and [[The Blackness]]. Fights with Stalker requires 4 players and carries random rules.

To enter Stalker raids, the players require one Stalker key, which is obtainable by defeating The Blackness or purchasing it with 40 gems.

## Story\[]

Rushing to the city's Concentration Hall to stop Lazarus from unleashing absolute carnage, the group was instead met with an eerie, ominous silence. In the center of the bizarre facility stood a massive crystal—the very heart of the Obelisk, serving as a dark accumulator for shadow energy. When May noticed a figure trapped inside the crystal, a dread-filled Simon realized that Sensei's worst fears about their situation were entirely correct. Before they could investigate further, a mutated, hostile creature known as the Stalker intercepted them. Drawn to the immense power radiating from Shadow, the monstrous guardian accused the hero of returning to "finish what he started" and violently attacked him. Shadow engaged the frenzied beast and successfully defeated it. With its dying breath, the Stalker delivered a desperate, cryptic warning: it urged the "Shadow-human" not to let the darkness spread, claiming he would be deceived and end up "like him," though a chance for salvation remained. Upon finally inspecting the crystal's imprisoned victim, Simon made a shocking discovery. Recognizing the Imperial ring on the trapped figure's hand, the group realized they were looking at the overthrown Patriarch himself. A horrified May immediately assumed Lazarus was responsible for the coup, deeply regretting their decision to free his spirit in the marshes. However, Simon dismissed this theory, noting that a son would never raise his hand against his father and that the true conspiracy was far more complicated. Suddenly, the environment around Shadow began to warp. As Simon tried to check on the visibly unwell warrior, the scientist's voice began to heavily glitch and distort, fading entirely from Shadow's perception. Plunged into a sudden mental isolation, a mysterious, glowing silhouette materialized within Shadow's mind. The enigmatic entity chillingly greeted him as "brother," celebrating that they could finally speak in private and setting the stage for a massive new mystery.

## Special Ability\[]

- **Insect Wings**

![[assets/Stalkerability.gif|Stalkerability]] ![[assets/Stalkerability.gif|Stalkerability]]

![[assets/Stalkerability.gif]]

Stalker can jump onto either wall during the fight, sprout wings and launch off of it, fly to the other side of the arena while impaling the player with his claws. With perfect timing, the attack can be jumped over or alternatively, players can either duck or roll forward/backward to avoid the attack.

While in flight, the Stalker is invulnerable. However, he can still be attacked by Charges of Darkness. The attack dealt during his flight can still deal a Critical Hit and Head Hit to the player.

Stalker cannot use this ability if the player is in between him and the wall or if he is too far from the wall, both of which can be taken advantage of by the player.

## Perks and Enchantments\[]

- **Crag**![[assets/Potion_rock.png|Potion rock]]

Critical hits cannot knock Stalker off.

- **Cobra** ![[assets/IconCobra.png|IconCobra]]

A chance that during first 3 seconds of a round, Stalker's first strike would reduce players' health by 25%.

- **Block Breaker** ![[assets/IconBlockBreaker.png|IconBlockBreaker]]

A chance for Stalker to bypass the players' blocks.

- **Mirror** ![[assets/IconMirror.png|IconMirror]]

Blocking a player's hit gives Stalker a 30% chance to inflict 85% more damage for 2 seconds.

- **Desperate** ![[assets/IconDesperate.png|IconDesperate]]

Stalker's damage is increased once his total HP drops below 20%. This perk remains on during the finishing fight even if his health is full.

- **Poisoning**![[assets/EnchantmentIntoxication.png|EnchantmentIntoxication]]

A chance that the player will start to lose 20% health per second over 5 seconds after being hit by his weapon. This chance is proportional to the damage dealt.

- **Magic Recharge**![[assets/EnchantmentMagicRecharge.png|EnchantmentMagicRecharge]]

A chance to increase Stalker's magic recharge after receiving a hit by 300%.

## Trivia\[]

- Stalker's raid OST is a slightly remixed version of Shurale's raid OST from 2022 Christmas and New Year event.
- Stalker's Wings ability is similar to a main story boss [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]]'s flying ability.
  - He is one of the few underworld bosses who have some of the main storyline bosses' abilities, such as [[Volcano]], [[Fungus]] and [[Fatum]].
- The Poisoning enchantment applied to his weapon depletes 20% health per second, whereas a normal Poisoning enchantment would only deplete 3% of health. Due to the strength of his weapon's enchantment, getting inflicted with it will certainly result in the player's defeat.
- Stalker is the only enemy who can attack the player with weapon while they are using small Charges of Darkness and medium Charges of Darkness.
- Stalker currently has the most shield points out of all raid bosses in the Underworld, outside of Power Mode.
- The identity of the glowing silhouette that appears in Shadow's mind at the end of Tier 4 remains one of the biggest unsolved mysteries in Shadow Fight 2. Because the voice addresses Shadow as "brother" and is accompanied by reality-warping effects, it is heavily speculated by the community to be an early manifestation of the Shadow Mind from [[Shadow Fight 3]], acknowledging Shadow as a fellow being of pure shadow energy.
