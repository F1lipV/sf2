**Tenebris** (also referred to as **The Creator**, **The Maker**, or **The Father** by the other Eternals) is the ruler of the [[Underworld]]. He is one of the three bosses in the third tier (along with [[Karcer]] and [[Drakaina]]), and is the last boss of tier 3.

Tenebris has a shield of 14,377 points, which has to be destroyed in 7 minutes and 30 seconds by six players before they can finish him. Players must reach [[Dan (SF2)|Dan]] 10 before they can challenge Tenebris. He wields a pair of long claws as his weapons and fights with him carry random rules.

The players can obtain the keys to fight him by defeating Drakaina. If the players do not have any Tenebris keys, they can buy one key for 35 [[Gem|gems]].

In battle, Tenebris is extremely aggressive. He fights mercilessly with many dangerous attacks and he also has a very powerful defense power. He is immune to the [[Enchantments#Medium Recipes|Stun]] enchantment, just like all other [[Underworld|Raid bosses]] and [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]].

### Quick Answers

![[assets/8941553e-df6a-4310-a080-b9da56fdcbdd.png]]

**What role does Tenebris play as a boss in the third tier?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

In the third tier of the Underworld in Shadow Fight, Tenebris serves as a formidable boss. He reigns as the Underworld's ruler and is recognized as the most potent deity, earning titles like Maker, Creator, and Father. Tenebris possesses a 17,377-point shield, which a six-player team must dismantle within a 6-minute timeframe. Players are required to reach the Dan 10 level to challenge Tenebris.

**What is the significance of the portal behind Tenebris?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

The portal behind Tenebris holds immense power, with the ability to create worlds and shape reality. Tenebris asserts that he used this portal to forge the world's light and darkness. It offers a possible salvation for humanity from the Eternals, but also threatens a potential disaster. The portal's destination is 'Terra Incognito', an uncharted territory. The narrative's conclusion hinges on the player's decision to enter the portal or retreat.

**What does Tenebris claim to have created in the world?**
![[assets/835ea5d8-5dd9-4dc6-83dc-8554b30552d7.png]]

Tenebris, also known as Maker, Creator, and Father, asserts that he created the world and its elements of light and darkness. He is also credited with the creation of the Eternals, entities with the power to unleash natural disasters. Tenebris has the capability to annihilate humanity and has expressed intentions to cleanse the world from shadow contagion. He is linked to a portal with the capacity to create worlds, which could either be humanity's salvation or cause its destruction.

## Story\[]

[[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] finally reaches the heart of the Underworld, where Tenebris awaits his arrival. Behind him is the portal where nothingness resides, in which worlds can be created. Tenebris says that it was he who forged the light and darkness of the world. [[Sensei (SF2)|Sensei]] realizes that the portal can probably save humanity from the Eternals. Tenebris replies by saying that they, humans, are only capable of destroying, and the portal will destroy them. Sensei tells Shadow that they may stop the Eternals by entering the portal, or they may cause a great catastrophe by doing so. It is a risk that has to be taken, as they have no other choice.

Shadow then challenges Tenebris to prove humanity's worth. Tenebris displays a fearsome demonstration of his might throughout the battle. This, combined with the harsh anomalies on the battlefield, makes this battle very challenging. Despite all that, Shadow emerges victorious against Tenebris at the end.

Tenebris warns them for the last time of the consequences of entering the portal. Shadow now has two choices that he must choose from:

- **Turn Back** - Shadow turns away from the portal. The portal then reacts and grants its power to Shadow and his companions, the power to form and shape reality in accordance with their will. Tenebris says that together with the Eternals, they can now decide what to do with this chaotic world. They can destroy it just like what the Eternals had done many times. Or they can opt to live in peace instead, something the Eternals could never do. Shadow now has the power to stop the cycle of destruction.

- **Enter** - Shadow enters the portal. Once inside, Shadow starts getting swallowed by the immense force of the portal. He also hears voices inside his head, telling him that these voices are just his imagination and to not give up. After Shadow survives this ordeal, he regains his consciousness and sees that the Underworld has been affected greatly - the Eternals have been eradicated. The portal switched humanity's place with the Eternals'. The judgement that was meant for humans has fall upon the Eternals instead. Even though the Eternals will be reborn one day, just like all life forms, it cannot be denied that their demise was directly caused by humans. With the new power from the portal, Shadow and humanity now have the Eternals' fate at their mercy. As Tenebris states, Shadow can choose to either let the Eternals live or die.

## Powers and Abilities\[]

Tenebris is an extremely powerful being who rules the Underworld, he is the most powerful eternal in the Underworld. He claims to have created the world, along with its light and darkness, hence him being referred as Maker, Creator, and Father by the other Eternals who respect and fear Tenebris. The way [[Arkhos]] and [[Karcer]] speak about him implies that Tenebris created the Eternals, the beings that had lived for thousands of years and are capable of making natural disasters to wipe out humanity. Tenebris himself is also more than capable in erasing humanity with ease, as [[Vortex]] believes humans have no way to avoid their judgement once Tenebris has decided their death, and the only choice they have is to choose how they will die. [[Fatum]] also emphasizes this point by stating that Tenebris is much more skillful than her in weaving the threads of fate, and he is merely testing human kind as he wants a decent rival.

## Personality\[]

Tenebris has his own moral compass. He is not good, but he is not evil either. He has been described as merciless and does not have any desire to understand and tolerate humans at all.

## Special Ability\[]

- **Beam of Fear**

![[assets/TenebrisFearBeam.gif|TenebrisFearBeam]]

![[assets/TenebrisFearBeamDodge.gif|Tenebris' beam can be interrupted or dodged.]] ![[assets/TenebrisFearBeamDodge.gif|Tenebris' beam can be interrupted or dodged.]]

![[assets/TenebrisFearBeamDodge.gif]]

Tenebris' beam can be interrupted or dodged.

Charges for a moment, and then unleashes a devastating purple beam from his body, dealing 45 damage and sending the player flying from the impact. Tenebris will become immune to melee attack when using this ability. The player can dodge this attack by a well-timed jump or by rolling. They also can interrupt this attack by throwing any ranged weapon, magic or Charge at Tenebris. It is counted as a magic attack, therefore it does not deal Head hit or Critical.

## Trivia\[]

- Tenebris utilizes the Shocking Strike enchantment, an enchantment unavailable to players. Banshee, [[Gatekeeper]], and Power Mode [[Megalith]] are the only other raid-bosses who use this enchantment.
- The helms obtained after the player makes a choice represents the outcome of both choices: "Crown of Harmony" for the peaceful ending of "Turn Back" and "Crown of Chaos" for the destructive outcome of "Enter".
- Tenebris means "darkness" in Latin.
- Tenebris tells the player that the portal leads to "Terra Incognito", which means 'unknown land' in Latin.
- The runes written on the sprite of Tenebris' Key, read as **ᚠᚨᛉᚱ ᚹᛉᛁᛇ ᛉᛇᛚᚨᚱ**. The words themselves are just gibberish.
