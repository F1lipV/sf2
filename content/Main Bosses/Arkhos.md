**Arkhos** is the sixth Eternal and the second boss encountered in [[Underworld]] Tier 2. He is one of the three bosses of the second floor (Along with [[Fatum]] and [[Hoaxen]]). Arkhos has a shield of 8,839 points which has to be destroyed in 8 minutes and 10 seconds by six players before they can finish him. The players must reach [[Dan (SF2)|Dan 5]] before they can challenge him. He wields [[The Sting]] as his weapon and fights with him carry random rules.

The players can obtain the keys to fight him by defeating Fatum. If the players do not have any Arkhos keys, they can buy one key for 23 [[Gem|gems]].

In battle, Arkhos is very aggressive. His speed is very high and his attacks can reach a considerable range. He can easily take away players' health with his attacks. Arkhos is immune to the [[Enchantments#Medium Recipes|Stun]] enchantment.

After the fight with him, Arkhos mentions the one that rules over the Eternals. If the player loses, he threatens the player by saying that he will report their failure to the Creator. After he is defeated, he asks why the Creator has forsaken him, for he has failed him again.

## Story\[]

Before a looming temple swarming with zealous followers, the fanatical cult leader known as Arkhos confronted Shadow, addressing his congregation as blind fanatics who remembered him and fed him with their unwavering faith in the Father. He ominously declared that a parent could be strict, expecting them to accept his punishment with absolute gratitude. Turning his twisted attention to the mortal challenger, the rat-like sorcerer mocked Shadow as an "old man," threatening that he had hard iron prepared for refractory children. He arrogantly predicted that the warrior would soon be begging to join their dark family, though Arkhos promised to remain entirely implacable to his pleas. However, defying the zealous leader's grim promises, Shadow engaged Arkhos in combat and struck the fanatic down in decisive defeat. Brought to his knees in his final hour, a despairing Arkhos agonized over who he should worship now, crying out to his Creator and questioning why he had been so completely forsaken. With his dying breaths, the fallen leader lamented that although he had been granted the divine power to lead his flock, he had ultimately failed his dark master once again.

## Special Ability\[]

- **Rat Wave**

![[assets/ArkhosRatWave.gif|ArkhosRatWave]] ![[assets/ArkhosRatWave.gif|ArkhosRatWave]]

![[assets/ArkhosRatWave.gif]]

Arkhos has the ability to summon a wave of rats that travel the ground to damage the player. The wave deals 45% damage, bleeding effect and it works similar to [[Dark Implosion]] magic. The players can avoid the wave by jumping, or they can prevent Arkhos from using it by either attacking him while he is trying to summon the wave or staying close to him. It is counted as a magic attack, therefore it does not deal Head hit or Critical damage.

## Trivia\[]

- Arkhos may represent trickery, lies and deceit, but given his appearance of a rat and usage of the Toxic Cloud magic, he may also represent disease and plague.
- Arkhos' Rat Wave Ability is the first Underworld special ability encountered in the [[Underworld]]
- Arkhos is based on [Skaven](https://warhammerfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Skaven), a kind of humanoid rats race from Warhammer.
- Arkhos is one of the Underworld bosses who have enchantments on their equipment. (The other ones are [[Vortex]], [[Fatum]], [[Hoaxen]], [[Karcer]], [[Drakaina]], and [[Tenebris]]).
