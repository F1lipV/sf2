**Karcer** is the eighth Eternal of the [[Underworld]]. He is one of the three bosses of Tier 3 (along with [[Drakaina]] and [[Tenebris]]). Karcer has a shield of 10,472 points, which has to be destroyed in 5 minutes by six players before they can finish him. Players must reach [[Dan (SF2)|Dan]] 8 before they can challenge him. He wields a pair of sickles as his weapons and fights with him carry random rules.

The players can obtain the keys to fight him by defeating [[Hoaxen]]. If the players do not have Karcer keys, they can buy one key for 29 [[Gem|gems]].

In battle, Karcer is very aggressive. He has extreme agility, and despite looking fragile, his body is able to withstand strong attacks. He is immune to the [[Enchantments#Stun|Stun]] enchantment.

After he is defeated, Karcer asks for liberation from the Creator. He sent Karcer many sustenance, yet at the same time giving him infinite hunger.

## Story\[]

Deep within a bone-littered, cavernous abyss, the grotesque and ravenous Eternal known as Karcer confronted Shadow, mocking the warrior's long journey and questioning if he had foolishly arrived to save ungrateful mortals who cared nothing for him. Relishing the psychological torment, the monstrous entity claimed he could intimately sense the human's deep-seated loneliness, hunger, and lingering fear. Gesturing to the unseen presence of his dark brethren, Karcer noted that while the other Eternals thirsted merely for cruel entertainment, their shallow cravings were absolutely nothing compared to his own insatiable hunger. However, instead of becoming the beast's next meal, Shadow engaged the grotesque entity and decisively struck him down in combat. Broken, defeated, and denied his feast, a desperate Karcer cried out to his dark Creator in his final moments. The fallen monster lamented that while the mortal had been sent to him as sustenance, it had ultimately proven insufficient, and he desperately pleaded with the divine for deliverance from his agonizing, eternal hunger.

## Special Ability\[]

- **Hunger Fossils**

![[assets/KarcerHungerClaws.gif|KarcerHungerClaws]]

![[assets/KarcerHungerClawsDodge.gif|Karcer's Hunger Fossils can be interrupted or dodged.]] ![[assets/KarcerHungerClawsDodge.gif|Karcer's Hunger Fossils can be interrupted or dodged.]]

![[assets/KarcerHungerClawsDodge.gif]]

Karcer's Hunger Fossils can be interrupted or dodged.

Performs a long leap forward, and then smashes the ground with his scythes. This will cause a number of bone-like spikes to emerge from below the player, surrounding them and dealing 45 damage to them. Karcer will become immune to melee attacks when leaping forward. The players can dodge this attack by a well-timed jump, rolling forward, or long-step forward. They also can interrupt this attack by throwing any ranged weapon, magic or Charge at Karcer before he reaches the ground. It is counted as a magic attack, therefore it does not deal Head hit. However, it still can deal Critical hit.

## Trivia\[]

![[assets/KarcerSuperSlash.gif|Karcer demonstrating his rapid fighting style.]] ![[assets/KarcerSuperSlash.gif|Karcer demonstrating his rapid fighting style.]]

![[assets/KarcerSuperSlash.gif]]

Karcer demonstrating his rapid fighting style.

- Karcer’s emaciated appearance and constant mention of hunger may mean he represents hunger and famine.
- His name is derived from the Latin word **carcer**, which means "prison" or "jail cell".
- Karcer wields Hunger Sickles, which have the appearance of oversized [[Sickles]] but with functions same to [[Mowers]]. Due to its size, it has a greater reach distance than both of the aforementioned weapons.
- He is the first raid-boss to use an unavailable weapon; other raid-bosses use gem weapons obtainable from the [[Shop (SF2)|shop]], and the special event bosses wield special weapons obtainable during their respective events.
- Although Karcer's Hunger Fossils ability is mechanically a magic attack, it may still deal a Critical hit. The only other magic in the game that is capable of dealing a Critical hit is [[Quantum Leap]].
- Despite having the animation of sharp bones protruding from the ground beneath the players, Hunger Fossils mechanics are identical to the [[Toxic Cloud]] magic.
- Karcer was the special raid-boss of the 2019 New Year update, where players only need Dan 2 to fight him as opposed to 8. They will receive one key to fight Karcer as a New Year gift, along with Healing Vine and Phoenix Elixirs. Defeating him and reaching first place may reward the player with an item from the [[Equipment Sets (SF2)#New Year 2018 Set|New Year 2018 Set]].
  - Due to a bug on some devices in this 2019 New Year update (version 1.9.38), Karcer had no weapons and his Hunger Fossils ability. He was also depicted with body and head like [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]], when he has no armor and helm equipped. Karcer only retained his magic and ranged weapon, though only his weapon would lose its enchantment. Additionally, some players did not receive the New Year gift as well. This bug has been fixed after the holidays were over.
