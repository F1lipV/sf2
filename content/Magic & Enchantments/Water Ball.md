**Water Ball** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 31, in [[Act VI Iron Reign|Act VI: Iron Reign]]. Water Ball is used by [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]] (after losing twice and at [[Gates of Shadows]] and [[Interlude]]), and by some [[Fighters|fighters]] and [[Ninja|ninjas]]. It is also used by the Eternal [[Vortex]].

## Description\[]

The user conjures a water ball with both hands, while pulling both hands, then leans forward and launches the water at the enemy. The water ball will travel horizontally across the battlefield. When the water ball hits, the enemy is knocked backwards and receives 45 damage.

This magic has a larger hit box than other horizontally thrown magic and can hit the player even if he rolls. In this case, the magic tends to hit in the middle of the roll, when the player's feet are up in the air.
