**Major Arcana** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available during the Halloween 2024/Shadow of Eternity event via an in-game offer.

## Description\[]

The user summons three magical Tarot cards and shoots it at the opponent, dealing 25 base damage per card.

![[assets/Majorarcanamagic.gif|Majorarcanamagic]]

## Trivia\[]

- This magic is one of the only two magics in the game that have the special ability to deal head hits and critical hits, the other being [[Quantum Leap]].
