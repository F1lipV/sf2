**Roaring Caldera** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the magic amulet of the Abyss Mark Set, which comes with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments#Mythical Recipes|Roaring Luminary]].

This magic could be acquired by chance by opening the Ritual Chest during the Ritual Battles Event. Opening a chest costed 600 event coins.

## Description\[]

The user raises their hands and commands a volcano to emerge from the ground, which emits a lava ball upwards into the sky which then falls down and hits the opponent. The eruption deals 15 damage, the lava ball deals 15 damage and the ground explosion deals 35 damage on impact.

## Abyss Mark Set\[]

Read More [[Equipment Sets (SF2)]]

All the items of the Abyss Mark Set bring **Roaring Luminary** mythical enchantment. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock the enchantment. Once all the items of the set are collected, Roaring Luminary will be available, and the player can enchant other equipment with this enchantment.
