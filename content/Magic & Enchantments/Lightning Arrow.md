**Lightning Arrow** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the first magic in [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V: The Greatest Temptation]], unlocked at level 25. Lightning Arrow is used by [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]], [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]] (after losing one round), and by various [[Fighters|fighters]] and [[Ninja|ninjas]].

## Description\[]

The user pulls one hand while forming an electricity on the hand, then leans forward and launches the lightning at the enemy. The lightning travels horizontally across the battlefield. When the attack hits, the enemy is knocked backwards and receives 45 damage. The magic can be dodged by either rolling, ducking, or jumping.

## Trivia\[]

- Unlike other magic, which lose their full charge when being summoned, Lightning Arrow will lose its full charge when the magic is being propelled forward. This means it is possible for the users to still keep its full charge if their deployment is interrupted.
