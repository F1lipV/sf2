**Mind Throw** _(official name unknown)_ is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is one of the unobtainable magics in the game and is only used by [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]].

## Description\[]

Titan activates and throws a blue energy orb across the arena. If the player is hit by the orb, Titan then suspends the player in midair, chokes them and then slams them onto the ground. Mind Throw can be dodged both by rolling or ducking.
