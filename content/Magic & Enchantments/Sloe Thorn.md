**Sloe Thorn** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This weapon was obtainable through the Battle Pass during Halloween 2021 event, by purchasing Premium Pass to unlock VIP rewards.

## Description\[]

Character swings one hand forward while launching a dark air wave to the enemy. The wave travels horizontally at a high speed. It deals 45 damage when hit. The magic can be dodged by either rolling, ducking, or jumping. This attack animation is the same as [[Force Wave]].

![[assets/Crimson_Corruption_Set.jpg|Crimson Corruption Set]]

All the items of the Warlock Set bring **Crimson Corruption** mythical enchantment. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock the enchantment. Once all the items of the set are collected, Crimson Corruption will be available, and the player can enchant other equipment with this enchantment.
