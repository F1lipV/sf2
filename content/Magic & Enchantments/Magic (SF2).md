**Magic** is one of the five types of equipment available in [[Shadow Fight 2]], along with [[Weapon (SF2)|weapons]], [[Armor (SF2)|armor]], [[Helm (SF2)|helms]], and [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapons]]. It is unlocked after defeating [[Hermit (SF2)|Hermit]], in [[Act II Secret Path|Act II: Secret Path]].

Depending on the context and for clarity, the terms **magic amulet** and **magic/magic attacks** are used with distinction. This equipment's item form is known as **magic amulets**, and it can be purchased from the shop or obtained through events. Upon equipping a magic amulet, the player will be granted the ability to use **magic attacks**.

Before using magic attacks in a fight, the player must have the energy meter for magic to be fully charged first. The energy meter can be charged by performing weapon or unarmed attacks on the enemy, or by receiving hits from the enemy, although to a lesser extent. Attacks with higher "power" (which can be found on the in-game moves list) will charge up the meter at a faster rate. Players can also charge their magic faster with the help of the [[Enchantments#Simple Recipes|Magic Recharge]] enchantment and [[Perk Tree#List of Perks|Martial Spirit]] perk.

Magics cannot be blocked but may be dodged. Magic has higher damage than weapons, unarmed attacks or ranged attacks, and when upgraded to the same level as other attacks can easily deal more than triple the damage. Although this comes at the cost of requiring magic to be charged before each usage, and as well as it being easier to be dodged by enemies. Several magics have similar functions to each other (e.g.: Fireball and Star's Breath, Dark Implosion and Fire Splash) but there are some with completely distinct mechanics (e.g.: Asteroid, Torturer's Rage). Magics cannot deal Head Hits or Critical Hits, except [[Quantum Leap]] and Major Arcana.

## List of Magic Amulets\[]

There are two types of magic amulets available in the shop. Standard magic amulets can be bought with either [[Currencies (SF2)|coins]] or [[Gem|gems]], and can be upgraded to a higher level using coins. They do not have [[Enchantments|enchantments]]. Magic amulets that come with enchantments can only be bought with gems. Unlike weapons, armors, and helms, the gem-priced magic amulets can be upgraded to the next level using coins, as long as there is no new magic amulet unlocked in the shop. When bought, the level and enchantment power of a gem-priced magic amulet will automatically scale to the player's current level.

## Trivia\[]

![[assets/ShadowChoke.gif|Choke from .]] ![[assets/ShadowChoke.gif|Choke from .]]

![[assets/ShadowChoke.gif]]

Choke from [[Shadow Fight 3]].

- The Choke ability features a move similar to the Titan's Mind Throw. Choke is a [[Shadow Ability]] from [[Shadow Fight 3]], available on Legion Helms.
- In the 2016 Christmas Update, the [[Shard of Eternal Ice]] magic was added for the first time, which functioned exactly like the unobtainable Ice Ball magic. This made it possible for players to be able to use the magic that was previously inaccessible.
  - Shard of Eternal Ice can also be bought from the shop in [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]].
- Most of the gem-priced magic amulets are only enchanted with either Overheat or Weakness.
  - Fire Pillar and Torturer's Rage are the only shop magic amulets that are not enchanted with either Overheat or Weakness, and Torturer's Rage is also the only shop magic amulet that is enchanted with Medium Recipe.
- Magic can be blocked in the Special Edition.
- Almost all magic amulets deal the same damage, being 45 damage. Some magics deal less or higher than the aforementioned amount.
  - Asteroid consists of three attacks in which each attack has lower damage compared to other magic (15 damage for small asteroid, 30 damage for big asteroid and 30 damage for the ground explosion), but will result in higher overall damage if all attacks hit the enemy (75 damage in total).
  - Monk's Amulet, Death Grip and Shivering Earth deals the least damage out of all magics, dealing only 15 damage, but it is compensated by their capability to stun the enemy for a moment which makes them very vulnerable to other attacks.
  - Quantum Leap and Star's Breath deal 50 damage.
  - Inmate's Shackles deals 40 damage.
  - Highest Measure deals 60 damage, currently making it the magic with the highest amount of damage per hit in the game.
  - Torturer's Rage can deal damage twice if the user is very close to the enemy, with overall 90 damage. In special circumstances, it can even hit thrice, dealing 135 damage.
  - Major Arcana deals 25 damage per card, dealing 75 damage total.
  - Plasma Cutter deals 35 damage.
  - Requiem deals 10 damage on the initial hit before dealing damage over time to the enemy when they are within the aura.
  - Spores of Discord deals 20 damage per clouds, up to 4 clouds under normal circumstances, totaling up to 80 damage. A bug allows the magic to spawn even more clouds, further increasing Spores of Discord's damage.
- [[Quantum Leap]] and Major Arcana is the only magic in the game that can deal a head hit or critical hit.
