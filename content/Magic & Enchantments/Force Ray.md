**Force Ray** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 44 in Chapter 1 of [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Revelation]]. Force Ray is used by some [[Fighters|fighters]] in Act VII and by the Halloween 2016 and 2018 raid-boss, Morgana.

## Description\[]

The user performs a physical signal and shortly afterwards, a thick purple beam of energy descends from the sky, over the target. If it strikes successfully, it will deal 45 damage, and the enemy is left winded for a very brief duration and will be unable to move for this time. It can be easily dodged by moving away from its location.
