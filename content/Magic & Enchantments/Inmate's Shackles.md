**Inmate's Shackles** is the exclusive 10th Anniversary [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic is available exclusively from a set bundle (including [[Vulture's Feather]], Snow Blind, Lunatic' Eye, Harpy's Bite and **Inmate's Shackles**) offer for $14.99. Later on, it became available in the weekly offer for $2.99.

## Description\[]

The user punches the ground, which creates an earthquake that makes the opponent fall down and take 40 damage. The mechanic of this magic is similar to Butcher's earthquake ability.

![[assets/Inmates_shackles.gif|Inmates shackles]]

## Trivia\[]

- **Inmate's Shackles** was originally intended for a special mythical Revival set, and it was originally called "Revival Skyfist", making many people think that this magic summoned a giant hand from the sky that crushed the opponent or something (considering it works identical to Force Ray).
