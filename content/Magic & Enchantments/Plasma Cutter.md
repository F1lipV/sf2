**Plasma Cutter** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available in a weekly offer for real money.

## Description\[]

The user charges for a moment before unleashing a blue beam from their body, sending the enemy flying from the impact. This beam has two "sections", one up close and one from far away and each functioning as two separate hitboxes. The "close section" deals 35 base damage and the "far section" deals 25 damage. If used at the right distance, the user is able to land both of these sections, dealing a total of 60 base damage.

![[assets/Plasmacutteramagic.gif|Plasmacutteramagic]]

## Trivia\[]

- This magic is nearly identical to [[Tenebris]]'s Beam of Fear ability.
  - Unlike the Beam of Fear ability, this magic has twice the range of that ability. In order to do this, the magic is granted an additional, entirely separate hitbox beyond Beam of Fear's original hitbox.
- Plasma Cutter only deals 35/25 base damage in one hit, which is less than the average magic's 45 and it is one of the few magics in the game that has this property. The others being [[Monk's Amulet]] (and similar magics like [[Death Grip]] and [[Shivering Earth]]), each hit from [[Spores of Discord]] and the initial hit of [[Requiem (SF2)|Requiem]].
- The beam VFX coming from the user's body is the same VFX as Corsair's ability from [[Shadow Fight Shades|Shadow Fight: Shades]].
