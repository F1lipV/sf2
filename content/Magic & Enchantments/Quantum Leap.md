**Quantum Leap** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the magic amulet of the Neo-wanderer Set. Quantum Leap is obtainable only BY purchasing the Neo-wanderer Chest in the [[Underworld|raid shop]] for 399 gems or 8500 shards. Upon purchase, the players may receive the Quantum Leap or other Neo-wanderer item, along with some Charges of Darkness, elixirs or Mystical Coupon. Quantum Leap is enchanted with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments|Arcane Martial Art]].

## Description\[]

The user will perform a back kick to unleash a powerful Arcane burst from their leg, dealing 50 damage. This magic has very short range and requires perfect timing in order to land it.

## Trivia\[]

- Quantum Leap reuses the [[Mind Throw]] sprite as its sprite image.
- The shop preview of Quantum Leap will perform the Arcane special attack movement instead of its actual movement.
- Quantum Leap is the only available mythical set magic that can grant a head hit or a critical hit.
