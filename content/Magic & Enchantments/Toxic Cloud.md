**Toxic Cloud** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 47 in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 2]]. It is used by some [[Fighters|fighters]] from Chapter 2 and 3 of Act VII. It is also used by [[Assassin]], [[Titan (SF2)|Titan]]'s first [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]], and the Eternal [[Arkhos]].

## Description\[]

The user puts their weapons on their back, then waves their hands and a green cloud of toxic fumes surrounds the enemy. It deals 45 damage and causes the enemy to cough and renders them unable to move for a short duration. It can be dodged by rolling or by taking a long step forward, and can be interrupted easily. It can also be avoided by attacking the enemy in a very little time.
