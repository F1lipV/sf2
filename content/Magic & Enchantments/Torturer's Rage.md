**Torturer's Rage** is the last main story [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet available in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked in [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 3]] at Level 51. It is used by [[Guru]], [[Corsair]], and some [[Fighters]] from Act VII: Chapter 3. It is also used by the Eternal [[Drakaina]] and [[Saturn]].

## Description\[]

The user jumps up, and a giant sawmill rolls horizontally across the battlefield, dealing 45 damage. Torturer's Rage can only be dodged by jumping. Due to how it works, it is possible for the Torturer's Rage to hit the enemy more than once and deal usually 90 damage in total but can hit three times in rare occasions dealing 135 damage.

## trivial\[]

- Torturer's Rage is the only shop magic that comes with a Medium enchantment.
- Technically, it is the most destructive magic in the game as it can hit the enemy more than once, which leads to even more damage than [[Asteroid]].
  - It is quite common to get a double hit due to the way torturer's rage works, however, a **much rarer** triple hit is possible if the enemy gets launched fast enough from the 2nd hit back into the saw blade.
- This magic is of a 'unique' type, unlike the majority of them which are thrown just as [[Ranged Weapon (SF2)|ranged weapons]].
- This is the most expensive magic in the game, and is also the only one to be nerfed.
- This magic is also available in [[Shadow Fight Shades|Shadow Fight: Shades]].
