**Shard of Eternal Ice** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It was obtainable from the [[Equipment Sets#Crystal Ice Chest|Christmas 2016 Special chest]]. In the [[Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition|Special Edition]], this magic is available in the shop. It is used by the Christmas 2016 special raid boss, Freeze. In the 2019 Christmas update, Shard of Eternal Ice came with a new appearance. It could be obtained after defeating Freeze or from the Gingerbread Chest.

## Description\[]

The user conjures a spiky ice ball with both hands, while pulling both hands, then leans forward and launches the ice at the enemy. The ice ball travels horizontally across the battlefield, and when it hits, the enemy is knocked backwards and receives 45 damage. This magic can be dodged by either rolling, ducking or jumping.

![[assets/Xmas16IceBall.gif|Xmas16IceBall]]

## Trivia\[]

- This magic is similar to _Ice Ball_ magic. In fact, the only difference is in their names as both magics share the same animation and cause the same effect.
- If the player already has the Shard of Eternal Ice from the 2016 Christmas update, then the player will have the same 2 items from the Christmas and New Year 2019 set.
