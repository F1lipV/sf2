**Death Grip** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic is obtainable through the Battle Pass during New Year 2022 and 2025 event, by purchasing Premium Pass to unlock VIP rewards.

## Description\[]

User moves their hand in a determined way and some brown colored roots rise from the ground and traps the opponent for a brief moment, during which the trapped opponent remains immobilized. A skillful attack during this moment can deal enormous damage to the opponent.

## Forest Guardian's Set\[]

Read More [[Equipment Sets (SF2)]]

![[assets/Forest_Guardian_Set.jpg|Forest Guardian Set]]

All the items of the Forest Guardian Set bring **Icy Resistance** mythical enchantment. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock the enchantment. Once all the items of the set are collected, Icy Resistance will be available, and the player can enchant other equipment with this enchantment.
