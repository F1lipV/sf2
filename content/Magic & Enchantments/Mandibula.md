**Mandibula** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the magic amulet of of the Karcer Set, which comes with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments#Mythical Recipes|Predator's Fury]].

This magic can be acquired by chance by opening the Ritual Chest during the Ritual Battles Event. Opening a chest costs 600 event coins.

## Description\[]

The user jumps forward while slamming their fists to the ground, causing spikes to emerge from the ground below the player which damage the opponent if they are inside the spikes. The user is invincible against any attacks during the whole animation and shortly after the user has finished the jump.

## Trivia\[]

- This magic works nearly the same as [[Karcer]]'s Hunger Spikes ability, except that the spikes emerges at player's position instead of the opponent, and cannot deal critical hit.
  - Unlike Hunger Spikes that has the exact same hitbox position as [[Toxic Cloud]], this magic's hitbox is adjusted to the correct position.
- Amulet sprite of this magic resembles a moth.
  - The face on it resembles Venom from Marvel.
