**Fire Ball** is the first available [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is purchased through [[Gem|gems]] in the store. Fire Ball is unlocked at level 14. It is used by various [[Fighters|fighters]] and by [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]] and [[Shogun (SF2)|Shogun]].

## Description\[]

The user forms a ball of fire in their hands, while pulling both hands. After a very brief duration, the user thrusts both arms forward and launches the fire ball at the enemy. The fire ball travels horizontally at a moderate speed. If the fire ball hits the enemy, it makes a dull extinguishing sound and deals 45 damage. This magic can be dodged by either rolling, ducking or jumping.
