**Energy Ball** is a [[Magic|magic amulet]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the second magic unlocked in [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III: Trail of Blood]] at level 16. It is used by [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]], various [[Fighters|fighters]] and [[Ninja]], and Kali, which she uses to immobilize [[Shroud]].

## Description\[]

The user conjures a sphere of energy between both hands, while pulling both hands, then leans forward and launches the attack at the enemy. The energy sphere emits an electrical sound, then a screeching noise as it is being formed, and an even higher pitched noise as it travels horizontally. When the attack hits, the recipient is knocked backwards and receives 45 damage. The magic can be dodged by either rolling, ducking, or jumping.
