**Star's Breath** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in **Shadow Fight 2**, which was available exclusively during 2024 Lunar New Year event. It could be obtained from Plasma Box or in-game offer.

## Description\[]

The user forms a large ball of flaming sun in their hands, while pulling both hands. After a very brief duration, the user thrusts both arms forward and launches the sun ball at the enemy. The sun ball travels horizontally at a moderate speed. If the sun ball hits the enemy, it makes a dull extinguishing sound and deals 50 damage. This magic is nearly undodgeable, as it can only be dodged by going behind the user before the ball becomes tangible and can only miss if the enemy is very low to the ground.

## Trivia\[]

- Star's Breath has a large sprite size and its hitbox is also greatly larger than most of the magic in the game.
- There's currently a bug involving this magic. If the magic misses the enemy, it won't despawn when it reaches the edge of the stage. The player can then "control" the magic by facing another direction, causing the fire ball to follow the direction the player is currently facing. This means that the magic will indefinitely stay in the battle until the player eventually controls the magic and hit the enemy with it.
