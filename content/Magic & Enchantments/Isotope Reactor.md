**Isotope Reactor** is a special paid [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] that was introduced in the New Year 2024 update of [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It can be acquired by purchasing the Cyber-Dragon Pack for \$7.99, which also includes the **[[Matter Splitter]]** weapon and **Dragon's Thirst** helm. The weapon and the bundle were initially available to all players upon the release of the New Year 2024 update. It is now only available to players who have reached level 5 or to returning players who were not there at its initial release.

## Description\[]

The user generates a signal by lowering their arm and then raising it toward the sky. This triggers a turquoise beam of energy to come crashing on the opponent, dealing 45 base damage. The foe will be stunned for a brief period before resuming movement if the attack is effective. Although this magic appears to be powerful, the conjuring stance of this magic takes more time than most magics. This may leave the caster vulnerable to attacks, which can halt the activation of the magic.

## Trivia\[]

- This magic, along with [[Inmate's Shackles]] are the only premium magics in the game that do not come with an enchantment.
- In real life, an isotope reactor is a type of nuclear reactor which is used to generate significant amounts of heat and energy. In contrast to other type of nuclear reactors, isotope reactors primarily use materials with heavier atomic nuclei such as uranium or plutonium as a fuel source for energy generation.
