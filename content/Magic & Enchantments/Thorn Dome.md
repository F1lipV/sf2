**Thorn Dome** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available in a weekly offer for real money.

## Description\[]

The user forms a ball of pink flower energy in their hands, while pulling both hands. After a very brief duration, the user thrusts both arms forward and launches the fire ball at the enemy. The fire ball travels horizontally at a moderate speed. If the fire ball hits the enemy, it makes a dull extinguishing sound and deals 45 damage. This magic can be dodged by either rolling, ducking or jumping.
