**Eye of the Storm** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the magic of the Creators of the Wind Set. This magic is obtainable through the Battle Pass during American Independence Day event 2022, by purchasing Premium Pass to unlock VIP rewards.

## Description\[]

The user conjures a ball of wind between both hands, while pulling both hands, then leans forward and launches the gust at the enemy. When the ball of wind hits, the opponent is knocked backwards and receives 45 damage. This attack animation is nearly identical to _Minor Charge of Darkness_ and [[Sugar Flash]].

## Trivia\[]

- This magic previously had the highest single-hit damage of any magic in the game, dealing 55 damage to enemies. It has since been adjusted to deal the average 45 base damage like most horizontally thrown magics.
  - The record now belongs to [[Highest Measure]], which deals 60 damage per hit.
- Eye of the Storm is nearly identical to the Sugar Flash magic, without the snow effect.
