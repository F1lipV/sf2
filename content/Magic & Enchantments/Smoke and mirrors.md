**Smoke and mirrors** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the magic of the Director Set. This magic was obtainable through the Battle Pass during Halloween event 2022, by purchasing Premium Pass to unlock VIP rewards.

## Description\[]

Smoke and mirrors works the same way as [[Fire Splash]]. The character forms fire on one hand and smashes it into the ground, creating a fire wave. It then travels the ground all the way to the enemy. When it hits the enemy, the fire will rise up from the ground, inflicting upon them 45 damage. This magic can be dodged by jumping at the right time.

## Trivia\[]

- **Smoke and Mirrors** has the form of a 3D glasses as its amulet.
- _Smoke and mirrors_ is a phrase that is intended to make someone believe something is being done or is true, when it is actually not.
