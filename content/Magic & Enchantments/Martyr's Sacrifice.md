**Martyr's Sacrifice** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]], which was available exclusively during 2025 Christmas and New Year event. This magic was available exclusively from an offer via in-game purchase.

## Description\[]

The user floats up, then generates a huge green fire aura that spreads in all directions, dealing 45 damage. The user is unbreakable when they are floating.
