**Sugar Flash** is the New Year 2018 and 2019 special [[Magic (SF2)|magic amulet]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be obtained as a possible reward by purchasing the Gingerbread Chest during New Year 2018. In order to purchase the chest, players needed 250 mint coins or 199 gems. Mint coins could be obtained by defeating Candy, the New Year 2018 special raid boss. Candy herself uses this magic. It is enchanted with the [[Enchantments#Medium Recipes|Stun]] enchantment, and functions like the _Minor Charge of Darkness_. In the 2019 Christmas update, Sugar Flash came with a new appearance. It could be obtained after defeating Freeze or from the Gingerbread Chest.

## Description\[]

The user conjures a sphere of snowball between both hands, while pulling both hands, then leans forward and launches the snowball at the enemy. When the snowball hits, the opponent is knocked backwards and receives 45 damage.
