**Spirits' Wrath** is the 2021 Independence Day special [[Magic#Special Magic|magic amulet]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be obtained by purchasing the Casket of Ancestors for 600 feather coins or 219 gems. Feather coins was the special currency for the Independence Day update, which could be obtained by defeating Adanti. Spirits' Wrath is enchanted with [[Enchantments|Bloodrage]] and its function is similar to [[Fire Pillar]].

## Description\[]

The user kneels, forming a swirling ball of spirit fire in their hand, and then slams it to the ground. After that, a huge pillar of spirit fire rises up from the ground from where the enemy is standing, damaging them.
