**Requiem** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available in a weekly offer.

## Description\[]

The user raises their hands and summons a thorny aura around them, which deals 10 base damage. During the casting animation, there is a period of time during which the player is unbreakable. After the initial hit, the aura lasts for 5 seconds, continuously reducing 16.66% of the enemy's health per second as long as the enemy is within the aura, dealing 83.33% at max.

![[assets/Requiemmagic.gif|Requiemmagic]]

## Trivia\[]

- The player's casting animation of this magic can be manually cancelled by the player (or by the player being hit in a very brief moment) earlier than the moment the thorny aura is spawned. This means that it is possible to completely prevent the aura from spawning, thus reducing the magic's ability to deal damage down to only the initial hit.
- In [[Underworld]], although this magic works the same way as it does in [[CategoryActs|story mode]], if the magic was used to deal any damage in any raid fight, the total damage done in that round may be halved, greatly decreased or completely nullified. Unfortunately, this is random and out of player's control so it would be advised to consider using other magics.
- Technically speaking, Requiem's initial hit deals the lowest damage out of all magics, only dealing 10 base damage.
- Requiem is the only magic that deals true damage, ignoring the enemy's defense entirely, similar to [[Enchantments]] like Poison, Bleeding, Crimson Corruption and Spore Souls.
