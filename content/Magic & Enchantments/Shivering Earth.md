**Shivering Earth** is a [[Magic (SF2)#Special Magic|special event]] [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] which was exclusively dedicated to International Women's Day 2024 in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be obtained by purchasing the Women's Day special paid offer for \$8.99 USD, which also came with the weapon - [[Weeper's Sorrow]], and armor - Poison Petal. The offer was available for a week starting from March 8, 2024.

## Description\[]

The user raises their hands with an upward motion to conjure numerous green vines with lilac-colored flowers sprouting from the ground. These vines would then ensnare the opponent, momentarily trapping them for a while. The vines would then recede back to the ground after a while, setting the opponent free.

This magic, as well as others similar to it, deals only 25 damage, which is the lowest in the game. However, this is compensated by allowing the player to freely deal significant damage to their opponent during the period in which they are incapacitated.

## Trivia\[]

- Upon its initial release, Shivering Earth temporarily used the effects of [[Monk's Amulet]], a magic with the same function. It later received its own unique effect after a subsequent update.
