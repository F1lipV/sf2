**Tamer of the Elements** is the Chinese New Year 2022 [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available exclusively from an offer via in-game purchase. It was also made obtainable again during Chinese New Year 2024. Tamer of the Elements was initially enchanted with [[Enchantments|Weakness]] in 2022 before it was enchanted with [[Enchantments|Lifesteal]] later on.

## Description\[]

The user conjures a water ball with both hands, while pulling both hands, then leans forward and launches the magic at the enemy. The Tamer of the Elements will travel horizontally across the battlefield. When the Tamer of the Elements hits, the enemy is knocked backwards and receives 45 damage.

Like [[Water Ball]], this magic has a larger hitbox than other horizontally thrown magic and can hit the opponent even if they roll. In this case, the magic tends to hit in the middle of the roll, when opponent's feet are up in the air.
