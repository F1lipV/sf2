**Highest Measure** is a [[Magic (SF2)#Special Magic|special event]] [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] exclusive to the Destiny in Red 2024 event in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be acquired as a a random reward by opening a Casket of Elephant's King, which costed 600 ![[assets/CoinIndia24.png|CoinIndia24]] All-seeing Coins or 249 gems to open. This magic was used by Nrityu, the grim reaper.

## Description\[]

The user raises their hands up to summon a [[Soul's Lot]] scythe above the opponent. After a while, the scythe will strike them from behind, dealing 60 damage and making them stagger towards the user or even fling them across the arena if they are moving upon impact.

Its main disadvantage is that its casting speed is more sluggish than most magics, which can render its users vulnerable to attacks while casting it, halting the magic attack in the case they get hit. However, it is compensated by its high damage and its ability to momentarily stagger or occasionally incapacitate the enemy.

## Trivia\[]

- Currently, this magic deals the highest single-hit damage in the game. Dealing 60 damage per hit, it surpasses the previous record holder by 5 damage - [[Eye of the Storm]] which deals 55 damage.
- Its image art depicts a shadow puppet that greatly resembles those used in **Tholpavakoothu**, a form of shadow puppetry/play commonly practiced in Kerala and Tamil Nadu, India.
- This magic has the same conjuring stance as with [[Asteroid]], despite being mechanically different.
