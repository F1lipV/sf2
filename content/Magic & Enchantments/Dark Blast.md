**Dark Blast** is a [[Magic|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 19, the first level of [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV: Pirate Throne]]. [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]] uses this magic after he loses one round. It is also used by various [[Fighters|fighters]] and [[Ninja|ninjas]].

## Description\[]

The user conjures a black energy sphere between both hands, while pulling both hands, then leans forward and launches the attack at the enemy. The sphere will travel horizontally across the battlefield. When the attack hits, the enemy is knocked backwards and receives 45 damage. Upon hitting, the sphere gives off the effects and noise of a small explosion, same as that of Time Bomb. The magic can be dodged by either rolling, ducking, or jumping.
