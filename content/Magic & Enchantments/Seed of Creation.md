**Seed of Creation** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available in a weekly offer for real money.

## Description\[]

Player forms a purple ball on one hand and hits it on the ground, creating galaxies united by a constellation. Then the wave flies along the ground all the way to the enemy. When it hits an enemy, stars rise from the ground, dealing them 45 points of damage.
