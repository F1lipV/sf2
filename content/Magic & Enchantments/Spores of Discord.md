**Spores of Discord** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the magic amulet of the Spore Soul Set, which comes with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments#Mythical Recipes|Cordyceps]]. It is wielded by the [[Fungus]] version of The Lamb.

This magic could be acquired by chance from opening the Ritual Chest during the Ritual Battles Event. Opening a chest costed 600 event coins.

## Description\[]

The user raises their hands to summon a giant mushroom from the ground which emits a green cloud of toxic fumes in its place. After the first cloud, the mushroom summons 3 more clouds every 4 seconds before it disappears. Each cloud deals 20 base damage and causes the enemy to cough, rendering them unable to act for a duration of time, allowing the user to perform follow-up combos. Another giant mushroom cannot be summoned until the current mushroom finishes spewing all 4 clouds.

The initial mushroom summoning can be interrupted easily due to its long startup and the fumes can be dodged by going under or escaping the mushroom's vicinity. However, once the mushroom is fully summoned, it can no longer be interrupted, making it quite hard to traverse around the stage while the mushroom is still active.

## Tips\[]

Spores of Discord currently has a very powerful bug. If the user manages to fully charge their magic and summon another giant mushroom shortly after the last one disappears, this mushroom is able to spew out more fumes than it normally would. This is because the previous mushroom's cloud summoning timer is stacked on top of the current mushroom's timer if the user is quick enough. This can be done indefinitely until the user fails to pull off this technique again or the round ends, which means it is theoretically possible to make a mushroom spew out tremendous amount of clouds per activation, dealing massive damage and giving a ton of combo potential.

## Trivia\[]

- Each of the cloud fumes summoned is similar to [[Toxic Cloud]] (and similar magics like [[Prisoned Blizzard]]).
- Individual Spores of Discord's clouds deal the 3rd least damage out of all magic, dealing only 20 base damage per cloud, higher than 15 base damage from [[Monk's Amulet]] (and by extension: [[Death Grip]] and [[Shivering Earth]]) and 10 base damage from the first hit of [[Requiem (SF2)|Requiem]]. However, it is able to deal the 2nd highest total damage out of all magic in the game, dealing 80 damage if all 4 hits connect, only losing to [[Torturer's Rage]]'s 90 - 135 damage.
  - Technically speaking, it is able to far surpass Torturer's Rage's total damage if the user performs the mushroom stacking technique mentioned above.
