**Cold Fusion** is a special magic amulet in **Shadow Fight 2**, available exclusively during 2023 Christmas and New Year event. It can be obtained from Sly's Bag of Rarities or in-game offer.

## Description\[]

The user floats up, then generates a huge blue fire aura that spreads in all directions, dealing 45 damage. The user is unbreakable when they are floating.

![[assets/Cold_fusion.gif]]

## Trivia\[]

- This magic and Wrath of Flame have bugs in their effect speed and size. Where the effect plays at high speed and the radius is too small for the actual magic hitbox.
