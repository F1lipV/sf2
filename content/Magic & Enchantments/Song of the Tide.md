**Song of the Tide** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available in a weekly offer for real money.

## Description\[]

The player makes a water wave with both hands, while simultaneously extending both arms, then leans forward and launches magic at the enemy. Song of the Tide will move horizontally across the battlefield. When the magic hits the target, the opponent is thrown back and takes 45 points of damage.
