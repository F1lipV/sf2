**Monk's Amulet** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Monk's Amulet is one of the possible rewards in the [[Underworld#Rewards|Raid chest]] at the end of each season. It is necessary to get this magic amulet, along with other equipment from the Monk Set, to unlock the mythical enchantment Tempest Rage. It is enchanted with Tempest Rage.

## Description\[]

User moves their hand in a determined way and some brown colored roots rise from the ground and trap the opponent for a brief moment, during which the trapped opponent remains immobilized. A skillful attack during this moment can deal enormous damage to the opponent (Suplex is highly recommended).

## Trivia\[]

- Monk's Amulet is used by the Halloween 2015 challenger [[Lilith]] and the Eternal [[Fungus]].
- Monk's Amulet deals the least damage compared to other magic (15 damage, compared to the usual 45). However, this downside is balanced with its fast deployment, high accuracy, and its ability to trap their opponents and make them vulnerable to attacks.
- Monk's Amulet will sometimes result in a bug because the opponent has a small time to jump (or make a flip in the air) before the user launches it, which results in roots appearing at different place than the disabled opponent.
- Earlier, Monk's Amulet was possible to obtain in the last stage of [[Ascension]], a mode in [[Act II Secret Path|Act II]]. The mode was removed, then brought back as [[Underworld]] raid.
