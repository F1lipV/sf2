**Gaze of the Void** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available in a weekly offer for real money.

## Description\[]

The user generates a signal by lowering their arm and then raising it toward the sky. This triggers a yellow beam of energy to come crashing on the opponent, dealing 45 base damage. The foe will be stunned for a brief period of time if the attack is effective. Although this magic appears to be powerful, the conjuring stance of this magic takes more time than most magics. This may leave the caster vulnerable to attacks, which can halt the activation of the magic.

![[assets/Gazeofthevoidmagic.gif|Gazeofthevoidmagic]]

## Trivia\[]

- This magic's VFX is a slightly altered version of Isotope Reactor. It is yellow instead of turquoise, slightly smaller, and uniquely causes a ground break visual effect after the beam lands.
