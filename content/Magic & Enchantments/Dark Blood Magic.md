**Dark Blood Magic** is the Halloween 2017 [[Magic#Special Magic|special magic amulet]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It was obtainable during Halloween 2017 update, as a possible reward by purchasing the [[Equipment Sets#Halloween 2017 Set|Mystical Chest]]. In order to purchase the chest, players need 250 bone coins or spend 199 [[Gem|gems]]. Bone coins can be obtained by defeating Whisper, the Halloween 2017 special raid-boss. Whisper herself also uses this magic. It is enchanted with the [[Enchantments#Medium Recipe|Bleeding]] enchantment. Dark Blood Magic functions like the [[Underworld#Charges|Medium Charges of Darkness]].

## Description\[]

While pulling both hands behind their body, the user conjures a sphere of red energy, then leans forward and launches the attack at the enemy. The energy sphere emits an electrical sound, then a screeching noise as it is being formed, and an even higher pitched noise as it travels horizontally. When the attack hits, the opponent is knocked backwards and receives 45 damage.

## Trivia\[]

- Like the [[Lightning Arrow]], Dark Blood Magic will lose its full charge only when the magic has been thrown forward. This means it is possible for the users to still keep its full charge if their deployment is interrupted.
