**Sentinel's Spirit** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the magic amulet of the Sentinel's Set. Sentinel's Spirit is obtainable only by purchasing the Chest of Souls Keeper in the [[Underworld|raid shop]] for 1199 gems. Upon purchase, the players may receive the Sentinel's Spirit, along with some Charges of Darkness. Sentinel's Spirit is enchanted with the mythical enchantment [[Enchantments|Typhoon of Spirits]].

## Description\[]

Shadow puts his weapons on his back, then waves his hands and a yellow dragon spirit rises from below the enemy, letting out a fearsome roar. The dragon deals 45 damage and causes the enemy to be pushed back. After casting this magic, Shadow becomes unable to block any incoming attacks as he is recovering his stance for 2 seconds, making this magic risky to use. The dragon can be dodged by rolling, long step forward, and can be interrupted. This magic has the same animation for its user as the [[Toxic Cloud]].
