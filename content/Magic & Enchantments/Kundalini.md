**Kundalini** is a special [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] amulet in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the magic of the Skanda Set. This magic is obtainable through the Battle Pass during Bihu festival event 2022, by purchase Premium Pass to unlock VIP rewards.

## Description\[]

Character swings one hand forward while launching an wind air wave to the enemy. The wave travels horizontally at a high speed. It deals 45 damage when hit. The magic can be dodged by either rolling, ducking, or jumping. This attack animation is the same as [[Force Wave]].

## Skanda's Set\[]

Read More [[Equipment Sets (SF2)]]

![[assets/Skanda_set_karma.jpg|Skanda set karma]]

All the items of the Skanda's Set are enchanted with the mythical enchantment **Karma**. It is necessary to collect the full set to unlock **Karma**. Once unlocked, the player can enchant other equipment with **Karma**. To use the **Karma** in fights, the players must equip either the full Skanda's Set or any items enchanted with the enchantment.

## Trivia\[]

- Kundalini is a form of divine feminine energy (or Shakti) believed to be located at the base of the spine, in the muladhara in Hinduism.
- **Kundalini** and [[Tamer of the Elements]] used to deal 50 damage, higher than the average. They have since been adjusted to deal 45 damage.
