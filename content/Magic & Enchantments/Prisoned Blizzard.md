**Prisoned Blizzard** is the New Year 2021 special [[Magic (SF2)|magic amulet]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It could be obtained as a possible reward by purchasing the Gingerbread Chest during New Year 2021. In order to purchase the chest, players needed 500 mint coins or 199 gems. Mint coins could be obtained by defeating Candy, the New Year 2021 special raid-boss. It is enchanted with the [[Enchantments#Simple Recipes|Bloodrage]] enchantment, and functions like the [[Toxic Cloud]].

## Description\[]

The user puts their weapons on their back, then waves their hands and a cloud of blizzard fumes surrounds the enemy. It deals 45 damage and causes the enemy to cough and renders them unable to move for a short duration. It can be dodged by rolling or by taking a long step forward, and can be interrupted easily. It can also be avoided by attacking the enemy in a very little time.
