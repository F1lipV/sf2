**Tender Tempest** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available in a weekly offer for real money.

## Description\[]

The user puts their weapons on their back, then waves their hands and a cloud of purple flower petals and floral energy surrounds the enemy. It deals 45 damage and causes the enemy to cough and renders them unable to move for a short duration. It can be dodged by rolling or by taking a long step forward, and can be interrupted easily. It can also be avoided by attacking the enemy in a very little time.
