**Fire Splash** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. Fire Splash is used by some [[Fighters|fighters]]. It is unlocked at level 40 in the [[Interlude]].

## Description\[]

Fire Splash works similar to the [[Dark Implosion]] unlocked at [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV]]. Character forms fire on one hand and smashes it into the ground, creating a fire wave. It then travels the ground all the way to the enemy. When it hits the enemy, the fire will rise up from the ground, inflicting upon them 45 damage. This magic can be dodged by jumping at the right time. Using it perfectly timed, it can dodge a throwing weapon as shown in the first gif.
