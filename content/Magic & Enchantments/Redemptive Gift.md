**Redemptive Gift** is a [[Magic|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. This magic was available in a weekly offer via an in-game offer.

## Description\[]

The user slowly summons a tidal wave with one hand, which travels across the stage and splashes onto the opponent, dealing 45 base damage. The wave causes a unique knockdown animation on hit, leaving the enemy vulnerable until they fully get up, similar to a critical hit's knockdown. This means that the user is able to perform follow-up combos under the right conditions.

![[assets/Redemptivegiftmagic.gif|Redemptivegiftmagic]]

## Trivia\[]

- This magic is identical to [[Arkhos]]'s ability.
- Despite being visibly knocked down, the enemy is considered "stable" during the knockdown animation, meaning any attack that hits the enemy during this period of time will forcefully make the enemy stand up and suffer the attack's hitstun animation instead of ragdolling, just like they would if the attack connected in normal circumstances.
