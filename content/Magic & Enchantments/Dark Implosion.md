**Dark Implosion** is a [[Magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is unlocked at level 21 in [[Act IV Pirate Throne|Act IV: Pirate Throne]]. [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]] uses this magic after he loses twice and during [[Gates of Shadows]] and [[Interlude]]. It is also used by [[Wasp (SF2)|Wasp]] and by various [[Fighters|fighters]] during Interlude.

## Description\[]

Dark Implosion is the first magic that travels on the ground when used. The user forms dark energy on one hand and smashes it onto the ground, creating a wave of explosions. The explosion will travel on the ground until it hits the enemy. The magic can be dodged by jumping at the right timing.
