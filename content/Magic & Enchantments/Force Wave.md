**Force Wave** is a [[Magic (SF2)|magic]] in [[Shadow Fight 2]]. It is the first magic available in the [[Interlude]], unlocked at level 38. It is used by the Demon [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]] during [[Gates of Shadows]], [[Interlude]] and later fights. It is also used by some [[Fighters|fighters]] during Interlude and by the ghost [[Fighters|Harpy]] from [[Act VII Revelation|Act VII: Chapter 2]].

## Description\[]

Character swings one hand forward while launching an air wave to the enemy. The wave travels horizontally at a high speed. It deals 45 damage when hit. The magic can be dodged by either rolling, ducking or jumping.

## Trivia\[]

- Compared to other magics, Force Wave's magic deployment is one of the fastest in the game.
  - If the player throws Force Wave at exactly the same time the enemy throws any horizontally thrown magic other than Force Wave, the enemy's deployment will be interrupted.
- Unlike most magics from the shop, Force Wave is rarely used by enemies.
