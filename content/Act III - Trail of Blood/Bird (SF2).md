**Bird** is the first [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]] and a member of his gang. Bird, like several other bodyguards of Butcher, has her name after an animal. [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] and his group first stumble upon Bird when they arrive at the location of the events of [[Act III Trail of Blood|Act III]]. She offers to show them around but is later revealed to be a member of Butcher's gang. She leads them to a secluded part of town saying that it will take their breath away, once and for all. Upon escorting them to her destination, she attacks, but Shadow manages to defeat her. After she is defeated, she begs him to leave her alone and let her go. According to [[Rhino]], Bird and him are lovers. Bird uses Knives as her weapon.
