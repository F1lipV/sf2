**Rhino** is the second [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]] and a member of Butcher's gang. Like most of Butcher's bodyguards, he is named after an animal. He wields the [[Crescent Knives]] as his weapon. Before his fight with [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]], Rhino reveals that he and [[Bird]] are lovers and threatens that Shadow will pay for hurting her. After his defeat, Rhino begs Shadow not to tell Butcher about his relationship with Bird.
He is fought once more during the [[Interlude]].

## Trivia\[]

- Rhino and Bird are one of the two couples to make an appearance in the game. The other is Butcher and his "favorite" wife [[Rose]].
