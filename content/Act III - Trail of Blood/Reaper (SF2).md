**Reaper** is the fifth and final [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]] and a member of his gang. He has a cold personality. He wields a [[Kusarigama]]. He seems to be very confident in facing [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]], but after defeat, he acts as if it was not a big deal. He is very loyal towards Butcher.

## Trivia\[]

- He is the first bodyguard to use Kusarigama, and the only one to use the one obtainable from the shop. The second being [[Puma]], the fifth bodyguard of [[Widow (SF2)|Widow]], who wields a unique type of Kusarigama that is unobtainable to the player.
- The helm that he wears is unobtainable and is the same worn by [[Ghost]], the fourth bodyguard of [[Lynx (SF2)|Lynx]].
- Reaper and Redhead are the only bodyguards in Butcher's gang to not be named after animals.
- The kusarigama is shown in his avatar to have an abnormally huge blade and a shorter chain; however it is just normal when he faces the player, as is the case with many bodyguards, fighters and ninjas to have their weapons in their avatars be altered in shape or characteristics.
- Reaper is the only male bodyguard who wears a top outfit.
