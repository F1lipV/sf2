**Bull** is the third [[Bodyguards|bodyguard]] of [[Butcher (SF2)|Butcher]] and a member of his gang. Bull, like [[Bird]] and [[Rhino]], takes his name from an animal. Bull uses [[Steel Batons|batons]] as weapons. He is dark-skinned and well-built.

When [[Shadow (SF2)|Shadow]] makes his way to fight him, Bull states that he owes everything to Butcher. After being defeated, Bull questions if Butcher only used him, but refuses to believe it. Shadow and his companions later learn that Bull joined the gang, like many others, after he was orphaned; Butcher had his parents killed and pretended to be a savior, as told by [[Redhead]]. He is later seen again in the [[Interlude]] as Butcher's bodyguard.

## Trivia\[]

- Bull is one of the three bodyguards in the game to use batons. The other two are [[Brick]] from [[Act I Hero Reborn|Act I]] and [[Irbis]] from [[Act V The Greatest Temptation|Act V]].
- Despite wielding wooden batons in his avatar, Bull uses the steel batons in battle, as is the case with all the other wielders of this weapon in the game.
